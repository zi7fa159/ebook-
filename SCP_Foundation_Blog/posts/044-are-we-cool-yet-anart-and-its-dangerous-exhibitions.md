# Are We Cool Yet?: Anart, Anartists, and Their Dangerous Exhibitions

## 1. Introduction: Art That Bleeds, Burns, and Bends Reality

In the clandestine world of anomalous phenomena policed by the SCP Foundation, not all threats come from ancient evils or cosmic horrors. Some arise from a far more contemporary and relatable source: human creativity, albeit a twisted and reality-defying form of it. "Are We Cool Yet?" (AWCY?) is a loosely affiliated collective of "anartists" – anomalous artists – who create "anart" (anomalous art). Their works often possess dangerous, unpredictable, or reality-altering properties, and their public "exhibitions" can result in chaos, casualties, and significant challenges for Foundation containment efforts.

AWCY? represents a unique type of Group of Interest, driven not by power, ideology, or profit in the traditional sense, but by a desire for artistic expression, notoriety, social commentary, or simply the thrill of creating something "cool," regardless of the consequences. This article explores the nature of AWCY?, their methods, their art, and their often-antagonistic relationship with the Foundation.

## 2. Who or What is "Are We Cool Yet?"

AWCY? is not a structured organization with a clear hierarchy or headquarters. Instead, it's more of a decentralized movement, an ethos, or a label adopted by various individuals and small groups who create anomalous art.

*   **Decentralized Collective:** Composed of independent anartists or small, temporary cells who may share techniques, philosophies, or exhibition spaces, but rarely operate under a central command.
*   **Anarchic Nature:** They often reject traditional artistic norms and societal rules, embracing chaos and transgression in their work.
*   **The "Cool" Factor:** The recurring phrase "Are We Cool Yet?" often appears in their art or manifestos. It can be interpreted as:
    *   A genuine question seeking validation or recognition for their transgressive art.
    *   A sarcastic taunt aimed at society, the art establishment, or organizations like the Foundation.
    *   A nihilistic or ironic statement about the pursuit of meaning or impact.
*   **Motivations Vary:** Individual anartists may have diverse motivations:
    *   **Art for Art's Sake (Anomalous Edition):** Pure creative expression using anomalous materials or methods.
    *   **Social/Political Commentary:** Using anart to critique society, power structures, or conformity.
    *   **Notoriety/Infamy:** Seeking fame or recognition within the anart underground or by provoking authorities.
    *   **Thrill-Seeking/Rebellion:** Enjoying the danger and illegality of creating and exhibiting anomalous art.
    *   **Nihilism/Destruction:** Some anartists may have more destructive or chaotic impulses.

## 3. Anart: The Anomalous Artworks

"Anart" encompasses a vast range of creations, limited only by the anartist's imagination and access to anomalous resources.
*   **Mediums:** Can include paintings, sculptures, installations, performance pieces, music, literature, digital art, graffiti, and even biological or memetic constructs.
*   **Anomalous Properties:** The "anomalous" aspect can manifest in many ways:
    *   **Cognitohazards/Memetics:** Art that affects the viewer's mind, perception, or behavior (e.g., a painting that induces madness, a song that compels specific actions).
    *   **Reality Bending:** Art that locally alters physical laws, creates pocket dimensions, or changes its own form.
    *   **Living Art:** Sculptures or paintings that are alive, sentient, or animate.
    *   **Dangerous Materials:** Art created from hazardous anomalous substances or SCP-derived materials.
    *   **Interactive/Reactive Art:** Pieces that react to viewers or their environment in unpredictable or harmful ways.
    *   **Conceptual Art:** Anart where the "idea" or the statement itself is the anomaly.
*   **Themes:** Often explores themes of death, transformation, societal decay, rebellion, the nature of reality, and the grotesque. However, some anart can also be whimsical, surreal, or satirical.
*   **"Exhibitions":** Anartists often display their work in public or semi-public "exhibitions," which can range from clandestine gallery showings in abandoned buildings to flash mobs, street art installations, or even digital releases. These exhibitions are frequently the source of Foundation interventions.

The key is that anart rarely conforms to mundane expectations of what art "should" be and often carries significant risk to those who create, view, or interact with it.

## 4. Methods and Modus Operandi

*   **Clandestine Operations:** Anartists typically operate in secret to avoid detection by the Foundation, GOC, or mundane law enforcement.
*   **Acquisition of Anomalous Materials:** How they acquire anomalous materials or learn anomalous techniques is often unclear. Possibilities include:
    *   Discovering minor, uncontained anomalies.
    *   Stealing from less secure Foundation sites or other GoIs.
    *   Learning "paracrafts" or thaumaturgical techniques from underground sources.
    *   Some anartists might be anomalous individuals themselves.
*   **Communication and Collaboration:** They may use encrypted online forums, darknets, or word-of-mouth within the anomalous underground to share ideas, plan exhibitions, or critique each other's work.
*   **Signature/Tagging:** The phrase "Are We Cool Yet?" or related symbols often serve as a signature or tag on their works or at exhibition sites.
*   **Disregard for Safety:** A hallmark of AWCY? is a general disregard for the safety of themselves, their audience, or the general public. The "art" and its impact are paramount.

## 5. Relationship with the SCP Foundation and Other GoIs

*   **SCP Foundation:**
    *   **Antagonistic:** The Foundation views AWCY? as a persistent nuisance and a source of dangerous, uncontained anomalies. MTFs are often dispatched to shut down anart exhibitions, contain the anart pieces (often classifying them as new SCPs), and apprehend the anartists.
    *   **"Art Critics from Hell":** Anartists often see the Foundation as oppressive censors, philistines who don't understand "true art," or simply part of "The Man" they are rebelling against.
    *   **Difficult to Target:** AWCY?'s decentralized, leaderless nature makes it difficult for the Foundation to dismantle them as an organization. They can only react to individual anartists or exhibitions.
*   **Global Occult Coalition (GOC):** The GOC likely views anart as Type Green parathreats and would typically opt to destroy the artworks and neutralize the anartists, possibly with more prejudice than the Foundation.
*   **The Serpent's Hand:** While both groups operate outside mainstream society and value freedom of expression, the Hand's focus is more on general anomalous rights and knowledge dissemination. They might find some anartists too reckless or self-destructive, but could also appreciate certain forms of anart that challenge established order. Some anartists might find refuge in The Wanderer's Library.
*   **Marshall, Carter, and Dark Ltd.:** MC&D might see anart as a potential commodity, seeking to acquire unique or infamous pieces for their wealthy clientele, though the unpredictable nature of many AWCY? works could make them risky investments.

## 6. Notable Examples of Anart (Conceptual/From SCP Lore)

*   **SCP-1733 ("Season Opener"):** A digital recording of a 2010 NBA game that, when played, causes the recorded individuals (players, audience) to become aware they are in a loop, leading to escalating violence, despair, and horrific transformations within the recording over repeated viewings. This could be interpreted as a piece of digital anart with devastating psychological effects.
*   **SCP-Big Exploding Humanoid Head (from "The Cool War" canon by qntm):** A recurring motif in some AWCY? related tales, representing a powerful, destructive, and perhaps nihilistic artistic statement.
*   **"Living Graffiti" SCPs:** Anomalous graffiti that moves, speaks, or affects those who view it.
*   **Sculptures that Rearrange Organs:** Pieces that cause bizarre biological transformations in viewers.
*   **Music that Induces Mass Hysteria or Specific Behaviors:** Anomalous sound-based art.

The SCP Wiki contains numerous SCPs that are explicitly or implicitly creations of AWCY? or similar anartist groups.

## 7. The "Cool War" Canon and AWCY? Philosophy

One of the most significant explorations of Are We Cool Yet? comes from qntm's "Cool War" series of tales. This canon delves deeper into the philosophy and internal dynamics of AWCY?, portraying them not just as pranksters but as engaged in a kind of ideological "war" using art as their weapon.
*   **Art as a Weapon:** Anart is used to challenge perceptions, disrupt normalcy, and provoke reactions.
*   **The Nature of "Cool":** The tales explore what "cool" means to different anartists – is it fame, transgression, enlightenment, or something else entirely?
*   **Internal Conflicts:** Even within AWCY?, there are differing opinions on methods, ethics (or lack thereof), and the ultimate purpose of their art.
*   **The Price of Anart:** The Cool War often highlights the self-destructive tendencies of many anartists and the heavy personal cost of their creations.

## 8. Why Are We Cool Yet? Matters

AWCY? serves several important functions in the SCP universe:
*   **A Different Kind of Threat:** They represent a more chaotic, unpredictable, and ideologically driven threat than organized militaristic groups or ancient cosmic entities.
*   **Critique of Art and Society:** Their existence allows for meta-commentary on the nature of art, censorship, rebellion, and the search for meaning in a modern (or postmodern) world.
*   **Source of Diverse SCPs:** They provide a plausible origin for a wide variety of strange, often disturbing, and creatively bizarre SCP objects.
*   **Human Element:** Unlike many GoIs that are ancient or non-human, AWCY? is fundamentally human (even if its members create inhuman things). This makes their motivations and flaws more relatable, in a twisted way.
*   **Dark Humor and Absurdity:** The concept of "dangerous art" and the often-absurd creations of anartists can be a source of dark humor.

## 9. Conclusion: The Unpredictable Palette of Anomalous Art

Are We Cool Yet? stands as a unique and unsettling presence in the SCP Foundation's world. They are the rogue artists, the cultural terrorists, the avant-garde of the anomalous, driven by an unyielding desire to create, provoke, and be "cool," often with little regard for the ensuing chaos or destruction. Their decentralized nature and the sheer unpredictability of their "anart" make them a persistent headache for the Foundation and a fascinatingly dangerous element for readers.

Through their often-lethal exhibitions, AWCY? challenges not only the Foundation's control but also our own perceptions of what art can be and what happens when creativity is untethered from responsibility and sanity. They are a reminder that in a world full of anomalies, even a paintbrush, a song, or a simple idea can become a weapon of terrifying potential. So, are they cool yet? The Foundation would likely answer with a resounding "No," followed by the deployment of an MTF and a lot of amnestic spray.
