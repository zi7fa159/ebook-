# SCP-055: The [Unknown] Anti-Meme That Cannot Be Remembered

## 1. Introduction: The Self-Keeping Secret

SCP-055 is one of the most enigmatic and conceptually fascinating anomalies in the SCP Foundation's database. It is an "anti-meme" – an idea or entity with self-censoring properties, meaning that any information about its physical appearance, nature, behavior, or origins is immediately forgotten by anyone who perceives or learns it. Personnel can remember that SCP-055 *exists*, and they can remember where it is *kept*, but they cannot recall any specific details about what *it* is. This makes it the ultimate "unknown," a self-keeping secret.

Its Keter classification stems not from active hostility, but from the profound informational hazard it represents and the Foundation's inability to truly understand or analyze a threat they cannot retain information about. This article delves into the perplexing nature of SCP-055, the challenges it poses, and its unique place in SCP lore.

## 2. What is an Anti-Meme?

To understand SCP-055, one must first grasp the concept of an "anti-meme" in the SCP context:
*   **Memes:** In SCP parlance, a meme is a piece of information or an idea that can spread and replicate (like a cultural virus).
*   **Anti-Memes:** An anti-meme is the opposite. It is an idea or phenomenon that, by its very nature, resists being known, remembered, or transmitted. It actively erases itself from memory, records, and observation.
    *   **Self-Censoring:** Information about an anti-meme spontaneously disappears from minds and documents.
    *   **Difficult to Study:** Researching an anti-meme is incredibly difficult, as any data gathered is quickly lost.

SCP-055 is the quintessential example of a potent anti-memetic anomaly.

## 3. Description (Or Lack Thereof)

The core of SCP-055's anomaly is that its physical characteristics **cannot be described or remembered.**
*   **The Paradox of Knowledge:** Personnel can enter its containment chamber, observe it, take notes, make recordings, and discuss it with each other *while they are directly observing it*. However, the moment they cease direct observation or attempt to record information externally, all specific details about its appearance, properties, and behavior vanish from their minds and often from their notes (or the notes become incomprehensible).
*   **Known Facts (Indirectly Deduced):** Through clever, indirect reasoning and analysis of *what is NOT SCP-055*, some facts have been inferred:
    *   **It is not a sphere:** This is a running gag and a key piece of negative information that personnel *can* sometimes remember (i.e., they can remember what it *isn't*, but not what it *is*).
    *   **It is not round (by extension).**
    *   **It is not silent (possibly):** Some personnel report vague, unremembered sensations after exposure, suggesting it might make sounds they can't recall.
    *   **It is contained:** The Foundation knows *where* it is and that it *is* contained, even if they don't know what "it" is.
*   **Size and Security:** It is kept in a square room (5m x 5m x 2.5m) with concrete walls (50cm thick) and a Faraday cage. Access is via a heavy door that must be relocked automatically, as personnel will forget to do so. Security guards are posted outside, but they too cannot remember what they are guarding.

The humor and frustration of SCP-055 come from the Foundation's meticulous documentation of their inability to document it.

## 4. Anomalous Properties: The Amnesic Aura

*   **Information Erasure:** Any specific information about SCP-055's nature, appearance, or direct properties is erased from human memory and often from physical or digital records shortly after observation ceases.
*   **No Direct Harm (Known):** SCP-055 is not known to be actively hostile or to directly harm personnel in a physical sense. Its danger is purely informational.
*   **Self-Keeping Secret:** Its primary anomalous property is its ability to prevent itself from being known.
*   **Potential for Unseen Influence:** The greatest fear regarding SCP-055 is that it *could* be doing something incredibly dangerous, or have dangerous properties, that the Foundation is simply incapable of remembering or recognizing. It could be subtly influencing events or personnel, and no one would ever know.

## 5. Object Class: Keter

Despite its apparent passivity, SCP-055 is classified as **Keter**. This surprises many new readers, but the reasoning is sound from a Foundation perspective:

*   **Informational Black Hole:** The Foundation cannot gather any actionable intelligence on it. They don't know what it is, what it does, what it wants (if anything), or what its capabilities are. An unknown of this magnitude is an unacceptable risk.
*   **Potential for Unrecognized Threat:** Because its properties cannot be retained, it could possess any number of dangerous secondary anomalous effects that activate under specific conditions, and the Foundation would be unable to prepare for or remember them. It could be a ticking time bomb, and they wouldn't know.
*   **Resource Drain (Indirect):** While its direct containment is simple (a locked room), the resources spent trying (and failing) to study it, and the constant need for personnel rotation due to memory loss, add up.
*   **Vulnerability to Misuse by Others:** If another Group of Interest (especially one with advanced understanding of anti-memetics, like the Foundation's own Antimemetics Division in some canons) found a way to understand or control SCP-055 when the Foundation cannot, it would be a catastrophic security breach.
*   **The "What If It Gets Out?" Problem:** If SCP-055 were to breach containment (though it shows no inclination to do so), how would the Foundation even know what to look for? How could they re-contain something they can't describe or remember?

Its Keter classification is a testament to the Foundation's principle that "the unknown is dangerous," especially when that unknown actively resists being known.

## 6. Special Containment Procedures: Containing an Enigma

The containment procedures for SCP-055 are more about managing the *personnel* around it than managing the object itself:
*   **Secure Room:** Kept in a specific, well-documented room at Site-██.
*   **Restricted Access:** Access is heavily restricted, requiring authorization from at least two (2) Level 4 personnel.
*   **No Note Removal (Effectively):** Personnel are forbidden from removing notes, recordings, or photographs of SCP-055 from the room (though this is somewhat moot, as the information would likely be lost anyway or the records become useless).
*   **Psychological Monitoring:** Personnel assigned to SCP-055 are subject to regular psychological evaluation, though the value of this is questionable given their inability to recall their experiences.
*   **Automated Security:** The containment chamber door must relock automatically because personnel will forget.
*   **Guards with No Memory:** Guards are stationed outside but are not expected to remember what they are guarding, only that they *are* guarding something important.

The procedures are a study in frustration, designed around the certainty of forgetting.

## 7. The Antimemetics Division and SCP-055's Role

In some SCP canons, particularly those focusing on the "Antimemetics Division" (popularized by author qntm), SCP-055 plays a crucial role.
*   **The Division's Nemesis/Tool:** The Antimemetics Division is a clandestine branch of the Foundation that deals specifically with anti-memetic threats – things that erase themselves from memory or perception. SCP-055 would be a prime subject of their (incredibly difficult) research.
*   **The "Anti-Narrative" or "Hole in Reality":** In these stories, SCP-055 is sometimes portrayed not just as an object, but as a kind of "hole" in reality or information, a concept that actively fights being part of any narrative.
*   **Is SCP-055 Actually SCP-001?** A popular fan theory, sometimes explored in tales, is that SCP-055 is, in fact, the *true* SCP-001, its anti-memetic nature being the ultimate form of secrecy. No one, not even the O5 Council, can remember what SCP-001 is because it *is* SCP-055.
*   **Paired with Other SCPs (SCP-579):** SCP-055 is famously (or infamously) linked with SCP-579 ("[DATA EXPUNGED]") in some storylines, particularly in SCP-2998 and SCP-5000. The interaction of these two highly enigmatic SCPs is often posited as a "reset button" or a way to neutralize catastrophic threats, though the mechanism is entirely unknown and highly dangerous. The idea is that putting an "unknown" (055) into contact with a "completely redacted" (579) results in a mutual annihilation or universal reset. This is known as the "Rounderhouse/Kaktus Keter Kuma" procedure or similar names.

## 8. The Humor and Horror of SCP-055

SCP-055 masterfully blends intellectual horror with a unique brand of bureaucratic, existential humor:
*   **Horror:**
    *   The fear of the unknown.
    *   The idea of a threat you cannot prepare for because you cannot understand it.
    *   The loss of memory and potentially, agency.
    *   The existential dread of a "gap" in reality.
*   **Humor:**
    *   The absurdity of the Foundation's attempts to document something undocumentable.
    *   The running gag about it "not being a sphere."
    *   The image of highly trained personnel constantly forgetting what they just saw or did.
    *   The dry, clinical tone applied to a situation of complete informational chaos.

This blend makes SCP-055 memorable precisely because its subject is unmemorable.

## 9. Philosophical Implications

*   **The Limits of Knowledge:** SCP-055 represents a hard limit to the Foundation's (and humanity's) ability to know and categorize everything. Some things may be inherently unknowable.
*   **The Nature of Secrecy:** It is the ultimate secret – one that keeps itself.
*   **Identity and Memory:** If identity is tied to memory, what does it mean to interact with something that erases those memories?
*   **Subjectivity vs. Objectivity:** Can something truly exist if it cannot be objectively described or consistently remembered? SCP-055 challenges these notions.

## 10. Conclusion: The Enigma That Defines Itself by What It Is Not

SCP-055 is a triumph of minimalist horror and conceptual ingenuity. It creates a compelling and deeply unsettling anomaly not through explicit descriptions of terror, but through the complete *absence* of such descriptions, replaced by the documented frustration and confusion of those who try to study it. Its Keter classification is a stark reminder that the greatest threats are not always the most overtly destructive, but can also be the most profoundly unknown.

It is the hole in the Foundation's knowledge, the question mark at the heart of Site-██, the anomaly that is famous for being forgotten. SCP-055 serves as a perfect embodiment of the idea that in a universe of infinite weirdness, some secrets are so deep, they refuse to be secrets at all – they simply refuse to be. And that, perhaps, is the most unsettling thought of all. We know it's there. We just don't know *what* "it" is... and we never will. Except, of course, that it isn't round.
