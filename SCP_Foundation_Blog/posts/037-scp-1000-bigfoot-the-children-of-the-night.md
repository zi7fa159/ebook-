# SCP-1000: Bigfoot and the Lost Civilization of the Children of the Night

## 1. Introduction: More Than Just a Cryptid

SCP-1000, commonly referred to as "Bigfoot" or "Sasquatch" in popular folklore, is far more than a mere cryptid within the SCP Foundation's universe. This Keter-class (conditionally Thaumiel) designation refers to a species of nocturnal, ape-like humanoids that once possessed a highly advanced, global civilization far surpassing modern humanity. Their tragic history, their current suppressed state, and the terrible secret the Foundation keeps regarding their past form the core of one of the most compelling and thought-provoking SCP articles.

SCP-1000 delves into themes of lost history, ecological balance, guilt, and the terrifying potential of a re-emergent power. This article explores the nature of these "Children of the Night," their forgotten empire, the cataclysm that befell them, and the Foundation's desperate efforts to prevent their return to dominance.

## 2. Physical Description and Behavior: The Sasquatch We Know (and Don't)

SCP-1000 instances generally match folkloric descriptions of Bigfoot:
*   **Appearance:** Large, hairy, ape-like humanoids, typically 1.5 to 3 meters (5 to 10 feet) in height, weighing between 90 and 270 kg (200 to 600 lbs). Fur color varies (brown, black, reddish, white).
*   **Nocturnal and Elusive:** Primarily active at night and exceptionally skilled at avoiding human detection, contributing to their cryptid status.
*   **Diet:** Omnivorous, with a diet similar to other great apes.
*   **Intelligence:** Possess human-level or near-human-level intelligence in their current, "devolved" state. However, their ancestors were vastly more intelligent.
*   **Social Structure:** Live in small family groups or tribes in remote wilderness areas, primarily in North America and the Himalayas.
*   **Anomalous Trait (Current):** While most individuals are not overtly anomalous beyond their elusiveness, they all share a genetic susceptibility or connection to the anomalous event that destroyed their civilization. They also possess an innate, instinctual fear and avoidance of humans and human technology.

The key is that their current state is a shadow of their former glory.

## 3. The Lost Civilization: Children of the Sun, Masters of Organic Technology

The most startling revelation of SCP-1000 is the existence of their past global civilization, referred to by themselves as the "Children of the Sun" (a stark contrast to their current nocturnal existence as "Children of the Night").
*   **Advanced Technology:** Their civilization was incredibly advanced, but their technology was primarily organic and symbiotic, based on manipulating flora and fauna through anomalous means.
    *   **Living Structures:** Cities made of shaped trees, tools grown from bone and sinew, vehicles that were living creatures.
    *   **Biotechnology:** Mastery of genetics, bio-engineering, and ecological manipulation on a planetary scale.
    *   **"Tree of Life":** They may have harnessed or cultivated immensely powerful anomalous biological entities or processes.
*   **Global Dominance:** They were the dominant sentient species on Earth for millennia, existing long before modern humans evolved or rose to prominence.
*   **Peaceful Coexistence (Initially with Early Humans):** Evidence suggests they initially coexisted, perhaps even benevolently, with early hominids, possibly viewing them as less developed relatives.
*   **Culture and Society:** Little is known about their specific culture, but it was likely deeply intertwined with nature and their organic technology.

This lost empire represents a major "Ozymandias" theme – a once-great power now reduced to scattered remnants.

## 4. "The Day of Flowers": Humanity's Betrayal and the Great Cataclysm

The turning point in SCP-1000's history was a catastrophic event known as "The Day of Flowers" (a human term; SCP-1000's term is unknown and likely unpronounceable).
*   **Humanity's Rise and Betrayal:** As anatomically modern humans evolved and developed their own rudimentary technology and societal structures, they somehow turned against the Children of the Sun.
*   **The Weapon:** Humans, perhaps with the aid of a stolen or repurposed piece of SCP-1000's own anomalous bio-technology, or through an alliance with another anomalous entity (the Fae are sometimes implicated in related lore, e.g., SCP-4000), unleashed a devastating "weapon" or event.
*   **Nature of the Cataclysm:** This event specifically targeted the Children of the Sun, causing:
    *   **Mass Death:** Wiping out the vast majority of their population (estimated 70-95%).
    *   **Technological Collapse:** Destroying their organic infrastructure and knowledge base.
    *   **Genetic/Psychological Damage:** Inducing a state of "pseudo-sapience" or cognitive regression in the survivors, along with an instinctual aversion to sunlight and an innate fear of humans. They became the nocturnal, elusive "Children of the Night."
    *   **The "Flower" Aspect:** The name "Day of Flowers" is cryptic. It might refer to a specific anomalous plant used in the attack, a memetic component, or a poetic description of the event's nature or aftermath.
*   **Humanity's Amnesia:** Crucially, humanity itself seems to have collectively forgotten this event, or the memory has been suppressed, possibly as a side effect of the weapon used or through subsequent anomalous means. Only fragmented myths and the Foundation's deepest records retain hints of this truth.

This event represents humanity's "original sin" in the SCP-1000 narrative – a genocidal act that allowed humans to become the dominant species.

## 5. Object Class: Keter (with Conditional Thaumiel Application)

SCP-1000 is classified as **Keter** due to the existential threat posed by the *potential* for their species to regain their former intelligence and technology and seek retribution against humanity.
*   **Why Keter?**
    *   **Existential Threat:** If SCP-1000 instances were to "awaken" or re-evolve, their vastly superior intellect and potential for anomalous bio-technology would make them an unstoppable threat to human civilization. They would have every reason to seek revenge.
    *   **Information Hazard:** Knowledge of "The Day of Flowers" and humanity's role in it is considered a dangerous infohazard. If SCP-1000 instances were to learn the full truth of their past, it could trigger their re-awakening.
    *   **Prevention of Re-emergence:** The Foundation's primary goal is to prevent SCP-1000 from ever regaining its former status. This involves suppressing information, monitoring their populations, and preventing any catalyst that might reverse their cognitive decline.
*   **Conditional Thaumiel Classification (SCP-1000-f1):**
    *   A specific document, SCP-1000-f1, details a message allegedly received from an SCP-1000 instance: "We forgive you. Given choice, let us back in." This message, and the potential for peaceful reintegration or even assistance from a re-awakened SCP-1000 species, is what gives rise to the conditional Thaumiel consideration.
    *   The Thaumiel aspect is highly controversial and debated within the Foundation. Could they be allies? Could they help solve other anomalous problems? Or is this a trick, a dangerous gamble? The O5 Council is deeply divided.

## 6. Special Containment Procedures: Suppression and Secrecy

The Foundation's containment of SCP-1000 is primarily focused on information control and preventing their "re-awakening":

*   **Preventing Public Knowledge:** All evidence of SCP-1000's true nature and past civilization must be suppressed. The "Bigfoot" myth is actively encouraged as a form of misdirection – it keeps people looking for a mere cryptid, not a fallen global superpower.
*   **Monitoring SCP-1000 Populations:** Covert observation of known SCP-1000 groups.
*   **Amnestic Use:** Any civilians or unauthorized personnel who discover the truth about SCP-1000 are administered Class-A amnestics.
*   **No Contact Order:** Foundation personnel are strictly forbidden from making contact with SCP-1000 instances.
*   **Project Pseudolunatic:** A (fictional) highly classified project aimed at understanding and potentially reversing SCP-1000's condition, or ensuring it never reverses. The ethics of this are dubious.
*   **The "We Forgive You" Message (SCP-1000-f1):** This document is subject to extreme secrecy. Its implications are profound:
    *   **Sincerity?** Is the offer of forgiveness genuine, or a deception by a species biding its time?
    *   **The Choice:** The message implies humanity has a "choice" – to continue suppressing them or to risk allowing them to "return." This choice weighs heavily on the O5 Council.

The core of containment is ensuring SCP-1000 *never remembers* what they were, and humanity *never remembers* what it did.

## 7. The Moral and Philosophical Implications

SCP-1000 is rich with ethical and philosophical quandaries:
*   **Collective Guilt:** Does modern humanity bear responsibility for the ancient atrocities committed by its ancestors?
*   **The Price of Dominance:** Was the destruction of SCP-1000's civilization a necessary evil for human survival and ascendancy, or an unforgivable crime?
*   **Fear of the "Other":** The conflict may have stemmed from humanity's fear or jealousy of a more advanced, different species.
*   **The Nature of Forgiveness:** Can such a monumental betrayal truly be forgiven? What would "letting them back in" entail? Coexistence? Subjugation of humanity?
*   **The Danger of Suppressed Truth:** Does hiding this history create a festering wound that will inevitably lead to future conflict?
*   **Foundation's Role as Gatekeeper:** The Foundation has taken on the immense responsibility of managing this secret and deciding the fate of two species.

The article forces readers (and the Foundation itself) to confront uncomfortable questions about humanity's past and its potential future.

## 8. Connection to Broader SCP Lore

*   **The Fae (SCP-4000 "Taboo"):** Some interpretations link the Fae to "The Day of Flowers," suggesting they may have provided humans with the anomalous means to defeat the Children of the Sun, perhaps as part of a pact or for their own inscrutable reasons. The themes of lost names, broken treaties, and hidden worlds in Fae lore resonate with SCP-1000.
*   **Lost Civilizations:** SCP-1000 is a prime example of the "lost advanced civilization" trope within the SCP universe, suggesting that humanity is not the first, and perhaps not the last, dominant species on Earth.
*   **The Veil and its Justification:** The extreme measures taken to hide SCP-1000's truth underscore the lengths the Foundation will go to maintain The Veil, believing some truths are too dangerous for humanity to know.

## 9. Conclusion: The Sleeping Giant and Humanity's Shadow

SCP-1000, "Bigfoot," transforms a familiar cryptid legend into a profound and unsettling narrative about lost history, ecological power, and interspecies conflict. The Children of the Night, once the Children of the Sun, represent a fallen empire whose potential re-emergence poses an existential threat that the SCP Foundation desperately seeks to manage through secrecy and suppression.

The cryptic message "We forgive you. Given choice, let us back in" hangs over the entire scenario, a chilling offer that could be salvation, deception, or the prelude to a final, terrible reckoning. SCP-1000 is a masterful exploration of guilt, fear, and the cyclical nature of power, reminding us that the deepest shadows of our world may hide not just monsters, but the ghosts of civilizations we destroyed and the uncomfortable truths about our own rise to dominance. The Sasquatch in the woods is not just an animal; it's a memory of a king, and the Foundation is terrified it might remember its crown.
