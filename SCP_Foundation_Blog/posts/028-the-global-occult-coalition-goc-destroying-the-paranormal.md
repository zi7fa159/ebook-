# The Global Occult Coalition (GOC): Destroying the Paranormal Threat

## 1. Introduction: Humanity's Other Shield

While the SCP Foundation operates in the deepest shadows to secure, contain, and protect anomalous phenomena, they are not the only major clandestine player on the world stage dealing with the paranormal. The Global Occult Coalition (GOC), also known as the United Nations Global Occult Coalition (UNGOC), stands as another formidable force, often acting as both a rival and an occasional, uneasy ally to the Foundation. Their mission, however, differs significantly: the GOC's primary objective is the destruction of paranormal threats – or "parathreats" as they term them – to ensure the survival and continued dominance of the human race.

Operating under the aegis of the United Nations (at least officially), the GOC represents a more overtly militaristic and interventionist approach to the anomalous. This article explores the GOC's history, ideology, structure, methods, and its complex relationship with the SCP Foundation.

## 2. Ideology and Mandate: The Fivefold Mission

The GOC's operational philosophy is encapsulated in its "Fivefold Mission," often summarized as "Survival, Concealment, Protection, Destruction, Education."

*   **First Mission: Survival:** Ensuring the survival of the human race as the dominant species on Earth, free from paranormal threats or subjugation. This is their ultimate imperative.
*   **Second Mission: Concealment:** Maintaining secrecy regarding paranormal activities from the general public (similar to the Foundation's Veil, which they call the "Masquerade" or "Normalcy"). Public knowledge is seen as destabilizing and dangerous.
*   **Third Mission: Protection:** Protecting individual humans and humanity as a whole from parathreats.
*   **Fourth Mission: Destruction:** The physical destruction and neutralization of parathreats. This is the GOC's most defining characteristic and primary method of dealing with anomalies, contrasting sharply with the Foundation's containment policy.
*   **Fifth Mission: Education:** Acquiring knowledge and understanding of parathreats to better achieve the other four missions, particularly Destruction. This involves research, intelligence gathering, and reverse-engineering of paranormal technology.

Their core belief is that most paranormal entities are inherently dangerous ("Type Greens" in their parlance) and pose an unacceptable risk to humanity. Therefore, extermination is often the preferred, most logical, and safest course of action.

## 3. Origins and History: Forged in War

The GOC's official public history (as a UN body) is relatively recent, but its true origins are rooted in the aftermath of World War II, a conflict that saw the widespread weaponization of occult practices and anomalous artifacts by various factions (notably the Thule Society and Ahnenerbe Obskurakorps for the Nazis, and Allied efforts to counter them).

*   **Post-WWII Realization:** The Allied nations realized the immense danger posed by unchecked paranormal activity and the potential for future "thaumaturgical warfare."
*   **Formation from Allied Occult Initiative (AOI):** Many of the founding member organizations of the GOC were secret societies, occult orders, and government agencies that had been involved in paranormal activities during the war. These groups (e.g., the Bavarian Illuminati, the Knights Templar, MI666) united under a common banner to police the paranormal world.
*   **UN Charter:** The GOC was eventually granted a secret charter by the United Nations, giving it international legitimacy (at least among those in the know) and access to significant resources.

This history imbues the GOC with a more militaristic and pragmatic worldview, having seen firsthand the destructive potential of weaponized anomalies.

## 4. Structure and Organization: A Coalition of Powers

The GOC is not a monolithic entity but a coalition of 108 powerful occult organizations, secret societies, and national paranormal agencies.
*   **Council of 108:** The supreme governing body of the GOC, with each member organization having a seat. Decisions are made through complex political maneuvering and voting.
*   **Undersecretariat:** The GOC's administrative and operational command structure, often led by an Undersecretary-General seconded from the UN.
*   **PHYSICS Division (Strike Teams):** The GOC's elite military arm, composed of highly trained operatives equipped with advanced conventional and paratechnology. They are the ones who engage parathreats directly. Their callsigns often reference physics concepts (e.g., "Sparky," "Damage Control," "Quantum Leap").
*   **PTOLEMY Division (Research & Esoteric):** The GOC's research and intelligence wing. They study parathreats, develop new technologies ("Tangential Technology" or "Tangled-Tech"), and provide support to PHYSICS teams. This includes thaumaturgists, scientists, and analysts.
*   **PSYCHE Division (Internal Affairs & Diplomacy):** Handles internal security, diplomacy with member organizations, and interactions with other paranormal players (like the Foundation, when necessary).
*   **Nexus Hubs:** The GOC maintains command centers and operational bases in various paranormal "Nexuses" (areas of high anomalous activity) around the world.

This coalition structure means the GOC can sometimes be prone to internal politics and differing agendas among its member organizations, but it also gives it a broad base of expertise and resources.

## 5. Methods of Operation: Search and Destroy

The GOC's approach to anomalies is proactive and often aggressive:
*   **Identification of Parathreats:** They actively hunt for and assess anomalous phenomena.
*   **Threat Assessment Levels (TAL):** Parathreats are categorized by their potential danger:
    *   **Level 0 (Known):** Well-understood, minimal threat.
    *   **Level 1 (Potential Threat - "Type Blue"):** Anomalies that could be useful or are not immediately hostile. May be studied or, rarely, utilized.
    *   **Level 2 (Confirmed Threat - "Type Green"):** Actively dangerous or inherently unstable. Standard procedure is destruction.
    *   **Level 3 (Severe Threat - "Type Yellow"):** Poses a significant regional or global threat. Requires immediate and overwhelming response.
    *   **Level 4 (Critical Threat - "Type Black"):** Existential threat to humanity or normalcy. All resources deployed.
    *   **Level 5 (Apocalypse - "Type Grey"):** A K-Class Scenario is underway or imminent.
*   **Deployment of PHYSICS Teams:** Once a Type Green or higher is confirmed, strike teams are deployed to neutralize it.
*   **Use of Paratechnology:** The GOC employs advanced weaponry and equipment, often reverse-engineered from captured or destroyed parathreats. This includes energy weapons, reality anchors, psychic dampeners, and thaumaturgical implements.
*   **"Liquidating" Anomalies:** Their preferred term for destroying parathreats.
*   **Information Control:** Like the Foundation, the GOC is committed to maintaining secrecy and will use disinformation and amnestics (though perhaps less subtly than the Foundation) to cover up their operations.

## 6. Relationship with the SCP Foundation: Rivalry and Reluctant Cooperation

The GOC and the SCP Foundation are the two largest and most influential global players in the anomalous world. Their relationship is complex and often fraught with tension:

*   **Conflicting Philosophies:**
    *   **Foundation:** Secure, Contain, Protect. Believes anomalies should be studied and contained, as destruction can have unforeseen consequences or result in the loss of valuable knowledge. Some SCPs might even be useful (Thaumiel).
    *   **GOC:** Destroy, Destroy, Destroy (primarily). Believes most anomalies are too dangerous to exist and that containment is a risky half-measure that often fails. They see the Foundation's vast collection of contained SCPs as a ticking time bomb.
*   **Clashes and "Cold War":** They frequently clash over jurisdiction or how to handle specific anomalies. There's a sense of a "cold war" between them, with espionage, counter-intelligence, and occasional proxy conflicts.
*   **"Bookburners" vs. "Hoarders":** The Foundation might view the GOC as reckless "bookburners" who destroy potentially valuable or misunderstood anomalies. The GOC might see the Foundation as irresponsible "hoarders" of dangerous items.
*   **Occasional Cooperation:** Despite their differences, they will sometimes cooperate when facing overwhelming common threats that neither organization can handle alone (e.g., a major K-Class Scenario, a particularly powerful hostile GoI). These alliances are usually temporary and marked by deep mistrust.
*   **Information Sharing (Limited):** They may share heavily vetted intelligence if it serves mutual interests, but generally keep their operations and full capabilities secret from each other.
*   **The "Council of Humanity":** In some canons, there are overarching, deeply secret councils or agreements (sometimes involving other major GoIs) that try to prevent all-out war between the Foundation and GOC, recognizing that such a conflict would be catastrophic.

A notable example of GOC action causing problems for the Foundation is SCP-1609 ("The Remains of a Chair"), which was a harmless teleporting chair until the GOC attempted to destroy it (by putting it through a woodchipper), turning it into an aggressive, teleporting pile of splinters that attacks anyone in a GOC-style uniform. This is often cited by the Foundation as an example of the GOC's recklessness.

## 7. Strengths and Weaknesses of the GOC

**Strengths:**
*   **Decisive Action:** Their willingness to destroy threats can be effective in quickly neutralizing immediate dangers.
*   **Military Power:** Possess significant conventional and paratechnological military capabilities.
*   **Broad Coalition:** Access to diverse knowledge and resources from its 108 member organizations.
*   **UN Backing (Nominal):** Provides a degree of international legitimacy and political cover.

**Weaknesses:**
*   **Risk of Unforeseen Consequences:** Destroying an anomaly without fully understanding it can make things worse (as with SCP-1609) or eliminate something that could have been useful.
*   **Internal Politics:** Being a coalition, they can be hampered by infighting or conflicting agendas among member groups.
*   **Less Focus on Long-Term Study:** Their emphasis on destruction means they may not develop as deep an understanding of the underlying principles of anomalies as the Foundation does.
*   **Moral Ambiguity of "Destruction":** Is it always ethical to destroy sentient, non-hostile (or potentially non-hostile) anomalies?

## 8. Conclusion: Humanity's Ruthless Protectors

The Global Occult Coalition represents a starkly different approach to managing the anomalous world compared to the SCP Foundation. Driven by a pragmatic and often brutal assessment of risk, the GOC acts as humanity's aggressive vanguard, seeking out and destroying paranormal threats with formidable efficiency and firepower. Their Fivefold Mission places the survival and dominance of the human race above all other considerations, even if it means eradicating unique, poorly understood, or potentially sentient phenomena.

While their methods often bring them into conflict with the Foundation's containment-focused philosophy, the GOC remains a critical player in the clandestine struggle to protect humanity from the horrors and wonders that lurk beyond the Veil. They are the fist to the Foundation's scalpel, a reminder that sometimes, in a world filled with existential threats, the only perceived solution is total annihilation of the danger. Whether their path is ultimately more effective or more perilous for humanity in the long run remains one of the enduring questions of the SCP universe.
