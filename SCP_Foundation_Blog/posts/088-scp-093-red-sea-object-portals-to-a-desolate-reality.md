# SCP-093: Red Sea Object - Portals to a Desolate, Alternate Reality

## 1. Introduction: The Mirror to a Dead World

SCP-093, known as the "Red Sea Object," is a Euclid-class anomaly that primarily takes the form of a red, disc-shaped object made from a carnelian-like composite. Its most remarkable property is its ability to act as a key, allowing individuals holding it to pass through any mirrored surface as if it were a portal. These portals, however, do not lead to the other side of the mirror in our reality, but into a desolate, alternate version of Earth, the nature of which changes based on the color SCP-093 emits when held. The extensive exploration logs associated with SCP-093 detail harrowing journeys into these dying or dead worlds, uncovering a chilling history of a technologically advanced but morally bankrupt civilization, a mysterious cataclysm, and terrifying entities known as "The Unclean."

This article will explore the properties of SCP-093, the various realities it grants access to, the horrifying discoveries made within them, and its status as a classic example of exploration-driven horror in the SCP Foundation.

## 2. Physical Description of SCP-093: The Shifting Disc

*   **Primary Form:** A disc, primarily red in color, made of a stone composite similar to carnelian or cinnabar. It is approximately 7.6 cm in diameter and 0.5 cm thick.
*   **Carvings:** Intricate circular carvings and unknown symbols are etched into its surface. These symbols do not correspond to any known language.
*   **Hume Level Variance:** SCP-093 itself exhibits unusual Hume field readings, suggesting it has reality-altering or stabilizing properties.
*   **Color Change Property:** When held by a living human subject, SCP-093 will change color. The specific color it adopts seems to be influenced by the regrets, moral failings, or significant past actions of the individual holding it. This color, in turn, dictates the specific version of the alternate reality (SCP-093-Alternate Universe, or SCP-093-AU) that the subject will access when passing through a mirror.
    *   **Known Colors:** Blue, Green, Violet, Yellow, Red (its default color when not held). Other colors may be possible. Each color corresponds to a different "test" or exploration log.

## 3. Anomalous Properties: Mirror Portals and Alternate Realities

*   **Mirror Travel:**
    *   When a human subject holds SCP-093 and presses it against any mirrored surface, they can pass through the mirror as if it were an open doorway.
    *   The mirror itself remains solid to outside observers and equipment. Only the subject holding SCP-093 can traverse it.
    *   The subject can return through the same mirror by pressing SCP-093 against its surface from the other side.
*   **Access to SCP-093-AU (Alternate Universes):**
    *   The destination is always a version of Earth, but one that is vastly different, usually desolate, post-apocalyptic, or otherwise alien.
    *   The specific characteristics of the SCP-093-AU encountered (environment, technology found, entities present) seem to be determined by the color SCP-093 displayed when the subject entered. This implies that the "regrets" or moral standing of the individual influence the "hell" or "purgatory" they access.
*   **No Anomalous Effect on the Mirror Itself:** The mirror used for transit does not become permanently anomalous. Once the subject returns or the connection is broken, it reverts to a normal mirror.
*   **Tether System:** For exploration, the Foundation uses a tether system attached to D-Class personnel to retrieve them, as radio communication is often unreliable or impossible within SCP-093-AU.

## 4. The Alternate Realities (SCP-093-AU): Echoes of a Lost Civilization

The exploration logs for SCP-093 detail several distinct alternate realities, each corresponding to a different color test. Common themes and discoveries across these realities include:

*   **A Lost, Advanced Civilization:** Evidence of a technologically advanced human civilization that once populated this alternate Earth. Their technology often seems more advanced or developed along different paths than our own, sometimes involving "blessed" or "holy" substances and energy sources.
*   **The "Lord" or "His Holy Presence":** Frequent references in recovered texts and environments to a powerful, possibly divine or god-like entity that this civilization worshipped or served. This entity's nature is often linked to a potent energy source or a substance called "His Tears" or "His Holy Tears."
*   **The "Unclean" / "Torsos" (SCP-093-T1):**
    *   Terrifying, hostile entities that roam many of the SCP-093-AU landscapes.
    *   Typically appear as giant, armless, legless humanoid torsos that move by writhing or crawling with incredible speed and strength. They have no discernible facial features beyond a gaping maw filled with oversized teeth.
    *   They are extremely aggressive and will attack any human on sight.
    *   Their origin is a central mystery – are they transformed former inhabitants? Biological weapons? Entities from another dimension drawn by the cataclysm?
*   **The Cataclysm:** All SCP-093-AU realities have suffered some form of devastating cataclysm that led to the collapse of civilization and the desolation of the environment. The nature of this cataclysm is often unclear but seems linked to the "Lord," "His Tears," or the emergence of the "Unclean."
*   **Vast, Empty Cities and Structures:** Explorers find sprawling, eerily empty cities, research facilities, and bunkers, all testaments to a dead or vanished populace.
*   **Pervasive Sense of Despair and Wrongness:** The environments are often oppressive, with strange skies (e.g., perpetually red or green), toxic atmospheres, or an unnerving silence broken only by the Unclean.
*   **The "Faithful" vs. "Heretics":** Recovered documents sometimes hint at societal schisms or conflicts between devout followers of the "Lord" and those who questioned or rebelled against the established order.

Each color-coded exploration log reveals more fragments of this tragic and horrifying alternate history.

## 5. Object Class: Euclid

SCP-093 is classified as **Euclid**.
*   **Why Euclid?**
    *   **Unpredictable Alternate Realities:** The nature of the dimensions accessed via SCP-093 is not fully understood, and they are inherently hostile and dangerous.
    *   **The "Unclean" Threat:** SCP-093-T1 instances are dangerous Keter-level entities if encountered. While they are confined to the alternate realities, the risk of one somehow crossing over (though not yet observed) contributes to the Euclid rating.
    *   **Psychological Impact:** Exploration of SCP-093-AU is psychologically damaging to personnel.
    *   **Unknown Full Capabilities:** The full range of colors SCP-093 can display and the corresponding realities are unknown. The mechanism by which it links a person's "regrets" to a specific reality is also a mystery.
*   **Why Not Safe?** The inherent dangers of the realities it accesses and the unknown variables make it too risky for a Safe classification.
*   **Why Not Keter?** SCP-093 itself is a passive object. It doesn't actively try to harm anyone or breach containment. The danger is initiated by a human choosing to use it. The Unclean are contained within their own dimensions.

## 6. Special Containment Procedures: Controlled Exploration and Secure Storage

*   **Secure Storage:** SCP-093 is kept in a secure locker at a designated Site when not in use for testing.
*   **Mirror Requirement:** Testing requires a mirror of sufficient size, securely mounted.
*   **Subject Selection (D-Class):** Only D-Class personnel are used for manned explorations into SCP-093-AU.
*   **Tether and Retrieval System:** Subjects are fitted with a harness and a strong polymer tether, monitored by a winch system, to allow for retrieval (if possible).
*   **Video and Audio Recording:** Subjects are equipped with video cameras and microphones to record their experiences.
*   **No Unauthorized Use:** Access to SCP-093 and authorization for testing are strictly controlled (Level 4 clearance often required).
*   **Psychological Screening:** Personnel involved in analyzing SCP-093 test footage or debriefing returned D-Class (if any survive) undergo psychological screening due to the disturbing nature of the material.

The primary goal is to gather information about the alternate realities and the history of the civilization that inhabited them, while minimizing risk to Foundation personnel and preventing any crossover of threats.

## 7. The Exploration Logs: A Journey Through a Dying Multiverse

The extensive, color-coded exploration logs are the heart of SCP-093's narrative and horror:
*   **Blue Test:** Often depicts a relatively intact but eerily abandoned urban or facility environment, with early hints of the Unclean and the societal structure.
*   **Green Test:** May reveal more about the agricultural or resource-production aspects of the lost civilization, and often features more direct encounters with the Unclean.
*   **Violet Test:** Can delve into more esoteric or religious aspects, perhaps exploring temples or sites related to the "Lord."
*   **Yellow Test:** Might uncover more technologically advanced or military facilities, and often involves intense action and danger.
*   **Red Test (Special Case):** When SCP-093 is used in its default red state (e.g., by a robotic drone not held by a human, or by a human deemed to have "no regrets" or a specific moral state), the reality accessed can be different, sometimes even more alien or primordial.

These logs build a fragmented but compelling picture of a complex, tragic history and a universe of horrors. They are renowned for their detailed environmental storytelling and suspense.

## 8. Themes and Interpretations

SCP-093 explores several powerful themes:
*   **The Consequences of Unchecked Power/Faith:** The lost civilization, with its advanced technology and devotion to a powerful "Lord," ultimately met a terrible end. This can be read as a cautionary tale about hubris or blind faith.
*   **Environmental Apocalypse:** The desolate landscapes depict worlds ravaged by an unknown cataclysm.
*   **Body Horror and Existential Transformation (The Unclean):** The nature of the Unclean is profoundly disturbing.
*   **The Nature of Guilt and Regret:** The idea that SCP-093's color, and thus the reality accessed, is tied to the holder's inner state is a fascinating psychological element. Are these alternate realities reflections or punishments for past sins?
*   **Lost History and Cosmic Archaeology:** The Foundation acts as archaeologists of a dead or dying multiverse, piecing together a horrifying story from its ruins.

## 9. Conclusion: A Crimson Key to Infinite Sorrows

SCP-093, the Red Sea Object, is a classic and highly acclaimed SCP that masterfully combines object-based anomaly with extensive, immersive exploration logs to create a rich and terrifying narrative. It is more than just a portal; it is a key that unlocks different facets of a single, grand tragedy that played out in an alternate reality, leaving behind only echoes of a technologically advanced but ultimately doomed civilization and the monstrous "Unclean" that now roam its ruins.

The shifting colors of SCP-093, reflecting the inner landscapes of those who dare to hold it, suggest that the worlds it reveals are not just alien, but perhaps deeply, uncomfortably personal. It is a journey into the unknown, a descent into desolation, and a chilling reminder that sometimes, the greatest horrors are not the ones that invade our world, but the ones we find when we dare to look into the mirrors of other, forgotten realities.
