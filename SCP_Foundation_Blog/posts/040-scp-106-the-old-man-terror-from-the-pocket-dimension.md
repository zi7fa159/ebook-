# SCP-106: The Old Man - Corrosive Terror from a Pocket Dimension

## 1. Introduction: The Rotting Predator

SCP-106, commonly referred to as "The Old Man" or "Radical Larry" (a fan nickname), is a Keter-class humanoid anomaly renowned for its horrifying appearance, its ability to phase through solid matter, and its sadistic predatory behavior. This entity exudes a highly corrosive mucus, can transport its victims to a personal "pocket dimension" for prolonged torture, and is notoriously difficult to contain, requiring elaborate and often gruesome recall procedures.

SCP-106 embodies a visceral, decaying horror, a relentless hunter whose motivations are inscrutable beyond a seeming desire to inflict suffering. This article will explore the terrifying nature of SCP-106, its abilities, containment challenges, and its status as one of the Foundation's most dangerous and iconic Keter-class threats.

## 2. Physical Description: A Figure of Decay and Menace

SCP-106 appears as an elderly humanoid, generally male, with an appearance of advanced decomposition.
*   **Decomposed State:** Its skin is often described as rotting, blackened, or mummified. Features are gaunt and skeletal.
*   **Variable Appearance:** While the "old man" form is typical, its appearance can vary slightly.
*   **Corrosive Mucus:** SCP-106 constantly secretes a black, viscous, highly corrosive mucus. This substance coats any surface it touches, causing rapid decay and rust-like pitting in metals, and severe burns or disintegration of organic tissues. The mucus also facilitates its phasing ability.
*   **Eyes:** Often described as dark, empty sockets or possessing a malevolent gleam.
*   **Movement:** Typically slow and deliberate when not actively pursuing prey, giving an impression of age and decrepitude that belies its actual speed and agility when motivated.
*   **Scent:** Its presence is often accompanied by a strong smell of decay or chemicals.

The visual of a decaying, slime-coated figure that can walk through walls is inherently terrifying.

## 3. Anomalous Properties: Master of Corrosion and Pocket Dimensions

SCP-106 possesses several dangerous anomalous abilities:

*   **Phasing/Wall-Walking:** SCP-106 can pass through any solid matter, leaving behind a trail of its corrosive mucus. It can "hang" on vertical surfaces or ceilings, waiting for prey. This makes conventional physical containment extremely difficult.
*   **Corrosion Touch:** Direct physical contact with SCP-106, or prolonged exposure to its mucus, causes rapid and severe degradation of materials. For living beings, this results in horrific burns, liquefaction of tissues, and ultimately, death.
*   **Pocket Dimension (SCP-106-2):**
    *   SCP-106 can transport its victims to a personal extradimensional space, often referred to as its "pocket dimension." This dimension is described as a series of labyrinthine rooms and corridors, constantly shifting and reconfiguring.
    *   Victims trapped within this dimension are subjected to prolonged periods of torture, hunting, and psychological torment by SCP-106. Survival is rare, and those who do escape (or are released) are often severely physically and mentally scarred.
    *   Time may flow differently within the pocket dimension.
*   **Predatory Behavior:** SCP-106 exhibits a strong predatory instinct, specifically targeting humans. It seems to prefer individuals in states of distress, fear, or those who exhibit "desirable" traits (often young adults, typically between the ages of 10 and 25, though it will attack others).
*   **Temporary "Lair" Creation:** When it breaches containment, SCP-106 can create temporary "lairs" by coating an area with its mucus, from which it can more easily emerge to hunt.
*   **Teleportation/Emergence:** It can emerge from any surface coated in its mucus, or from walls and floors within its pocket dimension, making its attacks unpredictable.
*   **Psychological Influence (Subtle):** While not primarily a memetic or psychic entity, its presence and the nature of its pocket dimension can induce extreme fear, paranoia, and despair.

## 4. Object Class: Keter

SCP-106 is unequivocally classified as **Keter**.
*   **Extreme Difficulty in Containment:** Its ability to phase through solid matter makes standard containment cells ineffective. It requires complex, specialized containment protocols.
*   **Active Hostility and Predation:** SCP-106 actively hunts humans and seems to derive pleasure or sustenance from their suffering.
*   **Frequent and Dangerous Breaches:** It breaches containment with some regularity, leading to significant personnel casualties and site lockdowns.
*   **Psychological Terror:** Its methods and the nature of its pocket dimension inflict profound psychological trauma on victims and Foundation personnel.

Its containment is a constant, high-stakes challenge.

## 5. Special Containment Procedures: A Complex and Horrific Dance

Containing SCP-106 is one of the most complex and ethically troubling procedures in the Foundation:

*   **Primary Containment Cell:** A sealed container composed of lead-lined steel, suspended by an electromagnetic support system within a larger secondary containment cell. The entire structure is designed to be complex and difficult for SCP-106 to navigate or corrode through quickly. It often involves multiple layers and varied materials.
*   **No Direct Observation (Usually):** Direct visual observation is typically avoided, with monitoring done via remote sensors (e.g., pressure, sound, video feeds that are themselves at risk of corrosion).
*   **"Lure Subject" / Recall Protocol (Procedure ██-███ / "Femur Breaker"):**
    *   This is the most infamous and horrifying aspect of its containment. When SCP-106 breaches, a human subject (typically a D-Class, aged 10-25) is prepared as "bait."
    *   The subject is severely injured, usually by breaking their femur or another major bone, to produce sounds and emotional states (pain, fear, distress) that are highly attractive to SCP-106.
    *   The injured subject is placed in a designated recall chamber, and their vocalizations are broadcast throughout the facility.
    *   SCP-106, drawn by the "desirable" prey, will typically enter its pocket dimension and re-emerge within the recall chamber to capture the lure subject.
    *   Once SCP-106 has taken the bait and is within the recall chamber (or its primary cell after being lured back), containment systems are reactivated.
*   **Ethical Nightmare:** The "Femur Breaker" protocol is a stark example of the Foundation's willingness to commit horrific acts for the sake of containing an even greater threat. It is a constant source of ethical debate and horror.
*   **Site Evacuation during Breach:** During a breach event, non-essential personnel are evacuated, and MTFs are deployed to manage the situation and assist in the recall protocol.

The reliance on such a brutal luring method underscores the desperation and difficulty involved in managing SCP-106.

## 6. The Pocket Dimension: A Realm of Personal Hell

SCP-106's pocket dimension is a key part of its terror:
*   **Labyrinthine and Unstable:** Constantly shifting corridors, rooms that appear and disappear, and disorienting geometry.
*   **Sensory Deprivation and Overload:** May involve areas of complete darkness, deafening silence, or overwhelming, noxious stimuli.
*   **"Hunting Ground":** SCP-106 uses this dimension as its personal hunting ground, toying with its victims, allowing them fleeting moments of hope before recapturing them.
*   **Prolonged Torture:** Victims are not usually killed quickly but are subjected to physical and psychological torment over extended periods (days, weeks, or what feels like an eternity to the victim).
*   **Corrosive Environment:** The dimension itself is often coated in SCP-106's mucus, causing further injury and decay to those trapped within.
*   **Escape is Rare:** Few who are taken into the pocket dimension ever return, and those who do are often catatonic, horrifically mutilated, or die shortly after.

The pocket dimension represents a personalized hell, tailored by SCP-106 for its prey.

## 7. Origins and Motivations (Speculative)

The true origin of SCP-106 is unknown, though several theories and backstories exist in various tales and canons:
*   **Former Human:** Some stories suggest SCP-106 was once a human, perhaps a soldier from World War I (Corporal Lawrence is a name sometimes associated with him, tying into the "Larry" nickname) or an individual exposed to an anomalous substance or dimension that horrifically transformed him.
*   **Entity from Another Dimension:** It could be an entity native to its pocket dimension or a similar corrosive, alien reality.
*   **Embodiment of Decay or Predation:** Perhaps a symbolic entity representing concepts of rot, age, and predatory cruelty.
*   **Motivations:** Beyond a sadistic desire to hunt and torture, its ultimate motivations are unclear. Does it feed on fear or pain? Is it territorial? Is there some inscrutable intelligence behind its actions? It rarely communicates in a way that clarifies its purpose.

The mystery of its origins only adds to its unsettling nature.

## 8. SCP-106 in SCP Lore and Adaptations

SCP-106 is one of the most recognizable and feared Keter-class SCPs:
*   **Iconic Antagonist:** Features prominently in many SCP tales, often as a relentless and terrifying pursuer.
*   **Video Game Presence:** A major antagonist in popular SCP games like "SCP - Containment Breach," where its ability to walk through walls and its distinctive sound cues create intense horror gameplay.
*   **Symbol of Inescapable Dread:** Represents a type of horror that is slow, inevitable, and deeply personal in its cruelty.
*   **The "Femur Breaker" Meme:** The horrific recall protocol has become a dark meme within the SCP community, highlighting the grim necessities of the Foundation.

## 9. Conclusion: The Inescapable Corrosion

SCP-106, "The Old Man," is a masterwork of body horror, psychological dread, and existential terror. Its decaying form, its ability to glide through walls as if they were water, the corrosive touch that dissolves all it contacts, and the nightmarish pocket dimension where it torments its prey combine to create an unforgettable and deeply disturbing anomaly.

The extreme and morally repugnant measures required for its re-containment, epitomized by the "Femur Breaker" protocol, underscore the desperation the Foundation faces when dealing with such an elusive and malevolent entity. SCP-106 is not just a monster to be locked away; it is a persistent, seeping corruption, a hunter from a personal hell that can turn any solid structure into its hunting ground. It serves as a chilling reminder that some horrors cannot be walled off, only temporarily appeased by unthinkable sacrifices, and that the price of containing such entities can be a slow corrosion of the Foundation's own humanity.
