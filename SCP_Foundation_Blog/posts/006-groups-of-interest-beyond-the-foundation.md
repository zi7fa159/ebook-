# Groups of Interest (GoIs): Beyond the Foundation's Shadow

## 1. Introduction: A World Teeming with Anomalous Agendas

The SCP Foundation, despite its vast resources and global reach, is not the only organization aware of and interacting with the anomalous. The SCP universe is populated by a diverse array of "Groups of Interest" (GoIs), each with its own distinct ideology, goals, methods, and relationship with the paranormal. These GoIs range from rival clandestine organizations and religious cults to artistic collectives and commercial enterprises, all vying for control, understanding, or exploitation of anomalous phenomena.

Understanding these Groups of Interest is crucial for appreciating the broader political and ideological landscape of the SCP world. They provide context for the Foundation's actions, serve as antagonists or occasional, uneasy allies, and enrich the lore with a multitude of perspectives on what the anomalous means and how humanity should interact with it. This article will introduce some of the most prominent and influential GoIs that share the shadowy stage with the Foundation.

## 2. The Global Occult Coalition (GOC) / United Nations Global Occult Coalition (UNGOC)

*   **Ideology/Goals:** "Destroying the paranormal." The GOC, often depicted as a clandestine arm of the United Nations, views most anomalous phenomena (which they call "parathreats" or "Type Greens") as inherently dangerous and unnatural aberrations that must be neutralized or destroyed for the protection of humanity and the preservation of normalcy. Their motto is often cited as "Fivefold Mission: Survive, Conceal, Protect, Destroy, Educate."
*   **Methods:** The GOC possesses significant military power, advanced technology (often derived from studying and reverse-engineering parathreats), and a highly organized structure. They are known for their "scorched earth" approach to anomalies, preferring outright destruction over containment, which often puts them in direct conflict with the Foundation's "Secure, Contain, Protect" philosophy. They employ specialized strike teams (PHYSICS Division) and thaumaturgists (PTOLEMY Division).
*   **Relationship with Foundation:** Primarily antagonistic due to conflicting methodologies. While both aim to protect humanity, their approaches differ drastically. The Foundation sees the GOC as reckless and trigger-happy, potentially destroying anomalies that could be studied or even prove useful. The GOC views the Foundation as hoarders of dangerous items, risking catastrophe by keeping so many anomalies "alive." Occasional, uneasy truces or collaborations occur when facing overwhelming common threats.
*   **Key Traits:** Bureaucratic, militaristic, technologically advanced, ruthless pragmatism towards destruction.

## 3. The Chaos Insurgency (CI)

*   **Ideology/Goals:** Originally a splinter group that defected from the SCP Foundation (specifically, a rogue element of MTF Alpha-1 "Red Right Hand" in some canons), the Chaos Insurgency's motives are often portrayed as a desire to exploit anomalies for their own gain – be it personal power, global destabilization, weaponization, or achieving some ill-defined "new world order." They believe humanity should not just hide from anomalies but master and use them.
*   **Methods:** Covert operations, terrorism, arms dealing (often involving anomalous weaponry), infiltration of governments and other organizations, and direct raids on Foundation facilities to acquire SCPs. They are generally depicted as ruthless, well-equipped, and possessing a deep understanding of Foundation protocols, which they use to their advantage.
*   **Relationship with Foundation:** Actively hostile. The CI is one of the Foundation's primary and most dangerous antagonists. They represent a direct ideological counterpoint and a constant operational threat.
*   **Key Traits:** Ruthless, opportunistic, militaristic, pro-weaponization of anomalies, often enigmatic in their ultimate aims.

## 4. The Serpent's Hand

*   **Ideology/Goals:** Advocates for the rights, acceptance, and liberation of anomalous individuals and objects. They believe that anomalies are natural parts of the universe and should not be suppressed, imprisoned, or destroyed. They seek a world where humans and the anomalous can coexist openly. They are closely associated with the "Wanderer's Library," an anomalous nexus of knowledge accessible to those who know how to find it.
*   **Methods:** Covert operations, infiltration to free SCPs, disseminating information about the anomalous, providing sanctuary for anomalous individuals, and occasionally direct confrontation with Foundation or GOC forces. They often utilize anomalous abilities themselves.
*   **Relationship with Foundation:** Generally hostile, as the Foundation's containment mission is directly opposed to the Hand's liberation goals. However, some Hand members may be more moderate, seeking dialogue or specific reforms rather than all-out war.
*   **Key Traits:** Idealistic (often to a fault), pro-anomaly rights, decentralized, knowledgeable about esoteric lore, connected to the Wanderer's Library.

## 5. Are We Cool Yet? (AWCY?)

*   **Ideology/Goals:** An anarchic, decentralized collective of "anartists" (anomalous artists) who create art using anomalous materials, processes, or concepts. Their motivations range from pure artistic expression and social commentary to seeking notoriety or simply causing chaos. Their works ("anart") often have unpredictable and dangerous side effects.
*   **Methods:** Creation and public exhibition of anomalous art pieces. These exhibitions can be dangerous and often attract Foundation attention. They communicate through obscure online forums and manifestos.
*   **Relationship with Foundation:** Primarily a nuisance, though some anart pieces can be Keter-level threats. The Foundation generally tries to contain their creations and shut down their exhibitions. AWCY? views the Foundation as censors and oppressors of artistic freedom.
*   **Key Traits:** Anarchic, artistic, unpredictable, often reckless, focused on "coolness" and impact over safety.

## 6. Marshall, Carter, and Dark Ltd. (MC&D)

*   **Ideology/Goals:** A private, extremely exclusive "gentlemen's club" and business that caters to the super-rich and powerful, dealing in the acquisition, sale, and experience of anomalous objects, entities, and services. Their primary motivation is profit and providing unique, often decadent or dangerous, experiences for their clientele.
*   **Methods:** Operating through a network of shell corporations, auctions, and private deals. They employ agents to acquire anomalies, often through theft, bribery, or exploitation. They are known for their professionalism, discretion, and ruthlessness in business.
*   **Relationship with Foundation:** Primarily a rival in acquiring anomalies. The Foundation views MC&D as amoral profiteers who risk exposing anomalies and causing harm through their commercial activities. MC&D sees the Foundation as interfering with their business. Direct conflict is usually avoided in favor of covert competition.
*   **Key Traits:** Capitalistic, elitist, profit-driven, discreet, professional, amoral.

## 7. Doctor Wondertainment

*   **Ideology/Goals:** An individual, entity, or possibly a collective that creates and distributes anomalous toys, games, and products under the "Doctor Wondertainment" brand. Their products are often whimsical and brightly colored but frequently possess dangerous or reality-bending properties, sometimes unintentionally. Their ultimate motives are unclear – they could be genuinely trying to bring joy (in a very strange way), conducting experiments, or something else entirely.
*   **Methods:** Creation and distribution of anomalous products, often targeting children or collectors. These products sometimes appear mysteriously on store shelves or are delivered directly.
*   **Relationship with Foundation:** The Foundation views Dr. Wondertainment products as dangerous SCPs that need to be contained to prevent harm to civilians, especially children. The Foundation actively tries to intercept and secure these items. Dr. Wondertainment's direct interaction with the Foundation is rare and enigmatic.
*   **Key Traits:** Whimsical yet dangerous, focused on toys and entertainment, mysterious motivations, often child-like branding.

## 8. The Church of the Broken God (CotBG)

*   **Ideology/Goals:** A complex set of related religious sects that worship a deity known as "The Broken God" (often identified with the "MEKHANE" of Sarkic lore). They believe their god was shattered in a primordial conflict and seek to collect and reassemble its divine components – which are often powerful and dangerous SCPs (particularly mechanical or clockwork ones). They believe this will bring about a new age of technological and spiritual perfection, or in some interpretations, restore order to a chaotic universe.
*   **Major Sects:**
    *   **The Cogwork Orthodox Church:** The largest and most traditional sect, focused on literal reconstruction of the God.
    *   **The Church of Maxwellism:** A more modern, digitally-focused sect that interprets the Broken God in terms of information networks and cybernetics, often seeking to create a digital "God-Signal."
    *   **The Divisionists/Fragmentists (Heretical):** Believe the God is meant to remain broken, with each fragment holding unique sanctity.
*   **Methods:** Seeking out, acquiring, and sometimes worshipping mechanical or technological SCPs. They can range from peaceful scholars to militant zealots. Some sects are highly technologically advanced.
*   **Relationship with Foundation:** Often antagonistic, as the Foundation seeks to contain the very artifacts the Church wishes to acquire or integrate. However, some less militant factions might be more neutral or even unknowingly cooperative in specific contexts.
*   **Key Traits:** Religious, technologically focused (especially mechanical/digital), diverse internal factions, driven by prophecy and divine reconstruction.

## 9. Sarkicism (Nälkä)

*   **Ideology/Goals:** An ancient and often terrifying religious/philosophical system that revolves around biological manipulation, flesh-crafting ("Karcism"), ritual sacrifice, and the worship of powerful, often grotesque, bio-organic entities known as "Klavigars" who serve a progenitor deity called "Grand Karcist Ion." Sarkicism seeks to elevate humanity beyond its physical limitations through organic apotheosis, often embracing disease, mutation, and consumption as sacred acts. They are the ancient enemies of the Mekhanites (Church of the Broken God).
*   **Major Sects:**
    *   **Proto-Sarkic:** More primitive, tribalistic cults found in remote areas.
    *   **Neo-Sarkic:** More sophisticated, often wealthy and influential individuals operating secretly within modern society.
*   **Methods:** Biological rituals, flesh-crafting, use of bio-anomalies, indoctrination, and often brutal practices. They seek to spread their influence and bring about an age dominated by their "halmek" (sacred flesh).
*   **Relationship with Foundation:** Extremely hostile. The Foundation views Sarkicism as a major existential threat due to its potential for pandemic outbreaks, grotesque transformations, and the summoning of powerful, hostile entities.
*   **Key Traits:** Biological/flesh-based, ancient, ritualistic, hierarchical, often body-horror themed, driven by organic apotheosis and worship of flesh deities.

## 10. The Fifth Church (Fifthism)

*   **Ideology/Goals:** A surreal and reality-bending religious movement centered around the concept of "Five" or a cosmic starfish-like entity. Fifthist beliefs are often nonsensical, contradictory, and memetically infectious, aiming to spread their "truth" and bring about a transformation of reality aligned with their bizarre cosmology. They are heavily associated with smoke, stars, and the number five.
*   **Methods:** Spreading Fifthist ideology through memetic means (art, music, propaganda), infiltrating media and entertainment, and performing rituals that can warp reality.
*   **Relationship with Foundation:** The Foundation views Fifthism as a dangerous memetic and reality-altering threat. They actively work to suppress its spread and contain its influence. Interactions are often confusing and disorienting for Foundation personnel.
*   **Key Traits:** Surreal, memetic, reality-bending, obsessed with the number five and stars, often nonsensical or Dadaistic.

## 11. Other Notable GoIs

The SCP universe is rich with many other, often more niche, Groups of Interest:
*   **ORIA (Office for the Reclamation of Islamic Artifacts):** A shadowy organization operating in the Middle East, focused on securing and weaponizing anomalies according to a specific interpretation of Islamic prophecy.
*   **GRU Division "P" (Psychotronics Division):** A remnant of a Soviet-era agency dedicated to researching and weaponizing psychic phenomena and anomalies for the USSR. Though officially disbanded, its influence and rogue elements persist.
*   **The Horizon Initiative:** A multi-faith organization (Abrahamic religions) that aims to manage and, if necessary, neutralize anomalous threats that conflict with their religious doctrines, sometimes cooperating or clashing with the Foundation.
*   **Unusual Incidents Unit (UIU), FBI:** A significantly underfunded and often ridiculed division of the US Federal Bureau of Investigation that deals with paranormal occurrences within US borders. Often outmatched by the anomalies they encounter and reliant on the Foundation for serious threats.

## 12. Conclusion: A Complex Web of Anomalous Politics

The Groups of Interest transform the SCP universe from a simple "Foundation vs. Monsters" narrative into a complex ecosystem of competing powers, ideologies, and agendas. They demonstrate that humanity's reaction to the anomalous is not monolithic; it is as varied and fractured as humanity itself.

These GoIs provide endless storytelling opportunities, allowing writers to explore different facets of the paranormal, create intricate political dynamics, and challenge the Foundation's methods and worldview. They ensure that the Foundation's mission to Secure, Contain, and Protect is never simple, as they are not just fighting the anomalies themselves, but also those who would worship, liberate, exploit, or misunderstand them. The world of the SCP Foundation is a crowded and dangerous place, and the shadows are full of more than just SCPs.
