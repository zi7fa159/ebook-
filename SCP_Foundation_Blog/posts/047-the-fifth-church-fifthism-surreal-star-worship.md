# The Fifth Church (Fifthism): Surreal Star-Worship and Reality Corruption

## 1. Introduction: The Smoke, The Stars, and The Number Five

Among the more bizarre and reality-bending Groups of Interest encountered by the SCP Foundation, The Fifth Church (often referred to as Fifthism or the Church of the Fifth Star) stands out for its surreal cosmology, memetically infectious beliefs, and its worship of a strange, cosmic, starfish-like entity or concept associated with the number five. Fifthist anomalies and activities often involve smoke, stars, altered perceptions, and a nonsensical, dream-like logic that can be incredibly disorienting and dangerous.

This article will attempt to navigate the strange currents of Fifthist belief, exploring their origins, doctrines (such as they are), methods of propagation, and the unique threat they pose to consensus reality and individual sanity.

## 2. Core Beliefs and Cosmology: A Surreal Universe

Fifthist doctrine is notoriously difficult to pin down, often appearing contradictory, nonsensical, or deliberately obscure. However, some recurring elements and themes provide a glimpse into their worldview:

*   **The Number Five:** The number five is sacred and central to their beliefs. It appears in their symbolism, rituals, and understanding of the universe. The significance of "five" is multifaceted and often unexplained.
*   **The Fifth Star / The Great Starfish:** They worship or revere a cosmic entity, sometimes depicted as a massive, five-pointed starfish, a constellation, or a trans-dimensional being associated with the "Fifth World" or a higher reality. This entity is often linked to themes of smoke, starlight, and transformation.
*   **Smoke and Stars:** Smoke (particularly cigarette smoke or otherworldly types of smoke) and starlight are important symbolic and perhaps literal elements in their rituals and manifestations. They may believe smoke can act as a conduit or that stars hold hidden truths.
*   **Transformation and Ascension:** A core goal of many Fifthists seems to be achieving a form of "ascension" or transformation, often involving merging with the cosmic starfish, entering the "Fifth World," or shedding mundane reality for a higher, more "real" state of being. This transformation is usually depicted as bizarre and horrifying from an outside perspective.
*   **Reality is Malleable/Illusory:** Fifthism often posits that consensus reality is a fragile construct or an illusion. Their activities can cause local reality to fray or become infused with their surreal logic.
*   **Non-Linear Time/Logic:** Fifthist beliefs and manifestations often defy linear time and conventional logic, operating more like a dream or a surrealist painting.
*   **The "Truth" of Five:** They believe they possess a unique insight into the true nature of the universe, centered around the power and significance of "five."
*   **Multiple "Denominations" or Interpretations:** Like many religions, Fifthism is not monolithic. Different Fifthist cults or cells may have varying interpretations of their core tenets, leading to diverse and sometimes conflicting practices. Examples include:
    *   **The Church of the Fifth Star:** More traditional, focused on worship and ritual.
    *   **The Constellation Starfish:** Perhaps a more abstract or cosmic interpretation.
    *   **Smokestackism/The Five Smokestacks:** Industrial or urban-themed variants.

## 3. Methods of Propagation and Influence: Memetic Infection

Fifthism primarily spreads through memetic and cognitohazardous means:

*   **Anomalous Media:** Fifthist beliefs are often embedded in music (especially jazz, blues, or psychedelic rock), films, literature, art, and even corporate branding or advertising. Exposure to this media can subtly or overtly indoctrinate individuals.
    *   **SCP-1425 ("Star Signals"):** A television mini-series that acts as a potent Fifthist memetic vector, converting viewers.
*   **Rituals and "Happenings":** Fifthist groups conduct bizarre rituals, public performances, or "happenings" that can alter local reality, expose attendees to Fifthist concepts, or directly transform participants.
*   **Subtle Infiltration:** They may infiltrate mundane organizations, businesses (e.g., "The Fifth National Bank"), or artistic communities to spread their influence.
*   **Cognitohazardous Symbols and Environments:** Certain symbols, patterns, or environments infused with Fifthist energy can directly affect perception and belief.
*   **Dream Logic:** Their influence can sometimes spread through dreams or altered states of consciousness.

The spread of Fifthism is often insidious, bypassing rational thought and appealing directly to subconscious desires, anxieties, or a yearning for something "more."

## 4. Anomalous Manifestations and Effects

When Fifthist influence takes hold, reality can become very strange:
*   **Perceptual Distortions:** Colors may shift, objects may appear to breathe or warp, and individuals may experience synesthesia or hallucinations.
*   **Physical Transformations:** In advanced stages, individuals may undergo grotesque physical transformations, often involving starfish-like appendages, extra orifices, or becoming part of a larger Fifthist biological construct.
*   **Localized Reality Bending:** Areas under strong Fifthist influence can become pocket realities governed by their surreal logic. Gravity might change, impossible geometry can manifest, and time can become non-linear.
*   **Appearance of Fifthist Entities:** Beings from the "Fifth World" or manifestations of the Great Starfish might appear.
*   **Obsessive Behavior:** Converts become obsessed with the number five, smoke, stars, and spreading Fifthist "truth."

## 5. Relationship with the SCP Foundation

The Foundation views The Fifth Church as a dangerous, reality-destabilizing memetic threat:
*   **Containment of Fifthist Anomalies:** The Foundation actively contains SCPs created by or associated with Fifthism (e.g., SCP-1425, SCP-3005 "A Light That Died").
*   **Counter-Memetics and Amnestics:** Efforts are made to develop counter-memes to protect personnel and the public, and amnestics are used to erase exposure to Fifthist indoctrination.
*   **Monitoring and Disruption:** The Foundation monitors suspected Fifthist groups and attempts to disrupt their rituals and the spread of their anomalous media.
*   **Difficult to Understand:** The inherently nonsensical and surreal nature of Fifthism makes it difficult for the Foundation's typically rational and scientific approach to fully grasp or predict their actions.
*   **Information Hazard:** Even studying Fifthism can be dangerous, as exposure to their core concepts can be memetically infectious. Foundation researchers working on Fifthism often require specialized psychological screening and memetic protection.

The Foundation's battle against Fifthism is a battle for consensus reality and sanity itself.

## 6. Key SCPs Associated with The Fifth Church

*   **SCP-1425 ("Star Signals"):** A television mini-series that serves as a powerful vector for Fifthist indoctrination, causing viewers to adopt Fifthist beliefs and sometimes undergo physical transformations.
*   **SCP-3005 ("A Light That Died"):** An extremely dangerous and reality-altering Fifthist anomaly that caused a localized CK-Class Reality Restructuring scenario in a small town, transforming its inhabitants and environment into a nightmarish Fifthist landscape. It demonstrates the catastrophic potential of unchecked Fifthist influence.
*   **SCP-1981 ("Ronald Reagan Cut Up While Talking"):** While not explicitly Fifthist in its main article, its surreal and memetic nature, and the appearance of a shadowy figure (sometimes interpreted as a Fifthist entity or related to the Scarlet King, who has thematic overlaps) has led to its association in some canons.
*   **The "International" Theme (from the "Competitive Eschatology" Canon):** This series of tales and SCPs often features Fifthism as a major player in a cosmic game between various apocalyptic cults and deities, with "The International" being a powerful, globe-spanning Fifthist organization.

## 7. Themes and Interpretations of Fifthism

Fifthism is a rich source of thematic exploration:
*   **Surrealism and Absurdism:** It embraces the bizarre and nonsensical, reflecting themes from surrealist art and absurdist philosophy.
*   **The Power of Ideas/Memes:** It highlights how ideas, even irrational ones, can spread like viruses and reshape perception and reality.
*   **Critique of Religion/Cults:** It can be seen as a satirical or critical take on the more bizarre or manipulative aspects of organized religion and cult behavior.
*   **Fear of the Unknown/Cosmic Horror:** The Great Starfish and the "Fifth World" represent incomprehensible cosmic forces that are indifferent or hostile to human sanity.
*   **Loss of Identity and Individuality:** Conversion to Fifthism often involves a loss of self and absorption into a collective, transformed state.
*   **The Nature of Reality:** Fifthism constantly questions the stability and "truth" of consensus reality.

## 8. The "Coolness" Factor and Counter-Culture Appeal

There's an element of "counter-culture" or "underground cool" associated with some portrayals of Fifthism, particularly in its connection to fringe art, music, and alternative lifestyles. This can make it appealing to individuals seeking something outside the mainstream, even before its anomalous properties take full effect. This mirrors how some real-world cults attract followers.

## 9. Conclusion: Dancing to the Tune of a Five-Pointed Star

The Fifth Church is one of the most unique and conceptually challenging Groups of Interest in the SCP Foundation universe. It is not a traditional organization with armies or political goals, but a pervasive, memetic faith that seeks to unravel reality and transform humanity according to its alien, star-born Gnosticcism. Its allure lies in its mystery, its dream-like logic, and its promise of a reality beyond the mundane – a promise that invariably leads to madness, transformation, and horror.

The Foundation's struggle against Fifthism is a battle against an idea, a creeping surrealism that threatens to overwrite consensus reality with the nonsensical truths of a five-pointed cosmos. In a world of tangible monsters and physical threats, The Fifth Church reminds us that sometimes the most dangerous anomalies are those that whisper from the static, shimmer in the starlight, and curl in the tendrils of smoke, inviting us to join a dance whose steps lead only to a beautiful, terrible, and utterly alien transformation.
