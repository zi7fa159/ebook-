# Foundation Sites and Areas: The Hidden Bastions of Containment

## 1. Introduction: Where the Anomalies Reside

The SCP Foundation's global mission to secure, contain, and protect anomalous objects, entities, and phenomena necessitates a vast, clandestine network of specialized facilities. These are not ordinary buildings; they are fortified, often hidden, bastions of research and containment, designed to house reality-bending horrors and incomprehensible wonders. Known broadly as Sites and Areas, these locations are the front lines in the Foundation's unending war to maintain normalcy.

Understanding the nature and diversity of Foundation facilities is key to appreciating the scale and complexity of its operations. From sprawling underground complexes to unassuming suburban houses, each facility is tailored to specific containment needs and operational roles. This article explores the types, functions, security measures, and significance of these hidden fortresses.

## 2. Sites vs. Areas: A Matter of Secrecy and Scope

While the terms are sometimes used interchangeably, there's often a subtle distinction implied in Foundation lore:

*   **Sites (Generally Overt or Known, Relatively Speaking):**
    *   **Nature:** Sites are facilities whose existence, while not public knowledge, is generally known to certain clandestine governmental agencies or specific Groups of Interest that the Foundation interacts with (e.g., the Global Occult Coalition might be aware of the designation "Site-19").
    *   **Function:** Often larger facilities, housing numerous SCPs (especially Safe and Euclid class), conducting extensive research, serving as administrative hubs, or manufacturing centers for specialized equipment.
    *   **Cover Story:** They may operate under plausible cover stories (e.g., private research institution, military base, pharmaceutical company) if their presence cannot be entirely hidden.
    *   **Examples:** Site-19 (one of the largest and most well-known, housing a multitude of SCPs), Site-17 (primarily focused on humanoid SCP containment and research), Site-64 (a major research and containment facility).

*   **Areas (Generally Covert and Highly Secret):**
    *   **Nature:** Areas are facilities whose existence is kept secret from as many external (and sometimes internal) parties as possible. They are typically smaller, more specialized, or house particularly sensitive or dangerous anomalies.
    *   **Function:** Often used for the containment of highly dangerous Keter-class SCPs, research into extremely hazardous phenomena, or operations requiring utmost discretion (e.g., housing Thaumiel-class SCPs, O5 Council meeting locations).
    *   **Location:** May be situated in extremely remote locations (deep ocean, Antarctica, other dimensions) or hidden in plain sight with exceptional camouflage and misdirection.
    *   **Examples:** Armed Bio-Containment Area-14 (for biological hazards), Dimensional Research Area-52 (fictional example for extradimensional research).

**Important Note:** This distinction is a general guideline and not a rigid rule. Authors on the SCP Wiki use these terms with varying degrees of consistency. Some Sites can be extremely covert, and some Areas might be relatively large. The key takeaway is that the Foundation uses a range of facility types with varying levels of secrecy and specialization.

## 3. Types of Foundation Facilities by Function

Beyond the Site/Area distinction, facilities are often categorized by their primary purpose:

*   **Containment Facilities:**
    *   **Primary Role:** Securely housing SCP objects and entities.
    *   **Features:** Specialized containment cells tailored to individual SCP needs (e.g., reinforced steel, acid-resistant lining, psionic dampeners, aquatic tanks, reality anchors). Extensive security systems, rapid response teams.
*   **Research Facilities:**
    *   **Primary Role:** Studying SCPs to understand their properties, develop better containment procedures, and potentially neutralize them or harness their effects (in the case of Thaumiel research).
    *   **Features:** Advanced laboratories, testing chambers, access to D-Class personnel for experiments, extensive data analysis capabilities.
*   **Administrative Facilities:**
    *   **Primary Role:** Housing command and control centers, departmental offices (e.g., Ethics Committee, Internal Security, Logistics), data archives, and communication hubs.
*   **Manufacturing Facilities:**
    *   **Primary Role:** Producing specialized equipment, amnestics, weaponry, or components for containment cells that cannot be sourced from the civilian market.
*   **Observation Posts/Outposts:**
    *   **Primary Role:** Monitoring immobile SCPs (e.g., anomalous locations, large-scale phenomena) or areas of known anomalous activity. Often small and discreet.
*   **Dimensional/Extradimensional Facilities:**
    *   **Primary Role:** Containing or researching SCPs that originate from or exist within other dimensions, alternate realities, or altered spacetime. May involve reality-stabilizing technology.
*   **Biological/Biohazard Containment Facilities:**
    *   **Primary Role:** Dealing with anomalous diseases, infectious entities, or SCPs with dangerous biological properties. Require extreme quarantine measures (BSL-4 or higher equivalent).
*   **Humanoid Containment Facilities:**
    *   **Primary Role:** Specifically designed for the containment, study, and interaction with sentient humanoid SCPs. May include more "humane" (by Foundation standards) living quarters, interview rooms, and specialized psychological staff.

Many larger Sites are multi-purpose, combining several of these functions.

## 4. Location, Location, Location: Secrecy and Strategic Placement

The placement of Foundation facilities is a critical consideration:

*   **Remote Locations:** Many facilities, especially those housing dangerous Keters or requiring large footprints, are built in remote, unpopulated areas (deserts, tundras, deep underground, ocean floor) to minimize civilian exposure in case of a breach and to avoid detection.
*   **Hidden in Plain Sight:** Some smaller facilities or those needing proximity to urban centers (e.g., for personnel recruitment, logistics, or monitoring urban anomalies) might be disguised as ordinary businesses, government buildings, or even private residences. This requires sophisticated cover operations.
*   **Underground Complexes:** A common trope is the sprawling underground Site, offering enhanced security, protection from surface threats, and discretion.
*   **Extradimensional Spaces:** In some advanced lore, the Foundation might utilize pocket dimensions or stabilized extradimensional spaces to house particularly problematic SCPs or to conduct research that would be too dangerous in baseline reality.
*   **Proximity to SCPs:** Some facilities are built directly around or near immobile SCPs (e.g., SCP-087 "The Stairwell" has a facility built around its entrance).

The choice of location is always a balance between operational needs, security requirements, and the imperative to maintain secrecy.

## 5. Security Measures: Fort Knox on Steroids

Foundation facilities are among the most secure locations on Earth (and sometimes beyond it). Security measures are multi-layered and comprehensive:

*   **Physical Security:** Reinforced walls, blast doors, automated turrets, vehicle traps, perimeter fences, seismic sensors, K-9 units (often anomalously enhanced).
*   **Technological Security:** Advanced surveillance systems (CCTV, thermal, memetic filters), biometric access controls, laser grids, pressure plates, motion detectors, cyber-warfare defenses.
*   **Personnel Security:** Armed guards, Mobile Task Force garrisons, internal security departments (the "Men in Black" of the Foundation), rigorous background checks, psychological screening, hierarchical clearance levels.
*   **Anomalous Security:** Use of Safe or Thaumiel-class SCPs for defense or detection (e.g., reality anchors to prevent teleportation, precognitive entities for threat assessment, psionically shielded areas).
*   **Information Security:** Compartmentalization of information ("need-to-know"), encrypted communication networks, data purges, counter-intelligence operations.
*   **Containment Breach Protocols:** Pre-defined emergency procedures for various breach scenarios, including site-wide lockdowns, evacuation routes (for essential personnel), deployment of specific MTFs, and, in extreme cases, on-site nuclear devices as a last resort to prevent catastrophic escape or information loss.
*   **Deception and Misdirection:** False fronts, dummy facilities, disinformation campaigns to mislead external threats.

Despite these measures, containment breaches are a constant threat, especially with Keter-class SCPs.

## 6. Life Within a Foundation Facility

For Foundation personnel, life within a Site or Area is often characterized by:

*   **High Stress and Pressure:** The constant proximity to dangerous anomalies, the strict adherence to protocols, and the weight of secrecy create a high-stress environment.
*   **Isolation:** Many facilities are remote, leading to social isolation for personnel stationed there for long tours.
*   **Compartmentalization:** Personnel often only know what is necessary for their specific job, leading to a fragmented understanding of the facility's overall operations.
*   **Strict Hierarchy and Discipline:** The Foundation operates with a quasi-military command structure.
*   **Access to Amenities (Varies):** Larger, long-term facilities might have relatively comfortable living quarters, cafeterias, recreational areas, and medical bays to maintain personnel well-being and efficiency. Smaller or more hazardous outposts might be extremely spartan.
*   **Constant Vigilance:** A sense that danger could manifest at any moment.

The culture within each facility can also vary depending on its director, its primary function, and the types of SCPs it houses.

## 7. Notable Named Sites/Areas (Examples)

While there are countless designated facilities, some have become particularly prominent in SCP lore:

*   **Site-19:** Often depicted as one of the largest and most important Foundation facilities, housing a vast number of diverse SCPs, including many "classic" ones like SCP-173. It's a frequent target in tales and a common setting for major events.
*   **Site-17:** Known for its focus on humanoid SCP containment and research. Often portrayed as having more resources dedicated to the psychological well-being and study of sentient anomalies.
*   **Site-81:** A major research and containment hub, often featured in tales involving scientific breakthroughs or complex anomalous phenomena.
*   **Area-12:** A biological research and containment facility, dealing with infectious or biohazardous SCPs.
*   **Lunar Area-32:** A Foundation facility located on the Moon, used for containing SCPs that are safer or easier to manage off-planet, or for research requiring vacuum/low gravity.

The specific numbers and functions of Sites/Areas are flexible, allowing authors to create new facilities as needed for their stories.

## 8. Conclusion: The Unseen Front Lines

Foundation Sites and Areas are the unseen, unsung fortresses that stand between humanity and the myriad horrors and wonders of the anomalous world. They are complex, dangerous, and absolutely essential to the Foundation's mission. From the deepest subterranean bunkers to hidden urban enclaves, these facilities represent the cutting edge of containment science, the dedication of countless personnel, and the grim reality of a world where normalcy is a carefully constructed illusion.

Each Site and Area is a story in itself, a testament to the Foundation's ingenuity, resources, and the constant, precarious struggle to keep the inexplicable locked away. They are the physical embodiment of "Secure, Contain, Protect," the bastions where humanity makes its stand against the darkness – and sometimes, against the light that burns too bright.
