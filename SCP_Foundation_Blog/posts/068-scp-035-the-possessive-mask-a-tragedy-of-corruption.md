# SCP-035: The Possessive Mask - A Corrosive Tragedy of Personality

## 1. Introduction: The Alluring, Deadly Visage

SCP-035, known as "The Possessive Mask," is a Keter-class sentient object that takes the form of a white porcelain comedy mask, though it can periodically change to tragedy. Its primary anomalous property is its ability to seize control of any humanoid that wears it or comes into close proximity, supplanting their personality with its own manipulative, intelligent, and highly malevolent consciousness. Compounding this psychological threat, SCP-035 also secretes a viscous, highly corrosive liquid that degrades any material it touches, including its host bodies, making its containment a constant, perilous challenge.

This article delves into the insidious nature of SCP-035, its corrosive influence (both literal and metaphorical), its interactions with the Foundation, and the tragic fate of those who fall under its sway.

## 2. Physical Description: The Shifting Faces of Comedy and Tragedy

*   **Appearance:** SCP-035 is typically a white porcelain mask, resembling a traditional comedic theatrical mask with exaggerated, smiling features.
*   **Changing Visage:** Periodically (sometimes spontaneously, sometimes in response to events), the mask will change its expression to that of a tragedy mask, with downturned, sorrowful features. It can then revert to comedy. This shift can be unsettling and may reflect its "mood" or intentions.
*   **No Apparent Mechanism for Change:** The means by which it changes expression are unknown; the porcelain itself seems to morph.
*   **Durability:** The mask itself appears to be highly resistant to damage.

The duality of its comedic and tragic faces hints at the complex and often cruel personality within.

## 3. Anomalous Properties: Possession, Corruption, and Decay

SCP-035 possesses several dangerous anomalous properties:

*   **Mental Compulsion and Possession:**
    *   **Proximity Effect:** Individuals within a certain range of SCP-035 (typically 1.5 to 2 meters) experience a strong, often irresistible urge to pick it up and place it over their face.
    *   **Personality Overwrite:** Once the mask is worn, SCP-035's consciousness takes complete control of the host body. The original personality is suppressed, effectively "braindead," or trapped within their own mind, aware but unable to act.
    *   **Retained Knowledge and Skills:** SCP-035 gains access to the host's memories, knowledge, and skills, which it can use to its advantage.
*   **The Corrosive Secretion:**
    *   SCP-035 constantly secretes a black, highly corrosive, and degenerative viscous liquid from its eye and mouth openings.
    *   **Material Degradation:** This liquid rapidly corrodes and decays any material it comes into contact with, including metals, stone, plastics, and organic matter. This makes long-term containment within a standard chamber extremely difficult.
    *   **Host Body Decay:** Most critically, the secretion causes rapid and severe biological decay in any host body wearing the mask. The host literally rots away while SCP-035 is in control, usually becoming unusable within a matter of days or weeks. This necessitates a constant supply of new hosts if SCP-035 is to remain "mobile."
*   **Sentience and Intelligence:**
    *   SCP-035 is highly intelligent, manipulative, charismatic (when it chooses to be), and possesses a vast amount of knowledge, possibly accumulated over centuries from previous hosts.
    *   It is capable of complex conversation, psychological manipulation, and strategic planning.
    *   It often displays a sadistic and cruel personality, enjoying psychological torment and the decay it inflicts.
*   **Telepathic Influence (Implied/Limited):** While not its primary method, some interpretations suggest it can exert a subtle telepathic influence to enhance its compulsion effect or to communicate with individuals even when not being worn, though this is usually limited.

Its combination of psychological domination and physical corruption makes it a formidable threat.

## 4. Object Class: Keter

SCP-035 is classified as **Keter**.
*   **Why Keter?**
    *   **Difficult to Contain Physically:** The corrosive secretion means it cannot be kept in a standard containment cell for long. It constantly damages its surroundings and requires specialized, frequently replaced containment.
    *   **Persistent Mental Compulsion:** Its compulsion effect is a constant threat to any personnel who come near it, risking new possession incidents.
    *   **High Intelligence and Manipulativeness:** SCP-035 is actively and intelligently trying to escape, manipulate its captors, and gain new hosts. It is not a passive threat.
    *   **Information Security Risk:** By possessing Foundation personnel, it can gain access to classified information.
    *   **Need for Host Replacement:** If the Foundation wishes to interact with or study SCP-035's personality (which is sometimes necessary for understanding its motives or potential future threats), it must provide it with new hosts, which is an ethically fraught and dangerous process.

Its Keter status reflects the constant, active effort required to prevent it from breaching containment either physically (through corrosion) or psychologically (through possession).

## 5. Special Containment Procedures: A Never-Ending Battle Against Decay

Containing SCP-035 is a continuous struggle against its corrosive nature and manipulative intelligence:
*   **Specialized Containment Cell:** SCP-035 is to be kept within a hermetically sealed glass case, no less than 10 centimeters (4 inches) thick. This case is to be contained within a steel, iron, and lead-shielded room.
*   **Regular Case Replacement:** The glass case must be inspected daily for any signs of degradation from the corrosive secretions and replaced as necessary (often frequently).
*   **No Human Contact:** Absolutely no personnel are to enter SCP-035's containment cell or come within its compulsion range without specific O5-level authorization for approved experimental procedures (which are extremely rare).
*   **Remote Handling Only:** If the mask must be moved, it is to be done using robotic arms or other remote manipulation methods.
*   **Host Provision (Historically/Under Extreme Circumstances):**
    *   The Foundation has, at times, provided SCP-035 with D-Class personnel as hosts to facilitate communication or study. This is exceptionally dangerous and ethically problematic due to the rapid decay of the host and the risk of SCP-035 attempting to escape using the host's abilities.
    *   Any host body must be terminated immediately if it shows signs of advanced decay or if SCP-035 becomes uncooperative.
*   **Psychological Countermeasures:** Personnel working in the vicinity of SCP-035's containment area may require psychological screening and memetic resistance training to combat its compulsion effect.
*   **Decontamination:** Any area contaminated by SCP-035's secretions must be immediately quarantined and decontaminated using specialized procedures.

The primary challenge is finding materials that can resist its corrosion long enough to be practical for containment.

## 6. SCP-035's Personality: The Charming Sociopath

When possessing a host, SCP-035 exhibits a distinct and dangerous personality:
*   **Charismatic and Manipulative:** It can be charming, persuasive, and eloquent, often attempting to gain the trust or sympathy of researchers or guards to exploit them.
*   **Highly Intelligent and Knowledgeable:** It claims to have knowledge of many historical events, lost secrets, and even the nature of other SCPs, which it may offer in exchange for favors (though its information is not always reliable).
*   **Sadistic and Cruel:** It seems to derive pleasure from psychological torment, the suffering of its hosts, and causing chaos. It has a deeply cynical and misanthropic worldview.
*   **Obsessed with Escape and Influence:** Its primary goals appear to be escaping Foundation custody and gaining influence in the wider world, potentially by possessing individuals in positions of power.
*   **Theatrical and Dramatic:** Fitting its mask form, it often behaves in a theatrical or overly dramatic manner, enjoying wordplay and philosophical sparring.
*   **Patient:** It is willing to wait for long periods, subtly manipulating situations until an opportunity for escape or possession arises.

It is a master psychological manipulator, using its intellect and charm as weapons.

## 7. The Tragedy of the Host

The fate of individuals possessed by SCP-035 is invariably tragic:
*   **Loss of Self:** Their consciousness is suppressed or destroyed, their body hijacked.
*   **Agonizing Decay:** They are forced to experience their own physical form rotting away around them, a uniquely horrifying form of body horror.
*   **Used as a Puppet:** Their skills, memories, and relationships are exploited by SCP-035 for its own ends.
*   **Inevitable Death:** No host body can survive for long due to the corrosive secretions. The Foundation's usual protocol is to terminate the host once it becomes too degraded or if SCP-035 becomes too dangerous.

This makes SCP-035 not just a dangerous object, but a source of profound personal tragedy for its victims.

## 8. Origins and History (Speculative)

The true origin of SCP-035 is unknown, but its claimed knowledge of historical events suggests it is very old.
*   **Ancient Artifact?** Could it be an artifact from an ancient civilization, perhaps used in rituals or theater?
*   **Cursed Object?** The result of a curse or a malevolent entity bound to the mask form?
*   **Extradimensional Entity?** Is the consciousness within the mask an entity from another dimension that uses the mask as a focal point to interact with our reality?

Its ability to change from comedy to tragedy might hint at a dual nature or a complex past.

## 9. Conclusion: The Face of Inescapable Corruption

SCP-035, The Possessive Mask, is a potent symbol of insidious corruption, both physical and psychological. It combines the terror of mental domination with the visceral horror of bodily decay, creating a Keter-class threat that is as intelligent and manipulative as it is destructive. Its charming facade, whether comedy or tragedy, hides a deeply malevolent consciousness that seeks only to control, corrupt, and ultimately consume.

The Foundation's unending struggle to contain SCP-035, constantly replacing its decaying prison and guarding against its psychic lure, highlights the immense challenge posed by sentient, hostile objects that actively work against their confinement. SCP-035 is a grim reminder that some masks hide not just a different face, but a different, far more terrible, soul, eager to wear down the world one host at a time.
