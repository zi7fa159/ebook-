# SCP-087: The Stairwell - A Descent into Auditory Dread and Unknown Depths

## 1. Introduction: The Endless Descent

SCP-087, commonly known as "The Stairwell," is a deceptively simple yet profoundly unsettling Euclid-class anomaly. It appears to be an unlit platform staircase that descends at a steep angle, seemingly without end. What makes SCP-087 so effective as a horror concept is not overt monsters or complex mechanics, but its masterful use of atmosphere, psychological dread, and the fear of the unknown lurking just beyond the limits of perception. Its exploration logs are a chilling testament to the terror of descending into perpetual darkness, accompanied only by unnerving sounds and a growing sense of despair.

This article will explore the physical characteristics of SCP-087, the phenomena encountered within it, its containment, and why its minimalist horror continues to resonate.

## 2. Physical Description: An Uninviting Portal

SCP-087 is located on the campus of [REDACTED] University, disguised as a janitorial closet or a similar unassuming entrance.
*   **The Entrance:** A plain steel door with a standard lock, now reinforced with additional security measures by the Foundation.
*   **The Staircase:** Beyond the door lies a semi-circular platform and a flight of stairs descending at a 38-degree angle. Each flight consists of 13 steps before reaching another semi-circular platform. This pattern repeats.
*   **No Illumination:** The stairwell is entirely unlit. Light sources brought by explorers do not illuminate beyond approximately 1.5 flights of stairs, and shadows appear deeper and more absorbing than normal.
*   **Construction:** The walls, platforms, and stairs appear to be made of concrete. No protrusions, decorations, or alternate exits have ever been found.
*   **Apparent Depth:** Despite numerous exploration attempts, no end to the staircase has ever been reached. The lowest point explored is far beyond the possible structural depth of both the building and the local geology, suggesting SCP-087 is extradimensional or spatially anomalous.

The mundane appearance of a concrete stairwell contrasts sharply with its unnatural properties, creating an immediate sense of unease.

## 3. Anomalous Phenomena within SCP-087

Exploration of SCP-087 has revealed several consistent anomalous phenomena:

*   **The Crying/Pleading Sounds (SCP-087-1):**
    *   **Nature:** The most prominent feature. Explorers consistently report hearing what sounds like a child or young adolescent crying, pleading, or whimpering. The sound is estimated to originate from approximately 200 meters below the initial platform.
    *   **Unchanging Source Distance:** Despite continued descent by explorers, the perceived source of the crying never seems to get closer. This suggests the sound is either a looping auditory illusion, a non-Euclidean acoustic effect, or that the source itself is descending at the same rate.
    *   **Psychological Effect:** The sounds are deeply distressing and often induce feelings of fear, anxiety, and a compulsion in some to locate and help the "child," luring them deeper into the stairwell.
*   **The Face (SCP-087-1, sometimes referred to as SCP-087-A or The Entity):**
    *   **Appearance:** A vaguely humanoid face, described as lacking pupils, nostrils, or a mouth in the conventional sense. Its features are smooth and indistinct.
    *   **Behavior:** It appears silently and suddenly in the darkness, usually just at the edge of an explorer's light source. It observes the explorer without making any sound or overt hostile action.
    *   **Effect:** Encounters with SCP-087-1 invariably cause extreme fear, panic, and an immediate desire to flee the stairwell. It is a potent source of psychological trauma. Its nature and intent are completely unknown. It does not appear to physically attack, but its presence is overwhelmingly terrifying.
*   **The "Knocking" or "Banging":** Some exploration logs mention faint knocking or banging sounds, as if from within the walls or from far below, distinct from the crying.
*   **Atmospheric Pressure and Temperature:** These generally remain consistent with the entrance level, despite the apparent depth, further suggesting anomalous spatial properties.
*   **Radio Silence:** Radio communication often becomes distorted or impossible beyond a certain depth.

The combination of perpetual darkness, the disturbing auditory lure, and the silent, watching face creates an atmosphere of intense psychological horror.

## 4. Object Class: Euclid

SCP-087 is classified as **Euclid**.

*   **Why Euclid?**
    *   **Geographically Stable:** The anomaly is fixed to a single location and does not actively attempt to expand or breach its immediate physical containment (the door).
    *   **Understood (Partially) Hazard:** The primary dangers (psychological trauma, potential for getting lost or injured in the dark) are known, even if the ultimate nature of SCP-087-1 is not.
    *   **Containment is Straightforward (Externally):** Keeping the door locked and guarded prevents unauthorized access.
    *   **Unpredictable Internal Environment:** The phenomena within the stairwell, particularly the behavior and true nature of SCP-087-1, are not fully understood, and exploration carries inherent risks. This prevents a Safe classification.

It's not Keter because it doesn't actively try to escape or cause widespread harm beyond its immediate confines if left undisturbed. The danger is primarily to those who enter it.

## 5. Special Containment Procedures: Sealing the Descent

Containment focuses on preventing access and managing information:

*   **Secure Entrance:** The steel door leading to SCP-087 is to be reinforced and fitted with an electronic locking mechanism. It is disguised as a janitorial closet consistent with the building's design.
*   **Restricted Access:** No personnel are to enter SCP-087. Access requires approval from at least two (2) Level 4 personnel.
*   **No Illumination of the Interior (Generally):** The interior is to remain unlit. Any light sources used during authorized explorations (which are now extremely rare or forbidden) must have a power rating of no more than 75 watts.
*   **Soundproofing:** The area around the door has been soundproofed to prevent the crying sounds from being audible to university staff or students.
*   **Monitoring:** Remote surveillance (e.g., seismic sensors, auditory sensors near the door) may be used to detect any changes in SCP-087's state, though direct internal surveillance is impossible or impractical.

The core of containment is simply keeping the door shut and ensuring no one stumbles upon it or is lured by the sounds.

## 6. Exploration Logs: Chronicles of Terror

The heart of the SCP-087 article lies in its **Exploration Logs (I, II, III, and IV)**. These logs document the experiences of D-Class personnel sent into the stairwell equipped with light sources, cameras, and microphones.
*   **First-Person Perspective:** The logs are transcripts of audio/video recordings, providing an immediate, visceral sense of the descent.
*   **Increasing Dread:** As the D-Class subjects descend, the logs capture their growing fear, paranoia, and reactions to the crying sounds and eventual encounters with SCP-087-1.
*   **Psychological Deterioration:** The subjects often become increasingly agitated, irrational, or desperate to escape.
*   **The Face Encounter:** The climax of each successful exploration (before termination or loss of contact) is usually the terrifying appearance of SCP-087-1, leading to panic and often the end of the recording.
*   **Limited Information Gained:** Despite multiple explorations, very little concrete information about the stairwell's true nature, depth, or the origin of its phenomena has been gained. Each exploration primarily serves to reconfirm the known dangers and the psychological toll.
*   **The Fate of Explorers:** D-Class personnel sent on these missions are generally considered expendable. Their fate after encountering SCP-087-1 or after communication is lost is usually unknown, presumed deceased or lost within the anomaly.

These logs are masterclasses in minimalist horror, relying on atmosphere, sound, and the reader's imagination to create terror, rather than explicit gore or complex monsters. The unknown is the primary source of fear.

## 7. Theories and Interpretations

The true nature of SCP-087 and SCP-087-1 is deliberately left open to interpretation:

*   **Extradimensional Space:** The most common theory is that SCP-087 is a portal to or part of an extradimensional space or pocket dimension that does not adhere to normal Euclidean geometry.
*   **Psychological Construct:** Could SCP-087-1 (the face and the crying) be a manifestation of the observer's fear, a psychic projection, or a memetic entity that preys on psychological vulnerability?
*   **Luring Mechanism:** The crying child sound acts as a lure, drawing victims deeper into the anomaly. The purpose of this luring is unknown. Is SCP-087-1 "feeding" in some way? Is it territorial?
*   **A Prison or Test?** Some theories posit the stairwell could be a prison for SCP-087-1, or a kind of anomalous test or filter.
*   **No Deeper Meaning:** Perhaps it is simply an inexplicable, hostile spatial anomaly with no grander purpose than to be terrifying and dangerous.

The lack of definitive answers is a key part of its enduring appeal.

## 8. SCP-087's Influence and Legacy

*   **Foundational Horror:** SCP-087 is one of the earliest and most iconic "Series I" SCPs, establishing a template for atmospheric, psychological horror within the Foundation universe.
*   **Minimalism in Horror:** It demonstrates how effective horror can be achieved with minimal elements – darkness, sound, a single, fleeting visual.
*   **Inspiration for Games and Media:** The concept of an endless, dark stairwell with a lurking presence has inspired numerous indie horror games and creepypastas. The "SCP-087-B" game, while not directly based on the original article's exploration logs, popularized the general concept.
*   **The Power of Exploration Logs:** SCP-087 was one of the first SCPs to make extensive and effective use of exploration logs as a narrative device, a technique now common across the SCP Wiki.

## 9. Conclusion: The Fear of the Unseen and Unending

SCP-087, The Stairwell, is a testament to the power of suggestion and the primal fear of the dark, unknown depths. It doesn't need complex lore or overt monstrosity to be terrifying; its horror is woven from the oppressive atmosphere, the persistent, sorrowful cries, and the fleeting glimpse of a silent, watching face in the impenetrable blackness.

The exploration logs draw the reader down into the stairwell alongside the doomed D-Class, sharing their mounting dread with every descending step. SCP-087 reminds us that sometimes the most frightening anomalies are not those that charge and roar, but those that wait patiently in the dark, their nature and intentions forever beyond our understanding, offering only an endless descent into fear. It is a perfect encapsulation of the SCP Foundation's core premise: confronting horrors that defy explanation, one cautious, terrifying step at a time.
