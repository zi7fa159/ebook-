# SCP-002: The 'Living' Room - Organic Horror in a Meteorite Shell

## 1. Introduction: The Flesh That Furnishes

SCP-002, often referred to as "The 'Living' Room," is a Euclid-class anomalous object that presents a deeply unsettling fusion of the organic and the mundane. Outwardly appearing as a tumorous, fleshy growth with a valve-like hatch, its interior resembles a sparsely furnished apartment room. However, this "furniture" is made of bone, sinew, hair, and other biological materials, all seemingly grown from the room itself. More horrifyingly, SCP-002 has the ability to "add" living humans to its macabre decor if they make contact with its interior components, slowly and painfully transforming them into new pieces of biological furniture.

This article will explore the bizarre nature of SCP-002, its gruesome method of assimilation, its origins, and the chilling implications of a living, growing room that hungers for human components.

## 2. Physical Description: A Tumorous Growth with a Mundane Secret

SCP-002 is a stark contrast between its exterior and interior:

*   **Exterior Appearance:**
    *   A roughly spherical, tumorous mass of flesh, approximately 60 cubic meters (about 2000 cubic feet) in volume.
    *   It has a single iron valve hatch on one side, which provides access to the interior.
    *   The exterior surface is described as pulsating faintly and feeling warm to the touch.
    *   Its origin is linked to a meteorite impact site.
*   **Interior Appearance (SCP-002-1):**
    *   Resembles a standard, low-rent apartment room of modest size.
    *   Contains "furniture": a table, a chair, a television (non-functional), a rug, a window (displaying an external, unidentifiable landscape that changes), and a bed.
    *   **The Horrifying Truth of the Furniture:** Upon closer inspection, all items within the room are found to be constructed from human biological materials:
        *   Bone (for frames and hard structures).
        *   Woven or matted human hair (for upholstery, rugs).
        *   Sinew and ligaments (for bindings or flexible parts).
        *   Teeth, skin, and other tissues are incorporated into the designs.
    *   All "furniture" items are part of a single, interconnected biological mass that forms the room itself. They are grown, not assembled.
    *   DNA testing reveals that the biological components come from numerous, unrelated human individuals.

The juxtaposition of a seemingly normal (if poorly furnished) room with the grotesque reality of its construction is a core element of SCP-002's horror.

## 3. Anomalous Properties: Assimilation into Decor

SCP-002's primary anomalous property is its method of "redecorating":

*   **Assimilation Process:**
    *   If a living human makes physical contact with any of the interior components (the "furniture" or walls) of SCP-002-1, they will become adhered to it.
    *   The subject is then slowly and painfully absorbed into the biological mass of the room.
    *   Over a period of hours or days, the victim's body is broken down and reformed, their biological materials incorporated into existing furniture or used to "grow" new items of decor.
    *   The victim remains conscious for a significant portion of this agonizing process.
*   **"Adding to the Room":** The Foundation believes that each new victim contributes their biomass and unique genetic material to the room, potentially allowing it to "grow" new or more complex pieces of furniture over time.
*   **No Escape Once Contact is Made:** Once adhesion occurs, it is reportedly impossible to remove the individual without causing severe trauma that is usually fatal to the victim and may damage SCP-002 itself.
*   **No Other Known Aggression:** SCP-002 does not actively try to lure victims in or reach out from its hatch. The danger is entirely conditional upon entering and making contact with its interior.
*   **The "Window":** The view from the "window" inside SCP-002-1 changes, showing different, unidentifiable outdoor scenes. It's unknown if this is a view into another dimension, a psychic projection, or an illusion. It does not correspond to SCP-002's actual external surroundings.

## 4. Origin and Discovery: A Fallen Star's Grotesque Cargo

SCP-002's discovery is linked to a celestial event:
*   **Meteorite Impact:** It was discovered in a shallow impact crater in northern Portugal. The fleshy mass was found within the remnants of a meteorite that had recently struck the Earth.
*   **Initial State:** When found, it appeared as a throbbing, fleshy sphere. The valve hatch was sealed.
*   **First Contact:** The hatch opened upon being exposed to low-level radiation (from a Foundation Geiger counter during initial investigation), revealing the furnished interior. This first contact unfortunately led to the assimilation of one of the responding Foundation agents.

This origin suggests an extraterrestrial or extradimensional source for the anomaly, a biological entity or construct that arrived on Earth via meteorite.

## 5. Object Class: Euclid

SCP-002 is classified as **Euclid**.

*   **Why Euclid?**
    *   **Stationary but Hazardous:** While SCP-002 itself is immobile, its interior is extremely dangerous to any human who enters.
    *   **Unknown Full Capabilities/Intent:** The full extent of its growth potential, the nature of the "window" view, and its ultimate purpose (if any) are unknown.
    *   **Requires Specific Handling:** Entry and interaction protocols must be strictly followed to prevent accidental assimilation.
    *   **Potential for Growth (Theoretically):** If it were to assimilate enough biomass, could it grow larger or develop new, more dangerous properties? This unknown potential contributes to its Euclid status.
*   **Why Not Safe?** The extreme danger posed by its interior and the unknown aspects of its nature prevent a Safe classification.
*   **Why Not Keter?** It does not actively attempt to breach containment, spread, or lure victims. Its threat is passive but lethal upon contact.

## 6. Special Containment Procedures: Isolation and No Contact

Containment of SCP-002 focuses on preventing any human contact with its interior:
*   **Secure Containment Chamber:** SCP-002 is kept in a reinforced containment chamber at a designated Foundation Site.
*   **Power Supply (for internal systems, if any):** The article mentions it requires a suitable power supply to maintain what Foundation researchers believe to be its "recharging" or "hibernation" state. This might refer to internal lighting or life support for the biological components. If power is cut, it may enter an "active" state, though this is not fully detailed.
*   **Strict No-Contact Protocol:** No personnel are to enter SCP-002-1 (the interior room) under any circumstances.
*   **Remote Observation Only:** Any observation of the interior must be done via remote means (e.g., cameras on robotic drones, if entry is absolutely necessary for research, though this is extremely risky).
*   **Handling of the Hatch:** The valve hatch is to remain sealed unless opened under controlled experimental conditions (which are now likely forbidden or extremely restricted).
*   **Biohazard Precautions (Implied):** Given its organic nature, appropriate biohazard protocols would be in place for handling any materials related to SCP-002.

The primary goal is to ensure the "door" to this horrifying organic room remains permanently closed to human touch.

## 7. The Horror of SCP-002: Biological Uncanniness and Loss of Self

SCP-002 taps into several deep-seated fears:
*   **Body Horror:** The transformation of living humans into grotesque furniture made of their own flesh and bone is a potent form of body horror.
*   **Loss of Identity and Agency:** Victims are not just killed but are *assimilated*, their bodies and very being becoming part of an inanimate, albeit living, object. They lose all individuality and control.
*   **The Uncanny Valley (Organic):** The room appears familiar (an apartment) but is made of "wrong" materials, creating a deeply unsettling uncanny effect.
*   **Fear of Consumption/Absorption:** The idea of being slowly absorbed and incorporated into a larger, alien biological entity.
*   **The Unknown Origin and Purpose:** Its extraterrestrial origin and the complete lack of understanding of its purpose make it an alien, incomprehensible threat. Is it a trap? A bizarre life form's "home"? A biological terraforming device gone wrong?

## 8. SCP-002's Place in Foundation Lore

As one of the very early SCPs (number 002), it holds a significant place in the Foundation's history and lore:
*   **Early Example of Organic/Body Horror:** It helped establish the Foundation's willingness to explore visceral, biological horror.
*   **Setting a Tone:** Its concise, clinical description of a deeply bizarre and disturbing anomaly set a tone for many future SCP articles.
*   **The "Low Number" Significance:** Being SCP-002 gives it an almost foundational status, representing one of the first major anomalous threats the early Foundation had to grapple with.
*   **Simplicity and Effectiveness:** Despite its early number, its core concept remains highly effective and memorable.

## 9. Conclusion: The Room That Feeds on Its Occupants

SCP-002, "The 'Living' Room," is a chilling and unforgettable anomaly that blends the mundane with the grotesque. Its unassuming fleshy exterior hides an interior that is a perversion of domesticity, a space where human beings are not inhabitants but raw materials for its ever-growing, gruesome decor. The slow, conscious assimilation of its victims into its biological structure is a uniquely terrifying fate.

This early SCP entry serves as a potent reminder of the sheer alienness and potential for body horror that can be found within the anomalous world the Foundation seeks to control. It is a room no one would ever want to furnish, because the price of its redecoration is one's own flesh and bone, absorbed into the quietly pulsating, ever-hungry heart of a fallen star's monstrous cargo.
