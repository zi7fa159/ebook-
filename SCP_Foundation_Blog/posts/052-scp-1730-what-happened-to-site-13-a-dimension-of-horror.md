# SCP-1730: What Happened to Site-13? - A Dimension of Catastrophic Failure

## 1. Introduction: The Silent, Ominous Ruin

SCP-1730, titled "What Happened to Site-13?", is an immense, complex, and terrifying Apollyon-class (formerly Keter) anomaly. It is not an object or entity in the traditional sense, but rather the ruins of what appears to be an alternate-reality SCP Foundation facility – Site-13 – which has suffered a catastrophic series of containment breaches and unknown events, transforming it into a nightmarish, extradimensional wasteland teeming with horrors. Discovered in the Gulf of Mexico after a seismic event, SCP-1730 is a sprawling complex that periodically drains power from the Foundation's own Site-15 and emits a distress beacon.

The exploration of SCP-1730 is one of the most extensive and harrowing narratives on the SCP Wiki, detailing the efforts of multiple Mobile Task Forces to uncover the truth behind Site-13's demise and the fate of its inhabitants. This article will delve into the discovery, nature, horrifying contents, and the epic exploration of this monument to Foundation failure.

## 2. Discovery and Initial Assessment

*   **Appearance:** SCP-1730 manifested approximately 15 km off the coast of Point Barrow, Alaska (this location detail can vary in some interpretations, but a remote, difficult-to-access location is key) following an anomalous seismic event. It appeared as a massive, partially submerged complex of unknown architecture, clearly artificial but not matching any known terrestrial construction.
*   **Power Drain:** Soon after its appearance, it began to draw massive amounts of power from the nearby Foundation Site-15, causing blackouts and system failures.
*   **Distress Beacon:** SCP-1730 emits a repeating, low-frequency distress beacon, the source and meaning of which are initially unknown.
*   **Initial Classification:** Keter, due to its unknown nature, power drain, and potential threat. Later upgraded to Apollyon as the full extent of its danger and the hopelessness of "containing" it in any meaningful sense became clear.

The Foundation's immediate priority was to investigate this sudden, ominous structure.

## 3. The Nature of SCP-1730: A Corrupted Foundation Site from Another Reality

Exploration gradually revealed the horrifying truth about SCP-1730:
*   **Alternate Site-13:** It is the remains of "Site-13," a Foundation facility from an alternate dimension or timeline. This Site-13 was far larger, more ambitious, and seemingly far less ethical or cautious than the Foundation's baseline reality counterpart.
*   **Catastrophic Containment Failures:** Site-13 suffered a cascading series of catastrophic containment breaches involving numerous, extremely dangerous SCPs, many of which were unknown or far more powerful versions of SCPs familiar to our Foundation.
*   **"The Flesh That Hates" (SCP-610) Overrun:** Large sections of the site are overrun by SCP-610 or a similar flesh-warping contagion, transforming personnel and structures into grotesque biological masses.
*   **Hostile Reanimated Personnel:** Many former Site-13 staff were killed and reanimated as hostile entities, sometimes retaining fragments of their memories or anomalous abilities.
*   **Weaponized SCPs:** This alternate Foundation seemed to have a policy of actively weaponizing or attempting to control SCPs to a far greater extent, which likely contributed to its downfall.
*   **The "Decommissioning" of Site-13:** Evidence suggests that the O5 Council of this alternate reality, facing an uncontainable situation, attempted a desperate, site-wide "decommissioning" procedure. This involved flooding the site with a cocktail of lethal amnestics, neurotoxins, and reality-destabilizing agents, and then attempting to shunt the entire site into a pocket dimension or otherwise sever it from their reality. This procedure clearly failed catastrophically, resulting in the corrupted, dimensionally unstable ruin that is SCP-1730.
*   **The Engine / The Heart of Site-13:** Deep within the complex lies a massive, anomalous engine or power source that seems to be the source of the power drain and the distress beacon. It is also heavily implied to be the "leash" or control mechanism for a being of immense power – the true horror of Site-13.

## 4. The Horrors Within: A Bestiary of Nightmares

SCP-1730 is a charnel house, filled with countless threats:
*   **Escaped SCPs:** Numerous dangerous SCPs, both familiar and unfamiliar, roam the halls. Many are more aggressive or mutated versions of those known to our Foundation.
*   **SCP-610 Contamination:** Vast areas are consumed by infectious, flesh-altering material.
*   **Reanimated Site Director Emerson:** The former Site Director of this alternate Site-13, now a powerful, malevolent, and seemingly immortal entity who actively hunts exploration teams. He retains his intelligence and knowledge of Foundation protocols, making him an incredibly dangerous adversary.
*   **The "Shadow Men" / Displaced Personnel:** Spectral or phase-shifted entities, possibly the remnants of personnel caught in the decommissioning.
*   **Malfunctioning Security Systems:** Automated defenses, turrets, and containment protocols that are still active but corrupted, often targeting anything that moves.
*   **Environmental Hazards:** Structural collapse, toxic environments, areas of distorted reality, radiation zones.
*   **The Leashed Entity (The True SCP-1730):** The ultimate horror hinted at throughout the exploration logs. A colossal, immensely powerful being (possibly a deific entity, a "child" of the Scarlet King, or something even worse) that was being "contained" or perhaps *utilized* by Site-13's former administration. The distress beacon is revealed to be *its* cry, and the power drain is it attempting to break free or maintain its failing containment/life support. Its full release would be an undeniable Apollyon-level event. The entity is often referred to as "The Gate Guardian's Brother" or similar epithets, implying a connection to other cosmic-scale SCPs.

## 5. The Exploration Logs: An Epic of Foundation Courage and Sacrifice

The SCP-1730 article is famous for its extensive and detailed exploration logs, which read like a found-footage horror epic.
*   **Multiple MTF Deployments:** The Foundation deploys several of its most elite MTFs, including:
    *   **MTF Tau-5 ("Samsara"):** Immortal cyborg clones, perfect for high-risk exploration.
    *   **MTF Apollo-3 ("Game Wardens"):** Specialized in tracking and containing large-scale anomalous entities.
    *   **MTF Epsilon-11 ("Nine-Tailed Fox"):** Internal security and recontainment specialists.
    *   **MTF Omega-12 ("Achilles' Heels"):** Composed of reality-bending Foundation loyalists.
*   **Heavy Casualties:** Despite their skills and equipment, the MTFs suffer horrific casualties as they delve deeper into Site-13.
*   **Piecing Together the Past:** Through recovered documents, computer logs, and environmental storytelling, the exploration teams slowly uncover the history of this alternate Site-13 and the nature of its downfall.
*   **Moral Dilemmas:** The teams face difficult choices, such as whether to rescue potential survivors (who are often hostile or dangerously altered) or prioritize their mission objectives.
*   **The Final Confrontation:** The logs culminate in attempts to reach the "Engine" and understand or neutralize the leashed entity, often with pyrrhic victories or tragic outcomes.

These logs are praised for their tension, world-building, character development (within the MTFs), and unflinching depiction of horror.

## 6. Key Figures and Discoveries

*   **Site Director Emerson (Alternate):** A central antagonist, a figure of immense power and cruelty, representing the hubris and failure of this alternate Foundation.
*   **Dr. Heikkilä:** A researcher from the prime Foundation who becomes obsessed with understanding SCP-1730, often pushing for further exploration despite the risks.
*   **The "Bobble the Clown" Incident:** A particularly disturbing log entry detailing an encounter with a seemingly harmless clown anomaly that turns horrifically violent, showcasing the unpredictable dangers within.
*   **The Nature of the Distress Beacon:** Revealed not to be a call for help from Site-13 personnel, but the "heartbeat" or cry of the massive entity contained/utilized at its core.
*   **The "Gods" of Site-13:** Evidence suggests this alternate Foundation was attempting to control or even create god-like beings.

## 7. Object Class: Apollyon

SCP-1730 is ultimately classified as **Apollyon**.
*   **Uncontainable Entity:** The primary entity within SCP-1730 is deemed uncontainable by current Foundation means. If it were to fully awaken or break free, it would likely cause an XK-Class scenario or worse.
*   **Irreparable Damage:** The Site-13 complex itself is a permanent, dangerous extradimensional fixture that cannot be easily removed or neutralized.
*   **Ongoing Threat:** The power drain and the potential for further entities or phenomena to emerge from SCP-1730 pose a continuous threat.
*   **Foundation's Response:** The Foundation's current "containment" is essentially to monitor SCP-1730, prevent access by unauthorized parties, manage the power drain as best as possible, and prepare for the worst-case scenario of the central entity's awakening. Some proposals involve attempting to sever its connection to our reality or even destroying our own Site-15 to cut off its power source, though these are desperate measures.

## 8. Themes and Significance

SCP-1730 is a landmark SCP for several reasons:
*   **Epic Scale Horror:** It's one of the largest and most detailed "dungeon crawl" style narratives on the site, creating a vast and terrifying environment.
*   **The Dark Mirror:** It serves as a dark reflection of the Foundation, showing what could happen if their ambition outpaced their caution or if their ethics were severely compromised. It's a cautionary tale about hubris.
*   **The Cost of Containment:** It highlights the immense sacrifices Foundation personnel make and the horrific consequences of catastrophic failure.
*   **Interdimensional/Alternate Reality Lore:** It significantly expands the concept of alternate Foundation realities and the potential for threats to cross over.
*   **Cosmic Horror:** The entity at the heart of Site-13 taps into Lovecraftian themes of ancient, incomprehensible powers beyond human control.
*   **Collaboration and Canon Building:** SCP-1730 incorporates and references numerous other SCPs and lore elements, creating a rich, interconnected narrative.

## 9. Conclusion: The Price of Playing God

SCP-1730, "What Happened to Site-13?", is more than just a dangerous location; it is a sprawling monument to an alternate Foundation's catastrophic ambition and ultimate downfall. Its exploration logs provide a harrowing journey into a dimension of unimaginable horror, where familiar SCP concepts are twisted into new nightmares and where the very protectors of normalcy became architects of their own destruction.

The silent, power-draining ruin serves as a constant, ominous warning to our Foundation: a testament to the thin line between control and chaos, between containment and catastrophe. The true horror of SCP-1730 is not just the monsters within its corrupted halls, but the chilling realization that it represents a path their own organization could have taken, and perhaps, in some dark future, still might. The distress beacon from its depths is a lament for a Foundation that fell, and a terrifying premonition of what could happen if the entities they seek to control ever truly break their chains.
