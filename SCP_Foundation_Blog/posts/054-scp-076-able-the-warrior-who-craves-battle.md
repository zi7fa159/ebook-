# SCP-076: Able - The Resurrecting Warrior Who Craves Eternal Battle

## 1. Introduction: The Unkillable Combatant

SCP-076, consisting of two components – SCP-076-1 (The Cube) and SCP-076-2 ("Able") – is a Keter-class anomaly centered around an incredibly skilled and violent humanoid entity who seemingly exists only for combat. Able is a figure of ancient myth and legend, a warrior of superhuman prowess who, upon death, eventually re-manifests from his containment cube, ready to unleash destruction anew. His insatiable lust for battle and his formidable abilities make him one of the Foundation's most dangerous and difficult-to-manage humanoid SCPs.

This article will explore the dual nature of SCP-076, Able's characteristics, his destructive rampages, the controversial history of Mobile Task Force Omega-7 ("Pandora's Box") which attempted to utilize him, and his significance as an icon of warrior archetypes within the SCP universe.

## 2. SCP-076-1: The Cube of Resurrection

The first component, SCP-076-1, is the containment and re-manifestation point for Able:
*   **Description:** A large cube, 3 meters on each side, made of black, speckled metamorphic stone. The exterior is carved with intricate patterns and symbols that do not correspond to any known civilization.
*   **Temperature:** Maintains an internal temperature of -180 degrees Celsius (-292 degrees Fahrenheit), cooled by an unknown internal mechanism.
*   **The Door:** One side of the cube features a locked door, surrounded by smaller locks arranged in a circular pattern. This door is the only known entrance.
*   **The Coffin:** Inside SCP-076-1 is a stone coffin, held in place by chains. This coffin is the direct resting place of SCP-076-2 when he is "dead" or inactive.
*   **Indestructibility (Implied):** SCP-076-1 is highly resistant to damage.

SCP-076-1 serves as both Able's tomb and his womb, the nexus from which he returns to life.

## 3. SCP-076-2: "Able" - The Warrior Incarnate

SCP-076-2, known colloquially as "Able," is the humanoid entity that emerges from SCP-076-1:
*   **Appearance:** A lean but heavily muscled humanoid male, appearing to be in his late twenties. Skin tone is Semitic or Middle Eastern, with black hair and grey eyes (though details can vary slightly by interpretation). Covered in numerous tattoos depicting occult and mythological scenes, often of a violent nature.
*   **Personality:** When active, Able is characterized by:
    *   **Extreme Aggression and Bloodlust:** His primary motivation is to seek out and engage in combat with skilled opponents. He shows disdain for weak or "unworthy" foes.
    *   **Arrogance and Contempt:** Views most modern humans and their technology with contempt.
    *   **Stoicism (when not in combat):** Can be calm, quiet, and uncommunicative when not actively seeking battle.
    *   **Proficiency in Ancient Languages:** Often speaks ancient Sumerian or other dead languages.
*   **Superhuman Abilities:**
    *   **Strength and Speed:** Possesses superhuman strength, speed, agility, and reflexes far exceeding human limits.
    *   **Durability and Pain Resistance:** Highly resistant to physical trauma and seems to feel little pain.
    *   **Weapon Manifestation:** Able can materialize bladed weapons (swords, knives, axes, etc.) seemingly out of nowhere, often from small dimensional rifts or "dark spots." These weapons are typically black, non-reflective, and incredibly sharp.
    *   **Master Combatant:** An exceptionally skilled warrior, proficient in virtually all forms of armed and unarmed combat, particularly with bladed weapons. His combat style is brutal and efficient.
*   **The "Death" and Reanimation Cycle:**
    *   When SCP-076-2 is "killed" (i.e., sustains sufficient damage to cease biological function), his remains will rapidly decompose until only dust is left.
    *   SCP-076-1 (The Cube) will then seal itself, and over a period ranging from six (6) hours to twenty-five (25) years, Able will reform within the coffin inside the cube, eventually emerging fully restored and ready for combat once more.

Able is the archetypal "unstoppable warrior," a force of nature dedicated to the art of killing.

## 4. Behavior When Active: A Rampage of Skill

When Able emerges from SCP-076-1, his behavior follows a predictable pattern:
1.  **Emergence:** He will attempt to exit SCP-076-1. If the door is sealed, he will enter a dormant state. If it can be opened (or if he forces it), he will emerge.
2.  **Assessment:** He will survey his surroundings, often ignoring inanimate objects or personnel he deems non-threatening or unworthy.
3.  **Seeking Worthy Opponents:** His primary goal is to find and engage what he considers "worthy" opponents – skilled combatants, powerful entities, or significant challenges. He often shows frustration or boredom if faced only with standard security forces.
4.  **Combat:** Engages targets with extreme violence and skill, utilizing his manifested weapons. He fights until he is "killed" or all perceived threats are neutralized.
5.  **Psychological State:** During combat, he often appears to be in a state of focused rage or battle ecstasy.
6.  **No Interest in Escape (Usually):** Unlike many SCPs, Able's primary goal upon breaching is not usually to escape the facility, but to find conflict within it. However, if no suitable opponents are present, he may attempt to leave to find them elsewhere.

## 5. Object Class: Keter

SCP-076 is classified as **Keter**.
*   **Why Keter?**
    *   **Extreme Hostility and Destructive Potential:** When active, Able is incredibly dangerous, capable of killing dozens of highly trained personnel in minutes.
    *   **Difficult to "Kill" Permanently:** His resurrection ability means any "termination" is temporary.
    *   **Containment Breaches:** He frequently attempts to breach containment upon reanimation if not immediately engaged or if his containment protocols fail.
    *   **Unpredictable Reanimation Cycle:** The time it takes for him to reanimate can vary wildly, making long-term planning difficult.
    *   **Psychological Impact:** His presence and actions are a significant source of fear and stress for Foundation personnel.

Containing Able is a constant state of readiness for his inevitable return.

## 6. Special Containment Procedures: Managing the Inevitable Emergence

Containment of SCP-076 focuses on managing Able's reanimation cycle and mitigating the damage during his active phases:
*   **Primary Containment Area (for SCP-076-1):** SCP-076-1 is kept in a heavily secured room, often at the bottom of a deep shaft or within multiple layers of reinforced material, sometimes submerged in water to slow Able's emergence.
*   **Armed Response Teams:** Multiple heavily armed Mobile Task Forces (specifically those trained for Keter-class humanoid combat) must be on standby, ready to engage SCP-076-2 immediately upon his emergence.
*   **"Killing Corridor" / Gauntlet:** The area around SCP-076-1 is often designed as a "killing corridor" with multiple defensive emplacements, traps, and chokepoints to slow Able down and inflict maximum damage.
*   **Termination as Containment:** The primary method of "containing" an active SCP-076-2 is to "kill" him as quickly as possible, forcing him back into his reanimation cycle within SCP-076-1.
*   **No Interaction When Dormant (Unless Authorized):** Personnel are generally forbidden from opening SCP-076-1 or interacting with the dormant Able without high-level authorization.
*   **Psychological Screening for Personnel:** Personnel assigned to SCP-076 must be psychologically screened for combat readiness and resilience to extreme violence.

## 7. Mobile Task Force Omega-7 ("Pandora's Box"): The Controversial Experiment

One of the most significant and controversial parts of SCP-076's lore is its involvement with Mobile Task Force Omega-7 ("Pandora's Box").
*   **Premise:** An experimental MTF created by the Foundation with the radical idea of utilizing cooperative (or coercible) humanoid SCPs as field operatives.
*   **Able as Primary Asset:** SCP-076-2 ("Able") was the primary asset and "star" of Omega-7. He was equipped with an explosive collar to ensure compliance (though its effectiveness was debatable) and deployed on missions against other dangerous SCPs or hostile GoIs.
*   **Initial Successes:** Omega-7, with Able at its spearhead, had several successful missions, showcasing the potential of weaponizing SCPs.
*   **Inherent Instability:** However, Able's violent nature, unpredictability, and disdain for authority made him an incredibly unstable asset. He frequently caused collateral damage, disobeyed orders, and attacked his own teammates.
*   **The Catastrophic Downfall:** MTF Omega-7 was eventually decommissioned after a catastrophic incident where Able went on a rampage, killing most of the task force (including its human commander) and causing a major site breach. This event served as a grim lesson about the dangers of trying to control entities like Able.
*   **Legacy:** The legacy of Omega-7 is one of hubris and failure, often cited as a reason why the Foundation is generally hesitant to weaponize SCPs. It also led to the creation of stricter protocols for humanoid SCPs and the formation of MTF Alpha-9 ("Last Hope"), a more cautious successor attempting a similar concept.

## 8. Origins and Mythology: A Warrior from a Lost Age

Able's origins are tied to ancient mythology, though specifics vary:
*   **Sumerian/Mesopotamian Links:** His ability to speak ancient Sumerian and the imagery of his tattoos suggest a connection to ancient Mesopotamian cultures or myths.
*   **Biblical Able?** His name "Able" (or "Abel" in some interpretations) has led to speculation that he is the biblical Abel, son of Adam and Eve, cursed or transformed into an eternal warrior after being murdered by Cain. This is a popular but not universally accepted theory.
*   **Daevite Connection:** In some canons, he is linked to the Daevites, an ancient, cruel, and magically powerful civilization that warred with Sarkicism and the ancient Mekhanites. Able might have been a Daevite champion or a weapon created by them.
*   **A Fallen Hero or Cursed Warrior:** A recurring theme is that he was once a great hero or warrior who, through some tragedy, curse, or dark pact, became the immortal engine of slaughter he is today.

His true history is lost to time, existing only in fragments of myth and the carvings on his containment cube.

## 9. Conclusion: The Eternal Berserker

SCP-076, "Able," is the embodiment of the eternal warrior, a being of immense power and skill trapped in an endless cycle of death, rebirth, and battle. He is a force of primal combat energy, a Keter-class threat whose containment is less about permanent imprisonment and more about managing his violent outbursts and inevitably "killing" him again and again.

His story, particularly his involvement with MTF Omega-7, serves as a crucial cautionary tale within the SCP Foundation mythos about the allure and ultimate folly of attempting to weaponize the anomalous. Able is not a tool to be wielded; he is a storm to be weathered. His disdain for weakness, his superhuman prowess, and his insatiable hunger for worthy conflict make him a terrifying and iconic figure, a dark legend striding out of a forgotten age to test the limits of the Foundation's strength and resolve, time and time again.
