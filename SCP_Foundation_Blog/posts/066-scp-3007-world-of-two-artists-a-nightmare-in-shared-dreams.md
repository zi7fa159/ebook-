# SCP-3007: World of Two Artists - A Nightmare Shared Through Dreams

## 1. Introduction: The Intrusive Thoughts of a Dying God

SCP-3007, titled "World of Two Artists," is a Keter-class cognitohazardous phenomenon centered around an extradimensional, possibly deceased or dying, entity whose thoughts and memories are capable of intruding into the dreams of sentient beings across multiple realities, including our own. These dream intrusions, designated SCP-3007-1, are initially disorienting but become progressively more coherent and terrifying, often depicting scenes of body horror, psychological torment, and apocalyptic imagery related to the entity's experiences or nature. Prolonged exposure can lead to severe psychological trauma, madness, and even physical manifestations of the dream content in reality.

This article explores the complex nature of SCP-3007, the characteristics of the dream intrusions, the entity behind them (SCP-3007-3), and the Foundation's desperate efforts to understand and mitigate a threat that invades the sanctity of the human mind.

## 2. The Phenomenon: SCP-3007-1 Dream Intrusions

The primary manifestation of SCP-3007 is through anomalous dream sequences:
*   **Initial Exposure (The "Two Artists" Paintings):** The trigger for SCP-3007 infection is often linked to viewing specific artworks created by two (fictional) artists, "Sachiko Yagami" and "Walter Jeffries," who were themselves early victims or conduits for the phenomenon. These artworks act as cognitohazardous gateways.
*   **Progressive Dream Coherence:**
    *   **Early Stage:** Dreams are fragmented, confusing, and filled with abstract, unsettling imagery (e.g., impossible colors, distorted sounds, feelings of dread).
    *   **Intermediate Stage:** Dreams become more narrative and coherent, often featuring recurring motifs like decaying cityscapes, grotesque biological entities, themes of imprisonment or torture, and a pervasive sense of immense suffering or loss. The "two artists" themselves may appear as figures within these dreams.
    *   **Late Stage:** Dreams become overwhelmingly vivid, intrusive, and often directly reflect the memories, thoughts, or sensory experiences of SCP-3007-3. This can include experiencing the entity's pain, its perception of its own decaying body, or witnessing apocalyptic events from its perspective.
*   **Shared Dreamscape:** Individuals infected by SCP-3007 may begin to experience shared elements in their dreams, suggesting they are tapping into a common, anomalous "dreamscape" influenced by SCP-3007-3.
*   **Psychological Impact:**
    *   Severe nightmares, sleep deprivation.
    *   Anxiety, paranoia, depression.
    *   Development of phobias related to dream content.
    *   Dissociation, derealization, and eventual psychosis.
*   **Physical Manifestations (Rare but Severe):** In extreme cases, elements from the SCP-3007-1 dreamscape can begin to manifest physically in reality around the affected individual (e.g., anomalous growths, environmental changes mirroring dream imagery, appearance of entities from the dreams). This indicates a breakdown of the barrier between dream and reality.

## 3. SCP-3007-2: The Artworks as Conduits

The artworks created by Sachiko Yagami and Walter Jeffries serve as the initial infection vectors:
*   **Cognitohazardous Nature:** Viewing these specific paintings (or high-quality reproductions) can trigger the onset of SCP-3007-1 dream intrusions.
*   **Thematic Content:** The paintings themselves often depict surreal, disturbing imagery that foreshadows or reflects the content of the anomalous dreams. They are windows into the "World of Two Artists," which is, in turn, a window into SCP-3007-3's mind.
*   **Foundation Containment:** The Foundation actively seeks out and contains all known instances of SCP-3007-2 artworks, restricting visual access and storing them in secure art archives. Digital copies are also suppressed.

## 4. SCP-3007-3: The Extradimensional Entity ("The Dying God")

The ultimate source of the SCP-3007 phenomenon is believed to be a powerful, ancient, extradimensional entity, often referred to by researchers (and in fan interpretations) as "The Dying God" or a similar epithet.
*   **Nature and State:**
    *   Likely a being of immense scale and power, existing in a reality with different physical laws.
    *   Currently in a state of decay, death, or extreme suffering. It may be imprisoned, mortally wounded, or simply at the end of its natural ( unimaginably long) lifespan.
    *   Its "thoughts," "memories," or "death throes" are somehow bleeding across dimensional barriers and manifesting as SCP-3007-1 dreams in susceptible sentient minds.
*   **No Direct Malice (Potentially):** The entity is not necessarily intentionally attacking humanity. The dream intrusions might be an involuntary side effect of its condition, like the pained cries of a dying creature echoing across realities. However, its suffering and the nature of its thoughts are inherently toxic and damaging to human psyches.
*   **Unknown Origin and Purpose:** Its original purpose, history, and the cause of its current state are unknown and likely incomprehensible.
*   **Location:** Resides in a distant, alien dimension, the characteristics of which are glimpsed through the late-stage SCP-3007-1 dreams (often depicted as a decaying, alien cityscape or a cosmic void).

SCP-3007-3 represents a cosmic-scale horror, a being whose very process of dying is a cognitohazardous threat to other realities.

## 5. Object Class: Keter

SCP-3007 is classified as **Keter**.
*   **Why Keter?**
    *   **Global Threat Potential:** If the trigger artworks (SCP-3007-2) were widely disseminated or if new, more potent infection vectors emerged, SCP-3007 could cause a global pandemic of psychological breakdown and reality distortion.
    *   **Difficult to Contain the Source:** The Foundation cannot directly access or contain SCP-3007-3, as it exists in another dimension. Containment focuses on mitigating its effects in our reality.
    *   **Irreversible Psychological Damage:** Prolonged exposure to SCP-3007-1 dreams often leads to permanent psychological damage or madness.
    *   **Potential for Physical Manifestation:** The risk of dream content physically manifesting poses a direct threat to reality.
    *   **Ongoing and Unpredictable:** The "broadcast" from SCP-3007-3 is ongoing, and its intensity or nature could change unpredictably.

Its Keter status reflects the pervasive, insidious, and ultimately uncontrollable nature of a threat that originates beyond the Foundation's direct reach.

## 6. Special Containment Procedures: Information Control and Mental Quarantine

Containment of SCP-3007 is primarily focused on information control, identifying and isolating infected individuals, and researching countermeasures:
*   **Containment of SCP-3007-2:** All known instances of trigger artworks are secured in high-security art containment units with restricted visual access. Digital copies are purged from public networks.
*   **Monitoring for New Vectors:** The Foundation monitors artistic communities, online platforms, and psychiatric institutions for signs of new SCP-3007-2 instances or outbreaks of SCP-3007-1 symptoms.
*   **Quarantine of Infected Individuals:** Individuals confirmed to be experiencing SCP-3007-1 dreams (especially in later stages) are to be detained, quarantined, and monitored in psych-eval facilities.
*   **Amnestic Therapy (Limited Effectiveness):** Standard amnestics may alleviate early-stage symptoms or erase memory of trigger artworks but are often ineffective against deeply ingrained late-stage dream intrusions.
*   **Memetic Resistance Training / Counter-Agents:** Research is ongoing into developing memetic resistance techniques or counter-memetic agents that could shield individuals from SCP-3007-1 or disrupt the dream intrusions.
*   **Project Communion (Fictional/Conceptual):** Some tales or interpretations might involve highly classified projects attempting to communicate with or understand SCP-3007-3, or even to "euthanize" it to stop its suffering and the resulting dream pandemic – all with extreme risk.
*   **No Direct Intervention on SCP-3007-3:** Currently, the Foundation has no known means to directly affect SCP-3007-3 in its home dimension.

## 7. The "Two Artists" Narrative: Sachiko Yagami and Walter Jeffries

The story of the two artists is central to understanding the initial discovery and spread of SCP-3007:
*   **Early Conduits:** They were likely among the first humans to become "infected" by SCP-3007-3's mental emanations, perhaps due to unique psychic sensitivity or accidental exposure to a dimensional weak point.
*   **Art as Expression of the Anomaly:** Their artworks became unconscious or semi-conscious expressions of the SCP-3007-1 dreamscape, inadvertently turning into cognitohazardous infection vectors.
*   **Shared Fate:** The narrative often implies they shared a deep connection, possibly experiencing the same dream world, and met tragic ends as the phenomenon consumed them. Their story is one of artistic inspiration twisted into horrifying paranormal contagion.

They are both victims and unintentional propagators of the anomaly.

## 8. Themes and Horror Elements

SCP-3007 is a potent example of several horror subgenres:
*   **Cosmic Horror:** The source is an ancient, dying god-like being from another reality, whose suffering is a planetary threat.
*   **Psychological Horror:** The primary impact is on the minds of its victims, through terrifying, inescapable dreams that erode sanity.
*   **Body Horror:** Late-stage dreams and physical manifestations often involve grotesque biological transformations and decay.
*   **Surrealism:** The dreamscapes are often bizarre, illogical, and nightmarish in a surreal, artistic way.
*   **Fear of Contagion (Mental):** The idea of a "mind virus" spreading through dreams or art is deeply unsettling.
*   **Loss of Control over One's Mind:** Dreams are a private space; SCP-3007 violates this sanctuary.

## 9. Conclusion: The Nightmare Echoes of a Dying Universe

SCP-3007, "World of Two Artists," is a complex and deeply disturbing Keter-class anomaly that explores the terrifying possibility of trans-dimensional mental contamination. It posits a dying god whose final, agonizing thoughts become a psychic plague upon other realities, transmitted through art and dreams, leading to madness and the potential collapse of reality itself.

The Foundation's struggle to contain SCP-3007 is a desperate battle against an intangible, pervasive threat whose source lies beyond their reach. It is a fight to preserve not just physical safety, but the very sanity of human consciousness from the nightmare echoes of a distant, decaying cosmos. The art of Sachiko Yagami and Walter Jeffries serves as a beautiful, terrible warning: some thoughts are too vast and too alien for the human mind to bear, and some dreams are gateways to apocalypses we can only pray remain asleep.
