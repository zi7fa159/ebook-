# SCP-4999: Someone To Watch Over Us - A Comfort in the Final, Lonely Moments

## 1. Introduction: A Gentle Anomaly at Life's End

In the vast and often terrifying catalog of the SCP Foundation, where anomalies frequently bring destruction, madness, or existential dread, SCP-4999 stands as a poignant and deeply humane exception. Designated "Someone To Watch Over Us," this Safe-class humanoid entity manifests exclusively to individuals who are on the verge of death, critically ill or injured, and entirely alone – with no friends, family, or companions present in their final moments. SCP-4999 offers silent comfort, a simple cigarette, and a reassuring presence, ensuring that no one truly has to die alone if they meet its specific criteria.

This article explores the compassionate nature of SCP-4999, its appearance, its unique manifestation conditions, and its profound emotional resonance as a symbol of empathy in a universe often characterized by cold, clinical detachment.

## 2. Physical Description: The Unassuming Comforter

SCP-4999 appears as an ordinary human male, typically middle-aged:
*   **Appearance:** A non-descript male, usually described as being between 30 and 50 years of age. His specific facial features, hair color, and build can vary slightly between manifestations, but he is always presented in a simple, dark suit.
*   **Attire:** Consistently wears a dark, unremarkable suit, often slightly rumpled or worn, appropriate for a formal but not ostentatious occasion.
*   **Demeanor:** Calm, quiet, and gentle. He exudes an aura of peace and empathy.
*   **No Identifying Features:** He carries no identification, and his appearance does not match any known individual in Foundation databases or public records.

His ordinary appearance is key to his unobtrusive, comforting presence. He is not there to frighten or awe, but simply to be there.

## 3. Anomalous Properties and Behavior: A Vigil for the Lonely Dying

SCP-4999's anomalous behavior is highly specific and consistent:

*   **Manifestation Conditions:** SCP-4999 will only manifest under a precise set of circumstances:
    1.  **The Subject is Human.**
    2.  **The Subject is Terminally Ill, Critically Injured, or Otherwise Dying:** Death must be imminent, typically within minutes or a few hours.
    3.  **The Subject is Alone:** This is crucial. The individual must be physically alone, with no other conscious human beings present in their immediate vicinity (e.g., in the same room or within close perceptual range). This includes family, friends, medical staff, or any other companions.
    4.  **The Subject is Conscious (or at least semi-conscious):** They must be capable of perceiving SCP-4999's presence, even if only minimally.
*   **Method of Appearance:** SCP-4999 appears silently and unobtrusively near the dying individual, often sitting on the edge of their bed or a nearby chair. There is no dramatic entrance; he simply *is there*.
*   **The Offer of a Cigarette:**
    *   Upon appearing, SCP-4999 will typically offer the dying individual a cigarette from a pack he produces.
    *   If the individual accepts (verbally or non-verbally), SCP-4999 will place a cigarette between their lips, light both their cigarette and one for himself, and smoke silently with them.
    *   If the individual declines or is unable to accept (e.g., due to medical equipment, being non-responsive), SCP-4999 will typically smoke his own cigarette alone, still maintaining his vigil.
*   **Silent Comfort and Presence:**
    *   SCP-4999 does not speak or attempt to engage in conversation beyond the initial offer of a cigarette (which itself is often non-verbal).
    *   His primary function is to provide a comforting, reassuring presence. He may offer simple physical comfort if appropriate and accepted, such as holding the individual's hand or placing a hand on their shoulder.
    *   He remains with the individual until they pass away.
*   **Demanifestation:** Once the individual has died, SCP-4999 will extinguish his cigarette (and the subject's, if applicable), bow his head or make a similar gesture of respect, and then demanifest, usually by simply ceasing to be visible or by walking out of view and disappearing. He leaves no trace of his presence, including the cigarette butts.
*   **No Other Interactions:** SCP-4999 does not interact with medical equipment, attempt to provide medical aid, or interfere with the dying process in any way. He does not communicate with Foundation personnel or any other individuals who might subsequently enter the room. He is solely there for the dying person.

His actions are entirely focused on alleviating the loneliness of death.

## 4. Object Class: Safe

SCP-4999 is classified as **Safe**.

*   **Why Safe?**
    *   **Non-Hostile:** SCP-4999 exhibits no aggressive or harmful behavior whatsoever. Its actions are entirely benevolent.
    *   **Predictable Behavior:** Its manifestation conditions and behavior are consistent and well-understood.
    *   **No Containment Required (in the traditional sense):** The Foundation cannot "contain" SCP-4999 as it manifests globally and unpredictably based on its criteria. Containment efforts focus on observation, data collection, and ensuring its activities do not breach The Veil.
    *   **No Threat to Normalcy (If Undetected):** As long as its manifestations are not widely observed or understood by the public, it does not pose a significant threat to The Veil. The people it visits are, by definition, about to die and thus cannot spread knowledge of it.

Its Safe classification reflects its harmless and even beneficial nature, despite being an uncontained anomaly.

## 5. Special Containment Procedures: Observation and Discretion

Given SCP-4999's nature, containment is primarily about information management and observation:
*   **Monitoring and Data Collection:** The Foundation monitors global obituaries, hospital records, and other sources for reports of individuals dying alone under circumstances that match SCP-4999's manifestation criteria.
*   **Covert Observation (When Possible):** If a potential manifestation event is identified in advance (e.g., a terminally ill patient in a hospital who is consistently alone), covert surveillance (hidden cameras) may be set up to record SCP-4999's appearance. This is how most data on it has been gathered.
*   **No Interference:** Foundation personnel are strictly forbidden from interfering with SCP-4999 during a manifestation event. Attempting to interact with, detain, or obstruct SCP-4999 could disrupt its compassionate purpose and potentially provoke an unknown reaction from an otherwise benign entity.
*   **Information Control:** Information regarding SCP-4999 is classified. Any evidence of its appearance (e.g., security footage from a hospital where it manifested) is to be confiscated, and witnesses (e.g., medical staff who might have briefly seen it before it demanifested) are to be administered amnestics if necessary.
*   **Research Focus:** Research is focused on understanding its origins, the full extent of its manifestation criteria, and whether it possesses any other anomalous properties.

The Foundation's approach is one of respectful, distant observation, recognizing the unique and positive nature of this anomaly.

## 6. The Significance of SCP-4999: An Anomaly of Empathy

SCP-4999 is significant for several reasons within the SCP lore:
*   **A Rare Benign Anomaly:** In a universe filled with horrors, SCP-4999 is a powerful example of an anomaly that is not only harmless but actively compassionate and beneficial (from a human emotional perspective).
*   **Challenging Foundation Paradigms:** It subtly challenges the Foundation's default stance that all anomalies are inherently dangerous or must be strictly contained. SCP-4999 is largely left to its own devices because its "work" is seen as humane.
*   **Thematic Depth:** It explores profound themes of loneliness, death, compassion, and the universal human desire for comfort in one's final moments.
*   **Emotional Resonance:** SCP-4999 is one of the most emotionally resonant SCPs for many readers, evoking feelings of sadness, comfort, and a sense of bittersweet beauty.
*   **Mystery of Origin and Intent:** Who or what is SCP-4999? Is it an angel, a psychopomp, a collective unconscious manifestation of human empathy, or something else entirely? Its unknown origin and selfless purpose make it all the more compelling.
*   **The Cigarette Symbolism:** The shared cigarette is a simple, almost anachronistic gesture of companionship and shared humanity, a small ritual of connection at the edge of oblivion. It's a moment of quiet solidarity.

## 7. Theories and Interpretations

*   **Psychopomp/Angel of Death (Benevolent):** A common interpretation is that SCP-4999 is a type of psychopomp (a guide of souls to the afterlife) or a benevolent angel of death, specifically tasked with comforting the utterly alone.
*   **Manifestation of Collective Empathy:** Perhaps it is a tulpa or psychic construct created unconsciously by the collective human desire for no one to die alone.
*   **An Ancient, Unknown Entity:** It could be an ancient being with its own inscrutable reasons for performing this specific act of kindness.
*   **No Grand Purpose, Just Is:** It might simply be an anomaly that *is*, its behavior a fixed property without a deeper cosmic plan.

The beauty of SCP-4999 often lies in not having a definitive explanation for its existence.

## 8. Conclusion: A Final, Gentle Presence

SCP-4999, "Someone To Watch Over Us," is a remarkable and poignant anomaly that offers a rare glimmer of warmth and compassion in the often cold and terrifying SCP Foundation universe. It is a silent testament to the idea that even in a world filled with inexplicable horrors, there can also exist inexplicable kindness. Its simple act of being present for the lonely dying, sharing a smoke in the face of oblivion, provides a profound counterpoint to the Foundation's usual fare of monsters and existential threats.

This entity, in its dark suit and with its offer of a final cigarette, embodies a fundamental human need: the desire not to be alone at the very end. SCP-4999 ensures that for a select, unfortunate few who have no one else, that final journey is not made in solitude. It is a Safe-class anomaly not because it is locked away, but because its very nature is to provide safety and comfort, one last quiet moment of shared existence before the darkness claims its due.
