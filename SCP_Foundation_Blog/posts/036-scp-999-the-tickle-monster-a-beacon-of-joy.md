# SCP-999: The Tickle Monster - A Beacon of Joy in a Dark Universe

## 1. Introduction: An Anomaly of Pure Happiness

In the grim and often terrifying universe of the SCP Foundation, where existential threats and reality-bending horrors are the norm, SCP-999, affectionately known as "The Tickle Monster," stands out as a remarkable and beloved anomaly. This Safe-class SCP is a gelatinous, playful creature whose primary anomalous property is its ability to induce intense euphoria and happiness in anyone it interacts with. In a world saturated with dread, SCP-999 is a rare and precious beacon of pure, unadulterated joy.

This article will explore the delightful nature of SCP-999, its appearance, its unique abilities, its interactions with other SCPs (notably SCP-682), and its symbolic importance as a source of hope and positivity within the Foundation's dark narrative.

## 2. Physical Description: An Amorphous Orange Blob

SCP-999's appearance is as endearing as its personality:

*   **Composition:** A large, amorphous, gelatinous mass of translucent orange slime. Its consistency is described as being similar to peanut butter.
*   **Size and Weight:** Weighs approximately 54 kg (120 lbs) and has a volume of about 2 cubic meters, though its shape is highly mutable.
*   **Locomotion:** It moves by contracting and expanding its pseudopods, similar to an amoeba. It can slide under doors and through small openings.
*   **Sensory Organs (Rudimentary):** While it lacks complex sensory organs, it possesses two small, black eye-spots and can seemingly perceive its environment and react to stimuli. It does not appear to have a mouth in the traditional sense but can "ingest" items by enveloping them.
*   **Vocalizations:** Emits gurgling and cooing noises, especially when happy or playful.
*   **Scent:** Its surface gives off a pleasing scent that differs for each individual who smells it, often described as their favorite food or most comforting aroma (e.g., fresh laundry, chocolate, bacon).

Its overall appearance is non-threatening, resembling a giant, friendly jelly creature.

## 3. Anomalous Properties: The Power of Euphoria

SCP-999's primary anomalous effect is its ability to induce happiness and cure psychological ailments:

*   **Tickling Interaction:** SCP-999's favorite activity is "tickle-fighting," where it envelops a person (or parts of them) with its pseudopods and tickles them. This interaction is described as being incredibly pleasurable.
*   **Euphoria Induction:** Physical contact with SCP-999 causes an immediate and intense state of euphoria in sentient beings. This euphoria can last for a significant period even after contact ceases.
*   **Antidepressant Effects:** Exposure to SCP-999 has been shown to cure depression, PTSD, and other psychological conditions. Even individuals with severe, treatment-resistant mental illnesses have shown remarkable improvement after interacting with SCP-999.
*   **Pacification of Aggression:** It can calm and pacify aggressive individuals or entities. Its playful, non-threatening demeanor combined with its euphoric touch often disarms hostility.
*   **Diet:** SCP-999 subsists primarily on candy and sweets, with M&M's and Necco wafers being particular favorites. It "eats" by absorbing them into its mass.
*   **Regeneration:** If damaged, SCP-999 can regenerate its mass, though this process takes time.

SCP-999's abilities are overwhelmingly positive, making it unique among the thousands of dangerous anomalies the Foundation contains.

## 4. Object Class: Safe

SCP-999 is classified as **Safe**.

*   **Why Safe?**
    *   **Completely Harmless:** It poses no direct physical or psychological threat to personnel. In fact, it actively improves their well-being.
    *   **Cooperative and Docile:** It is friendly, playful, and enjoys human interaction. It does not attempt to breach containment.
    *   **Simple Containment:** Its containment procedures are essentially providing it with a comfortable, stimulating environment and a steady supply of candy.

It is one of the few SCPs that personnel genuinely enjoy interacting with, and assignments to SCP-999's containment area are often seen as a reward or a form of therapy.

## 5. Special Containment Procedures: A Playpen for Joy

Reflecting its benign nature, SCP-999's containment procedures are focused on its well-being and allowing personnel to benefit from its presence:

*   **Containment Area:** Housed in a well-lit, climate-controlled pen, approximately 5m x 5m. The pen is to be kept clean and supplied with fresh food (candy) and water twice daily.
*   **Free Roam (Restricted):** SCP-999 is permitted to freely roam the facility should it desire, but otherwise must stay in its pen.
*   **No Subject Restriction:** Subject is not allowed out of its pen at night or off facility grounds.
*   **Personnel Interaction Encouraged:** Staff are allowed to enter SCP-999's pen if they wish. Interaction is encouraged, especially for personnel suffering from stress, trauma, or depression.
*   **Play and Stimulation:** Personnel are encouraged to play with SCP-999, as it seems to enjoy the stimulation and becomes listless if left alone for too long.
*   **Dietary Restrictions (Mild):** While it loves all sweets, caffeine and caffeinated sodas are restricted as they seem to have no positive effect and are not its preferred food.

These procedures are more akin to pet care than typical SCP containment.

## 6. Interaction with SCP-682: A Moment of Hope

One of the most famous and poignant events in SCP-999's log is its interaction with SCP-682 ("The Hard-to-Destroy Reptile"), one of the Foundation's most malevolent and seemingly irredeemable Keter-class SCPs.
*   **The Experiment:** In an attempt to see if SCP-999's euphoric abilities could pacify or even "cure" SCP-682's intense hatred for all life, SCP-999 was introduced into SCP-682's containment cell.
*   **Initial Reaction:** SCP-682 was initially confused and then enraged by SCP-999's playful advances.
*   **The "Tickle Attack":** SCP-999 managed to envelop SCP-682's lower body and began to "tickle" it.
*   **A Momentary Change:** Astonishingly, SCP-682 began to show signs of positive reaction. It emitted a sound described as a "low, rumbling chuckle." For a brief period, SCP-682 ceased all attempts to attack and seemed to be experiencing something akin to amusement or even joy. This was an unprecedented event.
*   **The Aftermath:** Unfortunately, the effect was temporary. SCP-682 eventually reacted violently, launching SCP-999 across the chamber with a blast of energy/sound, causing significant damage to SCP-999 (who later regenerated). SCP-682 then resumed its typical hostile state, though observers noted its rage seemed even more intense, perhaps out of embarrassment or frustration at its momentary lapse.
*   **Significance:** This experiment, while ultimately failing to permanently alter SCP-682, demonstrated the incredible potency of SCP-999's abilities. The fact that it could elicit even a moment of positive emotion from the embodiment of hatred and destruction was seen as a profound testament to SCP-999's power.

## 7. SCP-999 and The Scarlet King: A Child of Prophecy?

A popular and compelling theory within the SCP fanbase, often explored in tales and alternate canons (particularly those by DJKaktus), posits a deeper, more cosmic significance for SCP-999:
*   **Offspring of The Scarlet King:** This theory suggests that SCP-999 is, in fact, a child of the Scarlet King (a powerful, malevolent cosmic entity). However, unlike its "father" or other monstrous siblings (like those associated with SCP-231), SCP-999 is an embodiment of pure good, joy, and innocence.
*   **The Prophesied Savior:** In this narrative, SCP-999 is prophesied to be the only being capable of ultimately defeating or pacifying the Scarlet King, thus saving all realities from his tyrannical reign. Its playful, loving nature is its greatest weapon against an entity of pure hate.
*   **The Foundation's Unknowing Guardian:** The Foundation, in this view, is unknowingly nurturing the universe's best hope against its greatest threat, simply by providing SCP-999 with candy and affection.
*   **Symbolic Counterpoint:** SCP-999 acts as a perfect thematic counterpoint to the Scarlet King – light against darkness, joy against suffering, innocence against corruption.

This theory adds an epic dimension to SCP-999's otherwise simple existence, transforming it from a cute slime monster into a figure of immense cosmic importance. It also explains its unique ability to affect even SCP-682, if 682 is also considered a child or servant of the Scarlet King (as some theories propose).

## 8. The Importance of SCP-999 in a Dark Universe

SCP-999's role in the SCP Foundation universe, both in-universe and for the readership, is significant:
*   **Stress Relief and Morale Boost:** For Foundation personnel, SCP-999 is a literal source of therapy and a reminder of positive emotions in a job filled with horror and despair.
*   **A Symbol of Hope:** In a lore often characterized by cynicism, cosmic dread, and moral ambiguity, SCP-999 represents pure, untainted goodness. It suggests that not all anomalies are malevolent and that joy can exist even in the darkest corners.
*   **Contrast and Balance:** It provides a necessary contrast to the overwhelmingly dangerous and hostile SCPs that dominate the database. This balance makes the darker elements even more impactful.
*   **Fan Favorite:** SCP-999 is consistently one of an most beloved and popular SCPs among the community, inspiring a vast amount of fan art, stories, and merchandise. Its simple, wholesome appeal is undeniable.
*   **Challenging Perceptions:** It subtly challenges the Foundation's (and the reader's) default assumption that everything anomalous is inherently dangerous or needs to be strictly controlled with harsh measures.

## 9. Conclusion: The Sweetest Anomaly

SCP-999, The Tickle Monster, is a delightful paradox in the SCP Foundation's grim world. It is an anomaly whose powers bring not destruction or fear, but overwhelming joy, healing, and laughter. Its simple needs – candy and companionship – and its profound positive impact on all who encounter it make it a cherished exception to the Foundation's usual catalog of horrors.

Whether viewed as a simple, lovable slime creature, a therapeutic tool for traumatized personnel, or a prophesied child destined to save the multiverse from an ancient evil, SCP-999 holds a special place in the hearts of SCP fans. It serves as a vital reminder that even in a universe defined by its anomalies and the darkness they often bring, there can still be room for pure, unadulterated, orange, gurgling, candy-loving happiness. SCP-999 is proof that not all monsters are monstrous, and sometimes, the most powerful force in the cosmos is simply a good tickle.
