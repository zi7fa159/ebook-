# Inside the SCP Foundation: Personnel Roles, Classes, and Clearance Levels

## 1. Introduction: The People Behind the Veil

The SCP Foundation is a vast, global organization dedicated to securing, containing, and protecting anomalous objects, entities, and phenomena. Such a monumental task requires a diverse and highly structured workforce. From the researchers deciphering cosmic horrors to the security officers guarding their cells, and the D-Class facing them head-on, every individual plays a role. Understanding the Foundation's personnel structure – their roles, classes, and clearance levels – is key to comprehending how this clandestine organization functions and maintains its Veil of secrecy.

This article provides an overview of the various classifications and roles within the SCP Foundation, shedding light on the men and women who dedicate (or are forced to dedicate) their lives to managing the anomalous.

## 2. Security Clearance Levels: Access to Secrets

Information within the SCP Foundation is strictly compartmentalized using a system of security clearance levels. This ensures that personnel only have access to the information necessary for their duties, minimizing security risks and infohazard exposure.

*   **Level 0 (For Official Use Only):**
    *   **Access:** Granted to non-essential personnel with no direct need to know about anomalous phenomena (e.g., clerical staff in non-critical areas, janitorial staff in low-security sections).
    *   **Information:** Basic Foundation protocols, facility layouts (non-sensitive areas only). They are generally unaware of the true nature of most SCPs.
*   **Level 1 (Confidential):**
    *   **Access:** Granted to personnel working in proximity to, but not directly with, anomalous objects or entities in containment. Common for security officers in low-threat areas or logistics personnel.
    *   **Information:** Information regarding SCPs they might incidentally encounter or that pose a minor, indirect threat to their duties. Basic containment procedures for Safe or very low-risk Euclid SCPs.
*   **Level 2 (Restricted):**
    *   **Access:** Granted to security and research staff who require direct access to information regarding specific SCPs they are assigned to. This is a common level for general researchers and field agents.
    *   **Information:** Detailed containment procedures, research data, and incident reports for specific SCPs under their purview. They may know about multiple SCPs.
*   **Level 3 (Secret):**
    *   **Access:** Granted to senior research staff, project managers, security officers in command positions (e.g., MTF squad leaders), and site administrators.
    *   **Information:** In-depth information about the source, recovery circumstances, and long-term strategic planning related to multiple SCPs, including many Euclid and some Keter-class anomalies. They may have oversight of entire wings or departments.
*   **Level 4 (Top Secret):**
    *   **Access:** Granted to high-level administrative personnel, such as Site Directors, Mobile Task Force Commanders, and heads of major Foundation departments (e.g., Head of Research, Director of Memetics).
    *   **Information:** Site-wide or regional strategic data, long-term operational plans, detailed information on most Keter-class SCPs, and intelligence regarding Groups of Interest. They have significant decision-making authority.
*   **Level 5 (Thaumiel / O5 Access Only):**
    *   **Access:** Strictly limited to the O5 Council members and a very small number of their direct aides or specially designated personnel (e.g., commanders of MTF Alpha-1 "Red Right Hand").
    *   **Information:** Unfettered access to all Foundation data, including information on Thaumiel-class SCPs, SCP-001 proposals, the deepest secrets of the Foundation's origins and capabilities, and information deemed too sensitive or dangerous for any other level. This level grants ultimate authority.

**Need-to-Know Basis:** Even within a given clearance level, personnel are generally only given access to information on a "need-to-know" basis relevant to their specific tasks. Possessing Level 4 clearance doesn't automatically grant access to every Level 4 file.

## 3. Personnel Classes: Function and Expendability

Personnel are also assigned classes that define their general role, expected duties, and, grimly, their perceived expendability.

*   **Class A:**
    *   **Role:** Considered essential to Foundation strategic operations. Typically high-ranking administrative personnel, O5 Council members.
    *   **Restrictions:** Not allowed direct access to anomalies or hazardous areas to ensure their safety and continuity of command. Usually located in secure, off-site facilities.
*   **Class B:**
    *   **Role:** Considered essential to local Foundation operations. Site Directors, heads of departments within a specific facility, experienced researchers vital to a particular project.
    *   **Restrictions:** May be granted access to anomalies deemed Safe or Euclid if necessary for their duties, but generally exposure to high-risk situations is limited.
*   **Class C:**
    *   **Role:** Personnel with direct access to most anomalies not deemed overtly hostile or dangerous. This includes most research staff, field agents, and security personnel not on tactical teams. They form the bulk of the Foundation's "professional" workforce.
    *   **Expectations:** Expected to understand and follow containment procedures meticulously. Psychological screening is common.
*   **Class D (Disposable):**
    *   **Role:** Expendable personnel used for handling extremely hazardous SCPs, direct interaction with dangerous anomalies, testing, and tasks where loss of life is considered acceptable or likely.
    *   **Recruitment:** Typically drawn from death row inmates or other prison populations globally (see dedicated article on D-Class).
    *   **Treatment:** Dehumanized, referred to by number, and subject to monthly termination in many canons (though this varies).
*   **Class E (Provisional / Emergency):**
    *   **Role:** A temporary designation for personnel exposed to potentially harmful anomalous effects (e.g., memetic contamination, unknown biological agents).
    *   **Procedure:** Class E personnel are quarantined, monitored, and undergo debriefing and psychological evaluation. If cleared, they return to their previous class. If compromised, they may be terminated, amnesticized, or become D-Class (or even an SCP themselves).

## 4. Key Foundation Roles and Departments

Beyond clearance and class, personnel fulfill specific roles within various departments:

*   **Research Personnel:**
    *   **Function:** Scientists from diverse fields (physics, biology, chemistry, psychology, memetics, thaumaturgy, etc.) who study SCPs to understand their properties, develop containment procedures, and potentially find ways to neutralize them.
    *   **Titles:** Junior Researcher, Researcher, Senior Researcher, Project Lead, Head of Research.
*   **Security Personnel:**
    *   **Function:** Guarding facilities, overseeing containment cells, responding to minor incidents, escorting personnel, and enforcing Foundation protocols. They are the first line of defense within a Site.
    *   **Titles:** Security Officer, Guard, Containment Specialist, Security Chief.
*   **Mobile Task Forces (MTFs):**
    *   **Function:** Elite, specialized teams deployed for specific tasks such as initial anomaly retrieval, recontainment of breached SCPs, combat against hostile entities or GoIs, exploration of anomalous areas, etc. (See dedicated article on MTFs).
    *   **Structure:** Composed of highly trained operatives from various backgrounds (combat, research, technical).
*   **Field Agents:**
    *   **Function:** Operatives who work outside Foundation facilities, often undercover. They investigate reports of anomalous activity, identify potential SCPs, gather intelligence on Groups of Interest, and may perform initial, discreet containment or retrieval.
*   **Administrative Personnel:**
    *   **Function:** Managing the bureaucracy of the Foundation – logistics, human resources, finances, record-keeping, inter-site communication, legal affairs. Essential for the smooth operation of a global organization.
    *   **Examples:** Site Directors, Department Heads, O5 Council Aides.
*   **Medical Staff:**
    *   **Function:** Providing medical care to personnel, conducting research on anomalous diseases or biological effects, performing autopsies, and psychological evaluations.
*   **Technical/Engineering Staff:**
    *   **Function:** Designing, building, and maintaining containment cells, specialized equipment, computer systems, and facility infrastructure.
*   **Ethics Committee Liaisons:**
    *   **Function:** Reviewing procedures and experiments for ethical compliance, advising command staff. (See dedicated article on Ethics Committee).
*   **Memetics and Infohazards Division:**
    *   **Function:** Dealing with information-based threats, developing counter-memes, and screening personnel.
*   **Amnestics Administration Department:**
    *   **Function:** Overseeing the production, distribution, and application of amnestics to witnesses and compromised personnel.

This is not exhaustive; the Foundation is vast and contains many specialized sub-departments.

## 5. The O5 Council: The Apex of Command

*   **Composition:** Typically thirteen (O5-1 to O5-13) individuals of unknown identity and origin.
*   **Authority:** Possess Level 5 clearance and have ultimate authority over all Foundation operations globally. They make the most critical strategic decisions, authorize use of Thaumiel SCPs, approve Keter-level containment protocols, and are aware of the Foundation's deepest secrets (including SCP-001).
*   **Secrecy:** Their identities, locations, and even their true nature (human, anomalous, etc.) are among the most closely guarded secrets.
*   **Function:** Long-term strategic planning, crisis management, final arbitration on ethical and operational matters of extreme importance.

The O5 Council is the unseen hand guiding the entire Foundation.

## 6. Conclusion: The Cogs in the Great Machine

The SCP Foundation's personnel are the lifeblood of its clandestine mission. From the O5 Council member making world-altering decisions to the D-Class stepping into a Keter's cell, each individual, defined by their role, class, and clearance level, contributes to the monumental effort of keeping humanity safe (and ignorant) from the anomalous.

This intricate hierarchy of access and responsibility, while often harsh and demanding, is deemed necessary for an organization that confronts the unimaginable daily. It is a system built on secrecy, compartmentalization, and a grim understanding of risk and sacrifice. The dedication, brilliance, and often the tragic fates of these personnel are what allow the Foundation to stand as a bulwark against a universe far stranger and more terrifying than most could ever comprehend. They are the cogs in the great, shadowy machine that strives to Secure, Contain, and Protect.
