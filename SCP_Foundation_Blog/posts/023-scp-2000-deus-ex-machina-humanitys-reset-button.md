# SCP-2000: Deus Ex Machina - Humanity's Last Resort Reset Button

## 1. Introduction: The Ultimate Insurance Policy

In a universe teeming with existential threats capable of causing K-Class end-of-the-world scenarios, the SCP Foundation requires more than just containment cells and Mobile Task Forces. It needs an ultimate fail-safe, a contingency plan for when all else fails and humanity stands on the brink of annihilation or has already fallen over it. This contingency is SCP-2000, codenamed "Deus Ex Machina." Classified as Thaumiel, SCP-2000 is not an anomaly to be contained in the traditional sense, but a vast, subterranean Foundation facility designed to reconstruct humanity and rebuild civilization from scratch after a catastrophic global event.

SCP-2000 represents the Foundation's most audacious and resource-intensive gamble: a literal reset button for the human race. This article will explore the nature, function, components, and profound implications of this extraordinary Thaumiel-class SCP.

## 2. Description and Location: The Yellowstone Anomaly

*   **Nature:** SCP-2000 is an immense, automated Foundation installation located deep beneath Yellowstone National Park, USA. Its existence is one of the most closely guarded secrets of the O5 Council.
*   **Size and Complexity:** The facility is described as being at least 700 meters deep and covering a vast subterranean area, with its full extent possibly unknown even to the Foundation. It is an incredibly complex network of machinery, laboratories, computer systems, and living quarters.
*   **Origin (Speculative):** The article implies SCP-2000 is not entirely of human construction or was built with anomalous technology far beyond current human capabilities. Its "Lafayette Subterranean Tunnelling Device" and "Reality Anchors" suggest highly advanced or paracausal engineering. It may have been discovered and repurposed by the Foundation, or built over generations with anomalous aid.
*   **Purpose:** To ensure the survival and restoration of the human species and human civilization in the event of any K-Class scenario that results in the extinction or near-extinction of humanity.

The choice of Yellowstone is significant, as the supervolcano itself represents a potential natural K-Class threat, perhaps implying SCP-2000 is built to withstand even such cataclysms or that its location provides geothermal power or necessary geological stability.

## 3. Core Functionality: Rebuilding Humanity from the Ground Up

SCP-2000's primary function is executed through a series of sophisticated, largely automated processes:

*   **Human Genome Archival (Project Pneuma):**
    *   SCP-2000 maintains a vast, cryogenically stored archive of viable human gametes (sperm and ova) and zygotes, representing the full diversity of the human genome.
    *   It also stores DNA sequences for countless other terrestrial species to allow for ecological reconstruction.
*   **Pseudo-Human Creation (Bright/Zartion Hominid Replicators - BZHR):**
    *   These devices are capable of rapidly gestating and birthing new human beings from the archived genetic material. The process is described as taking mere days or weeks.
    *   The "new" humans are initially blank slates, devoid of memory or personality.
*   **Memetic Indoctrination and Skill Imprinting:**
    *   Once "birthed," the replicated humans are subjected to advanced memetic and hypnotic indoctrination programs.
    *   These programs implant false memories of a "normal" life, basic societal knowledge, necessary skills (language, trades, professions), and cultural norms, effectively creating individuals who believe they have lived full lives up to the point of their "awakening" in the post-apocalyptic world.
    *   The goal is to seamlessly reintegrate these individuals into a rebuilt society without the trauma of knowing they are clones or that the world ended.
*   **Ecological Restoration:**
    *   SCP-2000 has capabilities to reintroduce plant and animal species to restore damaged ecosystems.
*   **Infrastructure and Technological Reconstruction:**
    *   It contains archives of all human knowledge, technology, and culture, allowing for the rebuilding of infrastructure, industries, and societal systems.
    *   Automated systems can likely fabricate necessary tools, machinery, and even pre-fabricated structures.
*   **Reality Stabilization (Scranton Reality Anchors - SRAs):**
    *   The facility is equipped with numerous Scranton Reality Anchors, devices designed to stabilize local reality. This is crucial for protecting SCP-2000 itself from reality-bending threats and ensuring its functions are not disrupted during or after a K-Class event. It also helps in creating a stable baseline reality for the new civilization.

SCP-2000 is, in effect, a planetary-scale cloning, education, and terraforming system.

## 4. Activation and "Resets": The CYA-000 Protocol

SCP-2000 is activated under the "CYA-000 Protocol" (a rather blunt acronym for "Cover Your Ass") when a K-Class scenario has occurred or is deemed irreversible.
*   **Detection of K-Class Event:** SCP-2000's systems constantly monitor global conditions for signs of catastrophic events.
*   **O5 Authorization (Ideally):** Activation ideally requires O5 Council approval, but the system is designed to function autonomously if command structures collapse.
*   **The "Reset" Process:**
    1.  **Damage Assessment:** Determine the extent of destruction and loss of life.
    2.  **Environmental Neutralization/Restoration:** Deal with lingering threats (e.g., radiation, anomalous contaminants) and begin ecological repair.
    3.  **Human Replication and Indoctrination:** Begin mass production of new humans. The number and demographic profile would be tailored to the specific needs of rebuilding.
    4.  **Reintroduction into "New World":** Replicated humans are introduced into designated safe zones or newly constructed settlements, with implanted memories suggesting they are survivors of the catastrophe or part of a pioneering effort.
    5.  **Information Control:** All evidence of SCP-2000's existence and the true nature of the reset is meticulously erased or classified. The new civilization is led to believe its history is continuous, or that the catastrophe was less severe than it truly was.
*   **Number of Resets (The Disturbing Truth):** The SCP-2000 article contains a maintenance log (Log 2000-026-ADR) that chillingly reveals SCP-2000 has been activated **multiple times** before. The current iteration of humanity (our Foundation's timeline) is not the first. The log shows dates of previous "full system activations" centuries or even millennia apart, implying that humanity has faced and succumbed to world-ending events before, only to be unknowingly reset by SCP-2000. This is one of the article's most profound and unsettling revelations. Each "year zero" in the log marks another forgotten apocalypse.

## 5. Object Class: Thaumiel

SCP-2000 is classified as **Thaumiel** because it is an anomaly the Foundation actively uses to achieve its ultimate mission: the survival of humanity and the preservation of normalcy (even if that normalcy is an implanted construct).
*   **Not Contained, But Maintained:** Its "containment" procedures focus on ensuring its operational readiness, protecting it from internal and external threats, and maintaining the absolute secrecy of its existence and function.
*   **Strategic Asset:** It is arguably the Foundation's most important strategic asset, the final guarantee against total human extinction.

## 6. The "Date of First Known Activation" and its Implications

A key detail in the maintenance log is an entry: `Date of First Known Activation: [DATA CORRUPT] - est. 12-15,000 BCE`. This suggests:
*   **Ancient Origins:** SCP-2000, or at least its core function, has been around for a very long time, possibly predating the modern SCP Foundation by millennia.
*   **Lost Civilizations:** It implies the existence of previous advanced human (or proto-human) civilizations that may have built or utilized it.
*   **The Foundation as Inheritors:** The modern SCP Foundation might be just the latest organization to discover and operate this ancient machinery, or perhaps the direct descendant of its original creators.

This adds a layer of deep historical mystery to SCP-2000.

## 7. Threats to SCP-2000 and Its Imperfections

Despite its incredible capabilities, SCP-2000 is not infallible:
*   **Internal Malfunctions:** The maintenance logs show instances of system failures, data corruption, and required repairs. The BZHR units can malfunction, producing biologically non-viable or even hostile humanoids.
*   **External Threats:**
    *   **Anomalous Attack:** A sufficiently powerful reality bender, infohazard, or physical threat could potentially damage or destroy SCP-2000 despite its SRAs and defenses.
    *   **Hostile GoIs:** If a group like the Chaos Insurgency discovered SCP-2000, they would undoubtedly try to seize or destroy it.
*   **Information Breach:** The greatest threat is discovery. If humanity at large learned of SCP-2000 and the fact that their lives and histories might be fabricated, the societal and psychological impact would be devastating, potentially triggering the very chaos SCP-2000 is meant to prevent.
*   **"Divergence" from Baseline Humanity:** The logs note increasing "divergence" in replicated humans from the original genetic and psychological baselines with each reset. This implies a kind of "photocopy degradation" effect, where each new iteration of humanity is slightly different, potentially accumulating flaws or unpredictable changes over many resets. This is a subtle but deeply worrying long-term concern.
*   **The "Pestilence" Problem (Link to SCP-5000):** Some theories connect SCP-2000 to SCP-5000 ("Why?"). If the Foundation in SCP-5000 discovered something inherently wrong with humanity (the "IT" in the collective unconscious), could SCP-2000 be inadvertently perpetuating this flaw with each reset? Or is SCP-2000 the *reason* "IT" exists or persists? This is speculative but adds a dark dimension.

## 8. Ethical and Philosophical Implications

SCP-2000 raises profound questions:
*   **What is "Humanity"?** If memories, culture, and history can be fabricated and implanted, what defines a "real" human or a "genuine" civilization?
*   **The Value of Authenticity:** Is an unknowingly fabricated existence as valid as an "original" one?
*   **The O5 Council's Burden:** The O5 Council carries the terrible knowledge that their world might be a reconstruction, and they hold the power to make that decision for all of humanity. This burden is immense.
*   **The Nature of Progress and History:** If history can be reset, does progress have meaning? Are past mistakes truly learned, or just erased and potentially repeated?
*   **Hope or Despair?** Is SCP-2000 a symbol of humanity's ultimate resilience and the Foundation's dedication, or a horrifying indicator of an endless cycle of destruction and artificial rebirth, where true free will and authentic existence are illusions?

## 9. Conclusion: The Cycle of Rebirth and the Burden of Forgetting

SCP-2000, "Deus Ex Machina," is a masterful piece of SCP lore that transforms the Foundation from mere jailers of monsters into the secret shepherds of human existence itself, capable of raising civilization from its own ashes. It is a symbol of ultimate hope and ultimate despair, a technological marvel that ensures survival but at the potential cost of authenticity and the unsettling knowledge of forgotten apocalypses.

The revelation that our current reality may not be the first, and that humanity has been "reset" multiple times, is a chilling masterstroke that recontextualizes the entire SCP universe. SCP-2000 serves as a constant, silent reminder beneath Yellowstone that no matter how dire the threat, the Foundation has one last, desperate card to play – a card that redefines what it means to "Protect" humanity, even if it means protecting them from the truth of their own cyclical demise and engineered rebirth. It is the ultimate contingency, the quiet engine of manufactured normalcy.
