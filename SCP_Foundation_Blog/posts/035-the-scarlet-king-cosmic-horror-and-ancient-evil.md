# The Scarlet King: Cosmic Horror and Ancient Evil in the SCP Multiverse

## 1. Introduction: The Crimson Emperor of Dread

Among the pantheon of cosmic entities and overarching antagonists within the SCP Foundation multiverse, few inspire as much dread or possess as pervasive an influence as the Scarlet King. Known by many names – Khahrahk, the Crimson Khan, Shormaush Urdal, the King of the Darkness Below – he is an ancient, immensely powerful, and malevolent being often depicted as a god of tyranny, destruction, and despair, whose ultimate goal is the conquest or annihilation of all realities.

The Scarlet King is not a single SCP object to be contained, but rather a vast, mythological force whose presence is felt through his cults, his monstrous offspring, related artifacts, and prophecies foretelling his catastrophic return. This article delves into the lore surrounding this primordial entity, his nature, his followers, and the existential threat he poses to the Foundation and all existence.

## 2. Nature and Origins: A Primordial Force of Malevolence

The Scarlet King's origins are shrouded in deep, often contradictory, myth and apocrypha, varying across different SCP articles and canons. However, common themes emerge:

*   **Primordial Being:** He is often portrayed as existing before the current iteration of reality, or originating from a lower, more chaotic plane of existence (the "Darkness Below," the Abyss).
*   **Embodiment of Dark Principles:** He is associated with concepts like entropy, rage, domination, suffering, and the inevitable decay of order. He is not just evil; he is a fundamental force of cosmic negativity.
*   **Cosmic Scale:** His power and influence are not limited to Earth or a single dimension but extend across multiple realities and timelines.
*   **The Old God / Elder Deity:** Often fits the archetype of a Lovecraftian "Outer God" or "Great Old One" – a being whose true form and motivations are beyond human comprehension and whose mere presence can warp reality and sanity.
*   **The King of Tears / The Crimson Khan:** His titles evoke images of blood, suffering, and absolute, cruel dominion. He is a conqueror and a destroyer.
*   **Connection to "The Old Gods" and "The Ancient Ones":** In some cosmologies (like that hinted at in SCP-2317 or "Kaktusverse"), he is one of several ancient, powerful entities who existed before the current age, often in opposition to other cosmic forces or creator deities.

His true form is rarely, if ever, directly depicted, often described in terms of overwhelming dread, impossible geometry, or a suffocating crimson presence.

## 3. The Brides of the Scarlet King (SCP-231)

One of the most infamous and disturbing connections to the Scarlet King is through SCP-231 ("Special Personnel Requirements," also known as "The Seven Brides").
*   **The Seven Children:** SCP-231 refers to seven young women (SCP-231-1 through SCP-231-7) who were impregnated by the Scarlet King through horrific rituals performed by a cult known as the "Children of the Scarlet King."
*   **Harbingers of Doom:** Each "birth" from one of these women is believed to unleash a catastrophic event or entity, with the birth from SCP-231-7 (the last remaining "bride") prophesied to be the catalyst for the Scarlet King's full manifestation in our reality, triggering an SK-Class "Dominance Shift" or XK-Class "End-of-World" scenario.
*   **Procedure 110-Montauk:** To prevent this birth, SCP-231-7 is subjected to "Procedure 110-Montauk," a highly classified and ethically nightmarish procedure performed regularly. The details are heavily expunged but are implied to involve extreme, ritualized torture designed to keep the entity within her from being born. This procedure is a source of immense moral strain and horror within the Foundation.
*   **The "Tears" and Suffering:** The suffering of the Brides is often linked thematically to the Scarlet King's nature, as he is sometimes called the "King of Tears."

SCP-231 highlights the extreme measures the Foundation is willing to take to prevent the Scarlet King's full arrival and the horrific personal cost involved.

## 4. Manifestations and Influence

While the Scarlet King himself may be imprisoned, distant, or operating on a cosmic scale, his influence seeps into reality through various means:

*   **Cults and Followers (Children of the Scarlet King, Servants of the Scarlet Sign):**
    *   Numerous cults across history and realities are dedicated to worshipping the Scarlet King, seeking to hasten his return, gain his favor, or draw upon his power.
    *   These cults often engage in blood sacrifice, reality-warping rituals, and acts of terror.
    *   They may possess anomalous artifacts or knowledge granted by their dark deity.
*   **Offspring and Avatars:**
    *   Besides the SCP-231 entities, the Scarlet King is said to have spawned numerous monstrous "children" or avatars that act as his heralds or lieutenants. These are often powerful Keter-class SCPs themselves (e.g., SCP-682 is sometimes theorized in fan canons to be an offspring or related entity, though this is not explicit in its primary article).
    *   These beings may actively work to weaken the fabric of reality or prepare the way for the King's arrival.
*   **Anomalous Artifacts and Locations:**
    *   Objects or places tainted by his influence, radiating malevolent energy, causing madness, or acting as conduits for his power.
*   **Prophecies and Apocalyptic Texts:**
    *   Ancient texts and prophecies found across various cultures and realities often foretell his coming and the destruction he will bring. The Foundation's Department of Mythology and Folkloristics likely studies these extensively.
*   **Influence on Other SCPs:**
    *   The Scarlet King's mythos often intersects with other powerful SCPs or cosmic narratives, suggesting his influence is a pervasive thread in the tapestry of anomalous horrors.

## 5. SCP-2317: A Door to the Devourer

SCP-2317 ("A Door to Another World," later revealed/reinterpreted as "The Devourer of Worlds" or "Khahrahk") is another key article related to the Scarlet King, or a being of similar stature and threat.
*   **The Seven Chains:** SCP-2317 describes a salt flat containing a wooden door (SCP-2317-A) leading to another dimension (SCP-2317-B). Within this dimension is a colossal, monstrous entity (SCP-2317-K, "The Devourer") bound by seven massive chains.
*   **The Breaking Chains:** The horror of SCP-2317 is the revelation, through iterated clearance levels, that six of the seven chains have already broken. The final chain is weakening, and the entity's escape is considered inevitable.
*   **Keter, then Apollyon (Effectively):** Initially presented as Keter (due to the containment procedure for the door), the implications of the entity's eventual escape make it an Apollyon-level threat. The Foundation's "containment" is merely observing and waiting for the inevitable.
*   **Connection to Scarlet King:** While not always explicitly stated that SCP-2317-K *is* the Scarlet King, the themes of ancient, world-ending entities bound by failing seals align perfectly with the Scarlet King's lore. Many readers and authors consider them to be the same entity or closely related aspects of the same cosmic horror. The name "Khahrahk" is often used for both.

SCP-2317 embodies the Foundation's helplessness in the face of certain truly cosmic-scale threats.

## 6. Themes Associated with The Scarlet King

The Scarlet King's narrative is rich with powerful themes:

*   **Cosmic Horror (Lovecraftian):** The idea of vast, ancient, and incomprehensible entities whose existence dwarfs humanity and whose motives are malevolent or simply alien.
*   **Inevitability and Doom:** Many stories involving the Scarlet King carry a sense of impending, unavoidable doom. His return is often prophesied as a fixed point.
*   **The Cycle of Destruction and Rebirth:** His coming often signifies the end of one age or reality and, perhaps, the violent birth of another under his tyrannical rule.
*   **Corruption and Decay:** His influence corrupts individuals, societies, and even reality itself.
*   **The Price of Power and Forbidden Knowledge:** Those who seek his power or delve too deeply into his lore often pay a terrible price in sanity or soul.
*   **Patriarchal Tyranny (Interpretive):** Some interpretations see the Scarlet King and his cults, particularly the subjugation of the "Brides," as a dark metaphor for oppressive patriarchal structures and abuse.
*   **The Limits of Foundation Power:** The Scarlet King represents a threat that often seems beyond even the Foundation's ability to truly contain or defeat, forcing them into desperate measures or grim acceptance.

## 7. The Scarlet King in Broader SCP Canon ("Kaktusverse" and others)

The Scarlet King is a prominent figure in several interconnected sets of tales and SCPs, sometimes referred to by fans as the "Kaktusverse" (after author DJKaktus, who has written extensively on him and related entities) or other mythos.
*   **Complex Cosmology:** These canons often develop a detailed cosmology involving multiple ancient gods, cosmic wars, and the Scarlet King's place within this pantheon of primordial powers.
*   **Interconnected SCPs:** Articles like SCP-001 (Tufto's Proposal "The Scarlet King"), SCP-231, SCP-2317, SCP-999 ("The Tickle Monster" – sometimes posited as an antithesis or child meant to defeat the King), and various tales weave a complex narrative around him.
*   **Generational Conflict:** Themes of ancient pacts, inherited curses, and generational struggles against his influence are common.

This interconnectedness makes the Scarlet King a central pillar of a significant portion of SCP Foundation lore.

## 8. Conclusion: The Ever-Looming Crimson Shadow

The Scarlet King is more than just another monster in the SCP Foundation's bestiary; he is an archetype of cosmic evil, an ancient and enduring threat whose shadow looms large over countless realities. His lore taps into primal fears of overwhelming power, inevitable doom, and the corruption of all that is held dear. The stories surrounding him, particularly SCP-231 and SCP-2317, are some of the darkest and most impactful on the SCP Wiki, forcing readers and the Foundation alike to confront the limits of containment, the depths of moral compromise, and the terrifying possibility of an evil so vast it cannot be truly defeated, only delayed.

As long as there are whispers of ancient gods and prophecies of a crimson dawn, the Scarlet King will remain a potent symbol of ultimate malevolence in the SCP multiverse, a chilling reminder that some horrors are eternal, and their chains are always, slowly, breaking. He is the blood-soaked monarch of a dying universe, and his return is the Foundation's perpetual nightmare.
