## 3. Time Audit Log (Simple Version)

**Date:** _______________

| Start Time | End Time   | Duration | Activity Description                   | Category (e.g., Work-Project A, Email, Meeting, Personal, Distraction, Break) | Notes/Feelings (Optional) |
| :--------- | :--------- | :------- | :------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------ |
| 08:00 AM   | 08:15 AM   | 0:15     | Travel to work                         | Travel                                                                      |                           |
| 08:15 AM   | 08:30 AM   | 0:15     | Settle in, coffee, quick news check    | Misc/Personal                                                               | A bit slow to start       |
| 08:30 AM   | 09:00 AM   | 0:30     | Respond to overnight emails            | Email                                                                       | 5 urgent ones             |
| 09:00 AM   | 10:30 AM   | 1:30     | Write draft of marketing proposal      | Work-Project X                                                              | Good focus                |
| 10:30 AM   | 10:45 AM   | 0:15     | Interruption - Colleague Q             | Interruption                                                                | Lost train of thought     |
| 10:45 AM   | 11:00 AM   | 0:15     | Break - walk, water                    | Break                                                                       | Needed that!              |
| ...        | ...        | ...      | ...                                    | ...                                                                         | ...                       |

**How to Use:**
*   Try to log in real-time or in 15-30 minute intervals.
*   Be honest and detailed.
*   At the end of the tracking period (e.g., 3-5 days), sum up time spent in each category.
*   Analyze: Where is your time *really* going? How does it compare to your priorities? Where are your biggest time sinks?

## 4. Eisenhower Matrix (Urgent/Important) Worksheet

**Date:** _______________

List your current tasks. For each, assess if it's Urgent/Not Urgent and Important/Not Important. Then place in the appropriate quadrant and decide on the action.

**Task List:**
1.  _________________________
2.  _________________________
3.  _________________________
4.  _________________________
5.  _________________________
6.  _________________________
7.  _________________________
8.  _________________________

**Matrix:**

|                        | **URGENT**                                  | **NOT URGENT**                               |
| :--------------------- | :------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------- |
| **IMPORTANT**          | **Quadrant 1: DO** <br/> (Crises, Deadlines) <br/> Task(s): <br/> - <br/> - <br/> - | **Quadrant 2: PLAN/SCHEDULE** <br/> (Proactive, Goals) <br/> Task(s): <br/> - <br/> - <br/> - |
| **NOT IMPORTANT**      | **Quadrant 3: DELEGATE** <br/> (Interruptions, Others' Priorities) <br/> Task(s): <br/> - <br/> - <br/> - | **Quadrant 4: ELIMINATE/LIMIT** <br/> (Distractions, Trivia) <br/> Task(s): <br/> - <br/> - <br/> - |

**Reflection:**
*   Where are most of my tasks falling?
*   Am I spending enough time in Quadrant 2?
*   What can I delegate from Quadrant 3?
*   What can I eliminate/limit from Quadrant 4?

These templates are starting points. Feel free to modify them, combine elements, or create your own versions that best suit your individual style and needs. The act of using such templates consistently is often more important than the specific format.
