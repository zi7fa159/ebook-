# Appendix: Curated Resources for Your Sustainable Journey

This appendix provides a starting point for further exploration, offering a curated list of resources across various categories of sustainable living. This is by no means exhaustive, as the landscape of sustainability resources is vast and ever-growing, but it aims to provide valuable next steps for your learning and action.

**General Sustainability & Climate Change Information:**

*   **Project Drawdown (drawdown.org):** Comprehensive resource outlining solutions to reverse global warming.
*   **Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) (ipcc.ch):** The leading international body for assessing the science related to climate change. Reports can be technical but summaries are available.
*   **NASA Climate Change (climate.nasa.gov):** Excellent data, visuals, and articles on climate science.
*   **UN Sustainable Development Goals (sdgs.un.org/goals):** The 17 SDGs provide a global framework for sustainable development.
*   **Grist (grist.org):** Independent, non-profit media organization covering climate, sustainability, and social justice.
*   **Treehugger (treehugger.com):** Widely read sustainability news and green living advice site.

**Waste Reduction & Zero Waste Living:**

*   **Zero Waste Home (zerowastehome.com):** Blog by Bea Johnson, a pioneer in the zero-waste lifestyle movement.
*   **Trash is for Tossers (trashisfortossers.com):** Lauren Singer's blog and resources on zero waste.
*   **Litterless (litterless.com):** Resources and guides for reducing waste, with location-specific tips for some areas.
*   **The Story of Stuff Project (storyofstuff.org):** Videos and resources on consumerism and waste.
*   **Your Local Municipality's Website:** Crucial for specific recycling and composting guidelines in your area.

**Sustainable Food & Agriculture:**

*   **Monterey Bay Aquarium Seafood Watch (seafoodwatch.org):** Recommendations for sustainable seafood choices.
*   **Environmental Working Group (EWG) (ewg.org):** Resources like the "Dirty Dozen" and "Clean Fifteen" lists for produce and pesticide levels.
*   **FoodPrint (foodprint.org):** Information on sustainable food systems and how to make better food choices.
*   **Civil Eats (civileats.com):** News and commentary about the American food system.
*   **Local Harvest (localharvest.org):** Directory to find farmers' markets, CSAs, and local food producers in the US.
*   **World Wildlife Fund (WWF) Food (worldwildlife.org/initiatives/food):** Information on sustainable agriculture and food loss.

**Sustainable Fashion:**

*   **Good On You (goodonyou.eco):** Rates fashion brands on their ethical and sustainability performance.
*   **Fashion Revolution (fashionrevolution.org):** Global movement campaigning for a more sustainable and ethical fashion industry.
*   **Remake (remake.world):** Advocacy and education for fair labor and environmental justice in fashion.
*   **The True Cost (Documentary):** A powerful film exposing the impact of fast fashion.

**Energy Efficiency & Renewable Energy:**

*   **ENERGY STAR (energystar.gov):** US government-backed program for energy efficiency.
*   **Database of State Incentives for Renewables & Efficiency (DSIRE) (dsireusa.org):** Information on renewable energy and energy efficiency incentives in the US.
*   **Rocky Mountain Institute (RMI) (rmi.org):** Research and solutions for energy transition.
*   **Your Local Utility Company Website:** Often provides tips, rebates, and information on energy audits.

**Sustainable Transportation:**

*   **League of American Bicyclists (bikeleague.org):** Resources for cyclists and advocacy for better biking infrastructure.
*   **PlugShare (plugshare.com):** Map of EV charging stations.
*   **Your Local Public Transportation Authority Website:** Schedules, routes, and fare information.

**Ethical Finance & Investing:**

*   **Green America (greenamerica.org):** Resources on socially responsible investing and banking.
*   **US SIF: The Forum for Sustainable and Responsible Investment (ussif.org):** Information and resources on sustainable investing.
*   **Global Alliance for Banking on Values (GABV) (gabv.org):** Network of sustainable banks.
*   **B Corporation (bcorporation.net):** Directory of certified B Corps, including financial institutions.
*   **As You Sow (asyousow.org):** Promotes corporate social responsibility through shareholder advocacy and offers tools to analyze investments.
*   **Fossil Free Funds (fossilfreefunds.org):** Tool to check if your mutual funds invest in fossil fuels.

**Advocacy & Environmental Organizations (A Small Selection):**

*   **350.org:** Global grassroots climate movement.
*   **Greenpeace (greenpeace.org):** International organization campaigning on a wide range of environmental issues.
*   **Sierra Club (sierraclub.org):** US-based environmental organization focused on conservation and advocacy.
*   **World Wildlife Fund (WWF) (worldwildlife.org):** Global conservation organization.
*   **Friends of the Earth (foe.org):** International network campaigning for environmental justice and sustainability.
*   **Natural Resources Defense Council (NRDC) (nrdc.org):** Environmental advocacy group.
*   **Local Environmental Groups:** Search for "[Your City/Region] environmental organizations" to find groups working on issues in your community.

**Books (Classic & Contemporary - A Diverse Starting List):**

*   *Silent Spring* by Rachel Carson
*   *Small is Beautiful: Economics as if People Mattered* by E.F. Schumacher
*   *Diet for a Small Planet* by Frances Moore Lappé
*   *Cradle to Cradle: Remaking the Way We Make Things* by William McDonough & Michael Braungart
*   *This Changes Everything: Capitalism vs. The Climate* by Naomi Klein
*   *The Uninhabitable Earth: Life After Warming* by David Wallace-Wells
*   *Drawdown: The Most Comprehensive Plan Ever Proposed to Reverse Global Warming* edited by Paul Hawken
*   *Braiding Sweetgrass: Indigenous Wisdom, Scientific Knowledge and the Teachings of Plants* by Robin Wall Kimmerer
*   *Zero Waste Home: The Ultimate Guide to Simplifying Your Life by Reducing Your Waste* by Bea Johnson
*   *All We Can Save: Truth, Courage, and Solutions for the Climate Crisis* edited by Ayana Elizabeth Johnson & Katharine K. Wilkinson

This list is just a jumping-off point. The most valuable resources will often be those that are local to your region and specific to your interests. Happy exploring, and continue to learn and grow on your sustainable living journey!
