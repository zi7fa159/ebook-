# Chapter 15: Project - DIY Alien Antenna Headband

Greetings, Earthlings and others! Prepare for intergalactic accessorizing with the DIY Alien Antenna Headband. Whether you're attending a costume party, receiving transmissions from the mother-ship, or just want to add a touch of extraterrestrial flair to your Tuesday, this project is your ticket to becoming a cosmic fashion icon. It's simple, it's bouncy, and it's undeniably weird.

**Concept:** Creating a wearable headband with two (or more!) springy antennae, typically topped with pom-poms, glittery balls, or other alien-esque decorations.

## Materials & Tools

*   **Plain Headband:** A plastic or fabric-covered headband. Wider ones (about 1/2 inch to 1 inch) provide a more stable base.
*   **Pipe Cleaners (Chenille Stems):** At least 2, preferably more for wrapping or thicker antennae. Silver, green, or black are classic alien colors, but any color works!
*   **Pom-Poms (Medium to Large):** For the antenna toppers. Glitter pom-poms are extra alien-y.
*   **Optional Antenna Toppers:**
    *   Styrofoam Balls (Small): Can be painted or covered in glitter.
    *   Aluminum Foil: Can be scrunched into balls or other shapes.
    *   Large Beads or Buttons.
*   **Hot Glue Gun:** Essential for securing everything.
*   **Felt Scraps (Optional):** For covering the headband or making base pads for the antennae.
*   **Glitter, Sequins, Tiny Rhinestones (Optional):** For extra sparkle.
*   **Scissors.**
*   **Pliers (Needle-Nose, Optional):** For tightly wrapping pipe cleaners.

## Step-by-Step Antennae Construction

**Part 1: Preparing the Headband (Optional)**

1.  **Covering the Headband:** If you have a plain plastic headband and want to make it match your antennae or outfit, you can cover it with felt or ribbon.
    *   Cut a strip of felt wide enough to wrap around the headband and long enough to cover it.
    *   Apply hot glue to the underside of the headband and carefully wrap the felt around it, smoothing as you go. Overlap and glue the felt edge on the underside.
    *   Alternatively, wrap ribbon tightly around the headband, securing the ends with hot glue.

**Part 2: Creating the Antennae Structures**

This is where the bounce comes from!

1.  **Form the Pipe Cleaner Springs:**
    *   Take one pipe cleaner.
    *   Wrap it tightly around a pencil, pen, or your finger to create a springy coil. Leave about 1-2 inches straight at each end of the pipe cleaner (don't coil these ends).
    *   Carefully slide the coil off the pencil. You should have a bouncy spring.
    *   Repeat for the second antenna.
    *   *For thicker/sturdier antennae:* Use two pipe cleaners twisted together before coiling, or make two separate coils and then gently twist them around each other.
2.  **Straighten the Ends:** Ensure you have about 1-2 inches of straight pipe cleaner at the bottom of each spring (this will attach to the headband) and about 1 inch straight at the top (this will attach to the topper).

**Part 3: Attaching Antennae to the Headband**

1.  **Determine Placement:** Try on the headband. Decide where you want your antennae to sit. Symmetrically on top? Slightly forward? Angled out? Mark the spots lightly with a pencil if needed.
2.  **Create a Secure Base (Recommended):**
    *   Cut two small circles or squares of felt (about 1 inch across).
    *   Glue one felt piece to the headband at each marked antenna spot. This provides a better surface for the pipe cleaner to adhere to than smooth plastic.
3.  **Attach the Antennae Springs:**
    *   Take the straight bottom end of one pipe cleaner spring.
    *   Apply a generous blob of hot glue to the felt pad (or directly to the headband if not using felt).
    *   Press the straight end of the pipe cleaner firmly into the hot glue. You can bend this end into a small flat coil or zigzag shape first for more surface area to glue.
    *   Hold it in place until the glue sets. Ensure it's standing upright (or at your desired angle).
    *   Repeat for the second antenna.
    *   For extra security, you can try wrapping the very bottom end of the pipe cleaner around the headband once before gluing, if your headband is thin enough and the pipe cleaner long enough.

**Part 4: Adding the Antenna Toppers**

1.  **Prepare Your Toppers:**
    *   **Pom-Poms:** Usually ready to go.
    *   **Styrofoam Balls:** Paint them if desired (acrylic paint works well). Let dry. You can then roll them in glue and then glitter for a sparkly effect.
    *   **Foil Balls:** Scrunch aluminum foil tightly into ball shapes.
2.  **Attach Toppers to Antennae:**
    *   Take the straight top end of one pipe cleaner spring.
    *   Apply a dab of hot glue to this end.
    *   Carefully press your chosen topper (pom-pom, styrofoam ball, etc.) onto the glued pipe cleaner tip.
    *   If using a styrofoam ball, it helps to gently poke a small hole into it first with the pipe cleaner or a skewer, then remove, add glue to the pipe cleaner, and re-insert.
    *   Hold until the glue sets.
    *   Repeat for the second antenna topper.

**Part 5: Extraterrestrial Embellishments (Optional)**

*   **Sparkle it Up:** Add dabs of glue and sprinkle glitter onto the antennae coils or the headband.
*   **Wrap it Up:** Wrap a second, contrasting color pipe cleaner around the existing antenna coils for a striped effect.
*   **Miniature UFOs:** If you're crafty, try making tiny UFO shapes from felt or foil and gluing them to the headband near the base of the antennae.

## Customization Corner: LED Lights and Different Antenna Shapes

*   **LED Integration (Requires Basic Electronics):**
    *   For a truly out-of-this-world effect, you can incorporate small LEDs into your antenna toppers.
    *   You'll need: Small LEDs (e.g., 3mm or 5mm), coin cell batteries (like CR2032), coin cell battery holders, and thin insulated wire.
    *   **Basic Circuit:** Wire the LED to the battery holder (ensure correct polarity: LED long leg to positive, short leg to negative).
    *   Embed the LED in your styrofoam ball topper (poke it through) and secure the battery holder discreetly, perhaps at the base of the antenna or on the underside of the headband.
    *   **Safety:** Ensure all connections are secure and insulated to prevent shorts. This is a more advanced customization.
*   **Different Antenna Shapes:**
    *   Instead of coils, try bending pipe cleaners into zigzags, spirals, or even heart shapes before attaching them.
    *   Use more than two antennae for a truly alien look.
*   **Themed Antennae:**
    *   **Insectoid:** Black pipe cleaners, perhaps with feathery pom-poms or segmented bead toppers.
    *   **Robotic:** Silver pipe cleaners, maybe with nuts or small gears as toppers.
    *   **Love Bug:** Pink or red pipe cleaners, heart-shaped toppers.

## Challenge Your Weirdness: Sound-Activated Antennae (Simple Concept)

This is more of a conceptual challenge, as true sound activation is complex. But here's a playful, lo-fi approach:

1.  **"Listener" Cones:** Create two small cone shapes from colorful cardstock or craft foam.
2.  **Attach to Toppers:** Glue these cones to your antenna toppers, facing forward, like tiny satellite dishes or ears.
3.  **The "Activation":** When someone talks to you, wiggle your head slightly to make the antennae bounce. Explain that your antennae are "picking up their sound waves." The bouncier your antennae, the more "receptive" they are!

Your DIY Alien Antenna Headband is complete! Test out the bounce, practice your alien greetings, and get ready to charm (or mildly alarm) the inhabitants of this planet.

### Chapter Summary

*   This project creates a fun, bouncy alien antenna headband using pipe cleaners, a headband, and pom-poms or other toppers.
*   Key steps involve coiling pipe cleaners for the springs, attaching them securely to a headband, and adding decorative toppers.
*   Hot glue is the primary adhesive.
*   Customization can include different colors, topper types, and even simple LED lights for advanced crafters.

### Action Steps/Challenges

2.  **Headband Audit:** Do you have a spare plain headband lying around? If not, they are inexpensive at most craft or drug stores.
3.  **Topper Brainstorm:** Beyond pom-poms, what other small, lightweight objects could make funny or weird antenna toppers? (e.g., tiny rubber ducks, large plastic buttons, scrunched up sweet wrappers).
4.  **Alien Persona Pondering:** If you were an alien visiting Earth, what would your antennae look like, and what special "powers" or senses would they give you? Sketch your alien self with its unique antennae.
