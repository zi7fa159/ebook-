# Chapter 12: Project - Googly Eye Bombing Kit: Giving the World More Personality

Prepare to embark on a mission of whimsy, a quest to anthropomorphize the inanimate, a crusade of cuteness! We're assembling the ultimate Googly Eye Bombing Kit. "Googly eye bombing," also known as "eyebombing," is the delightful and harmless act of sticking googly eyes onto objects in public (or private) spaces to instantly give them personality. A boring trash can? Now it’s a hungry monster! A mundane lamppost? Suddenly a curious observer! Your kit will be your go-to arsenal for spreading smiles.

**Concept:** Creating a portable kit containing a variety of googly eyes and safe, non-damaging adhesives, ready for spontaneous acts of comedic character creation on everyday objects.

**The Golden Rule of Googly Eye Bombing: BE RESPONSIBLE AND RESPECTFUL!**

*   **Non-Damaging Adhesives ONLY:** Use adhesives that can be easily removed without leaving residue or damaging property. Blu-Tack, temporary glue dots, or even just the self-adhesive backing on some googly eyes (if they're not too strong) are good choices. **NO SUPER GLUE OR PERMANENT ADHESIVES ON PUBLIC OR OTHER PEOPLE'S PROPERTY.**
*   **Permission is Key for Private Property:** If it's not your stuff, ask permission or stick to your own belongings. Annoying your housemates by eye-bombing their toothbrush is funny (maybe), but eye-bombing your neighbor's prize-winning rose bush without asking is not.
*   **Safety First:** Don't climb on things or put yourself in dangerous situations to stick on eyes.
*   **Temporary Fun:** Understand that your googly eye creations in public spaces are likely temporary. Someone might remove them (the fun police!). That's okay. The joy is in the act and the momentary surprise it brings to others.
*   **Avoid Sensitive or Sacred Objects:** Use common sense and be respectful. Don't stick eyes on religious symbols, memorial plaques, safety warnings, or anything that could be considered offensive or disrespectful to deface, however harmlessly.
*   **The Goal is Smiles, Not Vandalism:** This is about lighthearted fun.

## Materials & Tools for Your Kit

*   **A Container for Your Kit:**
    *   Small tin box (like an Altoids tin).
    *   Small plastic craft organizer box with compartments.
    *   A resealable plastic bag (less durable, but works in a pinch).
    *   A small, zippered pouch.
*   **Googly Eyes (The Ammunition!):**
    *   **Variety of Sizes:** From tiny (a few millimeters) to impressively large (an inch or more). Different objects call for different eye scales.
    *   **Variety of Styles (Optional):**
        *   Standard black pupil.
        *   Colored pupils.
        *   Googly eyes with eyelashes (for extra glamour).
        *   Oval or other shaped googly eyes.
    *   **Self-Adhesive vs. Non-Adhesive:**
        *   Self-adhesive ones are convenient but ensure the adhesive isn't too strong for the surface.
        *   Non-adhesive ones give you more control over the adhesive you use.
*   **Adhesives (The Delivery System):**
    *   **Reusable Adhesive Putty (e.g., Blu-Tack, Fun-Tak):** Excellent choice. It's removable, reusable, and generally non-damaging. Pre-roll tiny balls of it.
    *   **Removable Glue Dots:** These are small, double-sided adhesive circles designed for temporary sticking.
    *   **Washi Tape (Low Tack):** You can make tiny rolls of it, sticky side out, to adhere eyes.
    *   **AVOID:** Super glue, permanent glue, strong double-sided tape (unless it's specifically for temporary use and you've tested it).
*   **Optional Kit Extras:**
    *   **Tiny Tweezers:** For handling very small googly eyes or placing adhesive putty precisely.
    *   **Small, Clean Cloth:** For wiping a surface quickly before applying eyes (helps them stick better).
    *   **A "Mission Log" Notebook and Tiny Pen:** To record your most successful eye-bombing locations or sketch ideas.

## Assembling Your Googly Eye Bombing Kit

1.  **Choose Your Container:** Select something portable and easy to carry discreetly.
2.  **Sort Your Eyes:** If your container has compartments, sort your googly eyes by size. This makes it much easier to find the perfect pair for your "target."
3.  **Prepare Your Adhesives:**
    *   If using adhesive putty, pre-roll many small pea-sized or smaller balls and store them in a compartment or a tiny baggie within your kit.
    *   If using glue dots, keep them on their backing paper.
4.  **Add Optional Extras.**
5.  **Keep it Stocked!** Googly eyes have a habit of disappearing on missions. Replenish your kit regularly.

## The Art of Strategic Eye Placement: Tips for Effective Bombing

*   **Look for "Faces":** Many objects already have features that suggest a face (e.g., two screws that look like nostrils, a handle that could be a mouth). Adding eyes completes the illusion.
*   **Scale Matters:** Tiny eyes on a huge object can be funny. Huge eyes on a tiny object can also be funny. Experiment!
*   **Symmetry vs. Asymmetry:** Perfectly symmetrical eyes often look "normal." Slightly asymmetrical placement can make an object look goofy, surprised, or shifty.
*   **Consider the Object's "Personality":** Does that fire hydrant look grumpy? Does that plant seem cheerful? Choose eye size and placement to enhance its inherent (imagined) character.
*   **Unexpected Locations:** Bollards, trash cans, mailboxes, statues (if appropriate and allowed!), office equipment, kitchen appliances (yours!), plants, rocks, fruit in the fruit bowl.
*   **Tell a Story:** Can you place eyes on two nearby objects so they look like they're interacting or having a conversation?

## Ethical Eye Bombing: Do's and Don'ts Recap

*   **DO:** Use removable, non-damaging adhesives.
*   **DO:** Stick to your own property or get permission.
*   **DO:** Aim to make people smile.
*   **DO:** Be prepared for your creations to be temporary.
*   **DON'T:** Use permanent adhesives on public/other people's property.
*   **DON'T:** Damage anything.
*   **DON'T:** Put yourself in danger.
*   **DON'T:** Eyebomb sensitive, sacred, or safety-critical items.
*   **DON'T:** Be a nuisance.

## Customization Corner: Different Eye Styles and Kit Themes

*   **The "Monster Maker" Kit:** Focus on larger eyes, perhaps some red or yellow ones, and maybe even tiny felt horns or teeth you could temporarily stick on with putty.
*   **The "Cute Critter" Kit:** Smaller eyes, maybe some with lashes, and perhaps tiny felt ears or whiskers.
*   **The "Stealth Bomber" Kit:** A very small, discreet tin with just a few sizes of eyes and pre-applied adhesive putty for quick, covert operations.
*   **Glow-in-the-Dark Eyes:** For nighttime eyebombing adventures (on your own property!).

## Challenge Your Weirdness: Themed Eye Bombing Sprees

1.  **Office Object Overlords:** Give every piece of equipment on your desk (computer monitor, stapler, tape dispenser, coffee mug) a distinct personality using googly eyes.
2.  **Kitchen Character Invasion:** Your refrigerator, toaster, blender, and even the fruit in your fruit bowl are now sentient. What are their stories?
3.  **Garden Gnome Enhancement:** If you have garden gnomes or statues, give them a googly eye makeover. Do they look wiser? More surprised?
4.  **The "Find My Eyes" Game:** Eyebomb a few objects in a room in your house. Have a friend try to find all the newly "ensouled" items.

Your Googly Eye Bombing Kit is more than just eyes and glue; it's a tool for injecting a little bit of unexpected joy and silliness into the everyday. Go forth and give the world the gift of sight, one inanimate object at a time!
