# Appendix B: Glossary of Weird Terms (Just for Fun)

Welcome to the unofficial lexicon of the delightfully bizarre! Crafting in the realm of the weird often comes with its own unique vocabulary. This glossary is here to define some key terms you might have encountered in this book or that you might coin yourself on your creative adventures. (Definitions are geared towards our weird DIY context and are mostly for amusement).

---

**A**

*   **Absurdify (verb):** To take an ordinary object or concept and make it wonderfully ridiculous through modification or recontextualization. *Usage: "She decided to absurdify the garden gnome by giving it a tiny disco ball and roller skates."*
*   **Anthropomorphize (verb):** To give human-like qualities or characteristics to inanimate objects or animals. The core principle of Googly Eye Bombing. *Usage: "My mission is to anthropomorphize every boring rock in this park!"*
*   **Avant-Craft (noun):** Crafting that pushes boundaries, challenges norms, and is generally too cool and strange for mainstream craft fairs. Lampshade hats are definitely avant-craft.

**B**

*   **Bizarro-Build (noun):** A DIY project that is exceptionally strange, unexpected, or defies easy categorization. *Usage: "His latest bizarro-build involved a singing banana holder powered by a potato."*
*   **Bodge (verb, often UK):** To mend or assemble something clumsily but effectively, often with an endearing lack of finesse. Weird DIY often embraces the bodge. *Usage: "I had to bodge the robot's leg back on with duct tape and hope."*

**C**

*   **Craft-astrophe (noun):** A crafting session or project that goes hilariously, catastrophically wrong. Often a source of good stories. *Usage: "The great glitter explosion of '23 was a true craft-astrophe."*
*   **Critterfy (verb):** To modify an object to resemble a creature, usually a cute or monstrous one. Sock monsters are a prime example of critterfication. *Usage: "Let's critterfy this old teapot with some felt ears and button eyes."*

**D**

*   **DIY (Do It Yourself):** The foundational act of making things yourself rather than buying them pre-made. The "W" in Weird DIY just adds the essential spice.
*   **Doodad (noun):** A small, often unidentifiable, decorative or functional bit added to a project. Similar to a greeblie. *Usage: "My robot needs more doodads on its control panel."*

**E**

*   **Eccentri-fy (verb):** To imbue an object or creation with eccentric charm and personality. *Usage: "She managed to eccentri-fy the plain wooden box with mismatched buttons and asymmetrical paint."*
*   **Eyebomb (verb):** The act of placing googly eyes on an inanimate object to give it instant personality. A sacred art form. *Usage: "I plan to eyebomb the office stapler when no one is looking."*

**F**

*   **Franken-Craft (noun/verb):** A project made by stitching, gluing, or otherwise combining parts from multiple, often disparate, sources or broken items. *Usage: "This plush toy is a Franken-Craft of three different teddy bears and a dishtowel."*
*   **Felt-astic (adjective):** Anything made primarily of felt that is also fantastic. *Usage: "Her collection of felt food was truly felt-astic!"*

**G**

*   **Greeblie / Greeble (noun):** Small, often intricate-looking but non-functional details added to a model or prop to make it look more complex or technical. Tin Can Robots love greeblies. *Usage: "He spent hours gluing tiny bits of plastic to his spaceship model as greeblies."*
*   **Googly-Eyed (adjective):** Possessing googly eyes. A state of high weirdness and charm. *Usage: "The googly-eyed pineapple observed me from the fruit bowl."*

**H**

*   **Happy Accident (noun):** An unintended or unexpected outcome in the crafting process that turns out to be beneficial, interesting, or even better than the original plan. The patron saint of Weird DIY. *Usage: "The paint dripped, but it was a happy accident that made my monster look like it was crying glitter."*

**I**

*   **Imaginarium (noun):** The wondrous, often chaotic, space within your mind where weird ideas are born and nurtured. *Usage: "My imaginarium is currently picturing a squirrel riding a tiny unicycle."*

**J**

*   **Junk-tique (noun):** An item that is part junk, part antique, and wholly perfect for upcycling into something weird. *Usage: "She found an amazing junk-tique clock at the flea market, ripe for robotification."*

**K**

*   **Kitsch (noun/adjective):** Art, objects, or design considered to be in poor taste because of excessive garishness or sentimentality, but sometimes appreciated in an ironic or knowing way. Weird DIY often flirts with kitsch. *Usage: "The plastic flamingo Chia Pet was pure kitsch genius."*
*   **Kludge (noun/verb):** A clumsy or inelegant solution to a problem; an ill-assorted collection of parts assembled to fulfill a particular purpose. Often overlaps with "bodge." *Usage: "The catapult's trigger mechanism was a total kludge, but it worked!"*

**M**

*   **Mad Crafter (noun):** An enthusiastic and often slightly unhinged creator of weird and wonderful things. You, dear reader.
*   **Monsterfy (verb):** To transform an ordinary object into a monster, usually through the addition of eyes, teeth, horns, etc. *Usage: "Tonight, we monsterfy the mailbox!"*

**O**

*   **Oddball-d'Art (noun):** A piece of art or craft that is delightfully strange and unconventional. (A pun on "objet d'art"). *Usage: "Her collection of lampshade hats were true oddballs-d'art."*

**P**

*   **Pareidolia (noun):** The tendency to perceive a specific, often meaningful, image in a random or ambiguous visual pattern, like seeing faces in clouds or inanimate objects. A key inspiration source for Weird DIY. *Usage: "Thanks to pareidolia, that electrical socket looks really shocked."*

**Q**

*   **Quirkify (verb):** To add quirky, unusual, or idiosyncratic features to something. *Usage: "Let's quirkify this plain picture frame with some mismatched buttons and pipe cleaner tentacles."*

**R**

*   **Repurpose (verb):** To adapt an object for use in a new way; to give an item a new purpose. The heart of much Weird DIY. *Usage: "She decided to repurpose the old colander into a fabulous hat."*
*   **Robotify (verb):** To transform an object or creature concept into a robot, usually by adding metallic elements, rivets, antennae, etc. *Usage: "He's trying to robotify his rubber ducky."*

**S**

*   **Sock-rifice (noun):** An orphaned sock donated to the cause of puppet or monster making. *Usage: "Many brave socks made the ultimate sock-rifice for this puppet show."*
*   **Stash (Craft Stash / Weird Stash) (noun):** A collection of craft materials, found objects, and general oddments saved for future DIY projects. Often takes on a life of its own. *Usage: "I'm sure I have a spare tentacle in my weird stash somewhere."*

**U**

*   **Upcycle (verb):** To reuse (discarded objects or material) in such a way as to create a product of higher quality or value than the original. Weird DIY is expert-level upcycling. *Usage: "She can upcycle literally anything into something amazing."*

**W**

*   **Weirdsmith (noun):** A skilled creator of weird things; an artisan of the absurd. (See also: Mad Crafter).
*   **Whimsy-craft (noun):** Crafting that is playful, fanciful, and full of lighthearted charm, often with a touch of the magical or strange. *Usage: "Her alien antenna headbands were pure whimsy-craft."*

**Z**

*   **Zombie-fy (verb):** To give an object or creature a delightfully undead or dilapidated appearance, often involving tattered materials, pale colors, and perhaps a strategically missing eye. *Usage: "He zombie-fied the teddy bear by adding some stitched scars and giving it one mismatched button eye."*

---
This glossary is ever-evolving, just like your weird creations. Feel free to invent your own terms as you continue your journey!
