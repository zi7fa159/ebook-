## Part 1: The Haunting Begins

1.  **Prologue: The Seed of Doubt**
    *   Alex, a skeptical coder, investigates an old Alpha Minecraft server after seeing a strange video.
    *   He has a terrifying encounter with a white-eyed Steve figure (Herobrine).
    *   Receives the chat message: `You shouldn't have come looking.`
    *   The server vanishes. Alex is shaken but tries to rationalize it.

2.  **Chapter 1: The Glitch in the Machine**
    *   One week later. Alex experiences subtle visual glitches on his computer (Minecraft landscapes flashing, client's face pixelating).
    *   Observes leafless trees in his private modded Minecraft server, which then vanish.
    *   Finds a rogue log message: `[UNKNOWN] Herobrine entity data updated.`
    *   His IDE screen is taken over by a Minecraft crafting table interface with a bedrock block displaying "HELLO."
    *   Alex decides to contact Ben Carter ("CryptoCurious").

3.  **Early Chapters (2-4): Initial Investigations & Growing Dread**
    *   **Alex & Ben Connect:** Alex contacts Ben, who is initially thrilled and validated by Alex's credible account. They compare notes; Ben shares historical Herobrine lore.
    *   **Sarah Chen Introduced:** The narrative introduces Sarah Chen, a game developer with a past at Mojang. She is initially dismissive of any Herobrine talk, perhaps reacting defensively to a work inquiry or a public article by Ben.
    *   **Escalating Digital Hauntings:** Alex experiences more personalized and unsettling digital events: files subtly changing, code behaving erratically, fleeting glimpses of Herobrine within other applications or reflections.
    *   **Ben's Research:** Ben digs into historical Herobrine sightings, looking for patterns that match Alex's experience, particularly the "bleed-through" effect. He might contact some old sources or victims.
    *   **First Contact from "Notch's_Ghost":** An anonymous entity, "Notch's_Ghost," makes initial contact, perhaps via an encrypted email or a cryptic comment on Ben's blog, offering a vague warning or a piece of obscure information.
