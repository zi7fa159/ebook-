## Prologue: The Seed of Doubt

*   **Scene 1:** Alex's late-night setup. Introduction to his skepticism and the Herobrine legend. Motivation: intrigued by a new, credible-seeming video.
    *   *Beat:* Alex replays the video, analyzes it. Decides to log into the obscure Alpha server.
*   **Scene 2:** Exploring the Alpha server. Initial normalcy, then the discovery of the 2x2 tunnel.
    *   *Beat:* Hesitation, then decision to explore the tunnel. Growing unease.
*   **Scene 3:** Inside the tunnel. Torches extinguishing behind him. Panic.
    *   *Beat:* Places his own torch. The oppressive feeling of being watched.
*   **Scene 4:** The cavern. Netherrack shrine. The figure of Herobrine.
    *   *Beat:* Alex's character freezes. He's unable to act.
*   **Scene 5:** The chat message: `You shouldn't have come looking.` Server disconnect.
    *   *Beat:* Alex's physical reaction (ripping off headphones, trembling).
*   **Scene 6:** Aftermath. Server gone from list. Alex tries to rationalize. The lingering dread.
    *   *Beat:* The seed of doubt is planted.

## Chapter 1: The Glitch in the Machine

*   **Scene 1:** One week later. Alex immersed in freelance work, trying to forget.
    *   *Beat:* Introduction to his custom Minecraft-themed IDE.
    *   *Beat:* First visual glitch: Minecraft landscape flashes on his secondary monitor. He dismisses it.
*   **Scene 2:** Video call with Henderson (client).
    *   *Beat:* Henderson's face pixelates into Minecraft-style graphics. Henderson is oblivious. Alex is unnerved.
*   **Scene 3:** Evening. Alex plays on his private modded Minecraft server to unwind.
    *   *Beat:* Notices leafless trees at the edge of a forest. They vanish when he approaches.
*   **Scene 4:** Checking server logs.
    *   *Beat:* Finds the anomalous red log message: `[UNKNOWN] Herobrine entity data updated.`
    *   *Beat:* Frantic attempts to find the source or any "Herobrine" entity fail.
*   **Scene 5:** Alex shuts down his computer. Pacing his apartment. Growing paranoia.
    *   *Beat:* Recalls the Alpha server message. Considers contacting Ben Carter.
*   **Scene 6:** The crafting table incident.
    *   *Beat:* Main monitor blacks out, then shows a Minecraft crafting table with a bedrock block displaying "HELLO."
    *   *Beat:* Alex's terror. Decides he *must* call Ben immediately.

## Chapter 2: CryptoCurious (Example Content)

*   **Scene 1:** Alex's frantic call to Ben.
    *   *Beat:* Alex struggles to articulate what's happening, fearing he sounds insane.
    *   *Beat:* Ben's initial reaction: excitement, validation of his own beliefs, but also concern for Alex's distress. He asks clarifying questions, trying to get concrete details.
*   **Scene 2:** Ben's home office. Video call between Alex and Ben.
    *   *Beat:* Ben listens intently as Alex recounts everything from the Alpha server to the "HELLO" bedrock. Ben takes notes, his office filled with paranormal paraphernalia in the background.
    *   *Beat:* Ben shares some historical Herobrine lore, comparing Alex's experiences to other accounts, highlighting the novelty of the "bleed-through" into other software and the direct messaging.
    *   *Beat:* They agree to collaborate. Ben suggests initial research steps: try to find any record of the Alpha server, document all future anomalies meticulously.
*   **Scene 3:** Alex, later. Trying to follow Ben's advice. Sets up recording software.
    *   *Beat:* He feels a mix of fear and a strange sense of purpose. He's no longer just a passive victim.
    *   *Beat:* Another minor glitch occurs (e.g., a system sound momentarily replaced by a Minecraft sound). He manages to record it. A small victory.
*   **Scene 4:** Ben, researching.
    *   *Beat:* Ben trawls through his archives, old forum posts, and contacts, looking for similar "bleed-through" accounts. Most are vague or easily debunked.
    *   *Beat:* He finds an old, obscure post detailing a user whose game files seemed to "infect" other programs briefly after an alleged Herobrine encounter years ago. It was dismissed at the time. Ben flags it.
*   **Scene 5:** Introduction to Sarah Chen (parallel narrative).
    *   *Beat:* Sarah at her AAA game studio, during a meeting or code review. She's professional, competent.
    *   *Beat:* A colleague mentions a popular new creepypasta or a resurgence in Herobrine sightings online. Sarah dismisses it curtly, perhaps a little too defensively, hinting at an unspoken discomfort.
    *   *Beat:* Later, alone, she looks up an article by "CryptoCurious" (Ben) about the pervasiveness of the Herobrine myth. A flicker of something unreadable crosses her face before she closes the browser.
