# Winter Garden Preparation: Protecting Your Plants from Frost

As winter approaches, it's essential to prepare your garden to protect your plants from the harsh cold and frost.

## Assessing Your Garden

Identify which plants are vulnerable and need protection. Hardy perennials might survive with minimal care, while tender plants will need more attention.

## Protective Measures

*   **Mulching**: Apply a thick layer of organic mulch around the base of plants to insulate the soil and roots.
*   **Covering Plants**: Use frost cloths, burlap, or even old sheets to cover sensitive plants during cold nights.
*   **Bringing Plants Indoors**: For potted plants or very tender species, bringing them indoors or into a greenhouse is the best option.

## Winter Watering

Reduce watering, but don't let plants dry out completely, especially if they are in containers.

## Conclusion

A little preparation can go a long way in ensuring your garden survives the winter and thrives in the spring. Good luck!
