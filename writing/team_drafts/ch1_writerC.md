**Chapter 1: Intake**

**Subject:** Leo Gallagher
**Status:** Provisional Containment
**Location:** Site-17, Humanoid Containment Wing, Cell 113

The subject awoke at 08:17 local time. He remained motionless for approximately two minutes before sitting up and performing a visual survey of the containment cell. The room’s dimensions are 4m x 4m x 3m, furnished with a standard humanoid containment bed, desk, and integrated toilet/sink fixture. The north wall is a one-way observation panel.

At 08:20, the subject rose and approached the observation panel. He knocked on the surface three times.

**Subject:** “Hello in there! Is this where I file a complaint with HR? The complimentary water service was, frankly, a little heavy-handed.”

Per standard protocol for initial contact with a sapient anomaly, Containment Monitor Li responded with the pre-approved script.

**Containment Monitor Li (via intercom):** “SCP-XXXX-A, please return to your bed. A medical professional will be with you shortly.”

The subject displayed a notable lack of distress, replaced by what appeared to be amusement.

**Subject:** “Actually, it’s Leo. And I think you’ll find I’m not an ‘A’. I’m the host for SCP-XXXX-B. Let’s try to use proper nomenclature, people. It’s in the guidelines.”

The subject’s awareness of internal Foundation terminology was noted. Security logged it as a potential Class-II information breach. Monitor Li did not respond further, awaiting the arrival of the psychiatric lead. The subject returned to his bed and was observed to be muttering to himself, a small, unnerving smile on his face. He seemed to be treating the situation as a novelty. This was not an uncommon initial reaction for sapient anomalies, but the subject’s specific knowledge base was flagged as a high-priority concern for the upcoming intake interview. The primary objective was clear: ascertain the source and full extent of the information leak. The secondary objective was to determine the nature of the relationship between the vessel (SCP-XXXX-A) and the entity (SCP-XXXX-B).
