# Main Characters: The Janus Protocol

## D-9341 (Marcus "Mars" Cole)

*   **Age:** Early 30s
*   **Physical Description:** Lean but wiry build, numerous faded tattoos from a previous life. Sharp, observant dark eyes that often hold a cynical glint. Close-cropped black hair. Carries himself with a coiled tension, a survivor's alertness.
*   **Personality:** Highly pragmatic and resourceful. Possesses a strong survival instinct honed by a harsh upbringing and criminal past. Initially deeply cynical and self-serving, distrustful of authority and alliances. Intelligent and a quick learner, capable of adapting to new, terrifying situations. Has a dry, sarcastic wit that he uses as a defense mechanism. Beneath the hardened exterior lies a buried capacity for empathy, which the events of the novel will slowly unearth.
*   **Background:** Former enforcer for a mid-level criminal organization. Convicted of multiple homicides during a territorial dispute that went sideways. His death sentence was commuted to D-Class assignment. His past life has given him skills in evasion, improvisation, reading people, and enduring hardship, but also a heavy burden of guilt and regret that he tries to suppress.
*   **Motivations:**
    *   Primary: Survival at any cost.
    *   Secondary (developing): To understand the bizarre world he's been thrust into, to expose the cruelty of the Janus Protocol, and protect those few he comes to trust.
*   **Core Flaw:** Deep-seated distrust and an initial unwillingness to rely on others, stemming from past betrayals. His cynicism can sometimes blind him to genuine opportunities for connection or aid.
*   **Internal Conflict:** The struggle between his ingrained survival-first mentality and the emerging desire to act for a greater good, or at least for the good of his fellow D-Class. The battle with his own desensitization to violence versus the horrors he witnesses.
*   **External Conflict:** Surviving the SCP encounters, outmaneuvering Dr. Thorne and the Foundation's machinations, navigating the treacherous dynamics within the D-Class population.
*   **Proposed Arc:** From a lone, ruthless survivor focused only on himself, to a reluctant leader who takes responsibility for others, even if it means sacrificing his own chances of escape. He will be forced to confront his past actions and make choices that define whether he is truly irredeemable or capable of a form of redemption, however twisted by the Foundation's world.

## Dr. Aris Thorne

*   **Age:** Mid 40s
*   **Physical Description:** Impeccably dressed in tailored grey suits. Thinning dark hair, neatly combed. Pale complexion, sharp features. His eyes are a cold, analytical blue, rarely showing emotion. Moves with a precise, controlled economy.
*   **Personality:** Cold, calculating, and utterly pragmatic. Embodies the Foundation's utilitarian ethos of "the greater good," even if it means sacrificing countless lives. Highly intelligent and manipulative. Possesses an unnerving calmness, even in the face of horrific events. Views D-Class as disposable tools, not human beings. Believes his work, however brutal, is essential for understanding and controlling dangerous anomalies.
*   **Background:** A rising star within the Foundation's research division. Specialized in anomalous threat assessment and countermeasure development. The Janus Protocol is his brainchild, a controversial but (in his view) necessary program to fast-track the evaluation of dangerous SCPs.
*   **Motivations:**
    *   Primary: The advancement of the Foundation's goals – to Secure, Contain, and Protect humanity by understanding and neutralizing anomalous threats.
    *   Secondary: Personal ambition within the Foundation hierarchy. He sees the Janus Protocol as a means to prove his theories and gain recognition.
*   **Core Flaw:** Lack of empathy and an over-reliance on detached logic, leading to cruelty and an inability to see the human cost of his actions. His ambition can cloud his judgment regarding ethical boundaries (or lack thereof).
*   **Internal Conflict:** Minimal. Thorne is largely convinced of his own righteousness and the necessity of his methods. Any internal conflict would be more about the failure of an experiment or a setback to his research rather than moral qualms.
*   **External Conflict:** Maintaining control over the Janus Protocol and its D-Class subjects. Dealing with the unpredictable nature of SCPs. Potentially facing scrutiny from superiors if the Protocol becomes too problematic or its exposure threatens the Foundation's secrecy. Serves as the primary human antagonist to D-9341.
*   **Proposed Arc:** Thorne is largely a static character in terms of his core beliefs, representing the unyielding, amoral aspect of the Foundation. His arc will be more about the consequences of his actions – whether his protocol succeeds or fails, and how the Foundation reacts to his methods when they are challenged or exposed.

## D-2877 (Lena Petrova)

*   **Age:** Mid 20s
*   **Physical Description:** Small, slender build, almost frail-looking. Pale skin, often smudged with dirt or grease. Large, intelligent hazel eyes that miss nothing. Dark, unruly hair often tied back hastily. Nervous energy, constantly fidgeting.
*   **Personality:** Highly intelligent, observant, and resourceful, particularly with technology and systems. Initially timid and easily intimidated, prone to anxiety. Possesses a strong moral compass, though it's often overridden by fear in the early stages. Loyal to those who earn her trust.
*   **Background:** Former hacktivist and information broker, convicted for leaking classified government documents (unrelated to the Foundation). Her skills in data retrieval, system infiltration, and pattern recognition become crucial. Lacks physical prowess but compensates with her intellect.
*   **Motivations:**
    *   Primary: Survival, and finding a way to escape or expose the truth.
    *   Secondary: A desire to help others if possible, driven by her underlying morality. She is often the voice of conscience within D-9341's group.
*   **Core Flaw:** Physical frailty and initial lack of courage, making her hesitant to take direct action or confront danger. Can be overwhelmed by fear.
*   **Internal Conflict:** The struggle between her fear and her desire to do the right thing. Learning to trust her own abilities and find her voice in extreme circumstances.
*   **External Conflict:** Surviving SCP encounters, avoiding the lethal intentions of other D-Class or Foundation personnel, using her technical skills to uncover information and aid the group.
*   **Proposed Arc:** From a fearful, hesitant individual to someone who discovers her inner strength and courage. She will learn to apply her intelligence in practical, life-or-death situations, becoming an invaluable asset and a more assertive character. Her arc is about finding bravery she didn't know she possessed.

## D-7650 (Javier "Javi" Ramirez)

*   **Age:** Late 30s
*   **Physical Description:** Broad-shouldered and muscular, with the physique of a former soldier. Weathered face, short-cropped dark hair, a few old scars. Stoic expression, often guarded.
*   **Personality:** Quiet, stoic, and observant. Possesses a strong sense of duty and a protective instinct, though it's buried under layers of trauma and cynicism. Initially distrustful and prefers to rely on his own strength. Can be blunt and direct. Haunted by his past.
*   **Background:** Former special forces soldier, dishonorably discharged and later convicted for violent crimes committed while suffering from severe PTSD (details of which are a source of his guilt). His combat training and tactical awareness are significant assets.
*   **Motivations:**
    *   Primary: Survival, and perhaps finding a measure of redemption for past actions.
    *   Secondary: Protecting those he deems worthy or helpless, a lingering echo of his soldier's code.
*   **Core Flaw:** His past trauma makes him prone to brooding and occasional outbursts of aggression if pushed too far. He struggles with guilt and self-forgiveness, which can make him reckless or overly fatalistic.
*   **Internal Conflict:** The battle with his past demons and PTSD. The conflict between his ingrained training to follow orders and his growing realization that the Foundation's orders are monstrous. Learning to trust again after past betrayals.
*   **External Conflict:** Physical confrontations with SCPs and hostile humans. Navigating the power dynamics within D-Block. Making difficult choices that test his moral boundaries.
*   **Proposed Arc:** From a haunted, solitary figure to a loyal protector and a key member of D-9341's group. He will confront aspects of his past by making choices in the present, seeking a form of redemption not through absolution, but through action and sacrifice. He will learn the difference between blind obedience and fighting for what's right.

## SCP-XXXX (Working Title: "The Shifting Mimic" / Later designated SCP-6713 "Janus Bloom")

*   **Designation:** SCP-6713
*   **Class:** Keter
*   **Description:** A polymorphous biological entity of unknown origin. Its baseline form is a large, translucent, gelatinous mass, but it can rapidly alter its shape, density, chemical composition, and even emit various forms of energy or biological agents. Its transformations are heavily influenced by the psychological and emotional states of nearby sentient beings, often manifesting their fears, aggressions, or even abstract concepts in physical form. It appears to possess a rudimentary, alien intelligence focused on stimulus-response and self-preservation, but its motives are inscrutable.
*   **Role in Plot:** The primary anomalous threat in the initial stages of the Janus Protocol at Site-113. Its unpredictable and adaptive nature makes it an ideal test subject for evaluating D-Class responses and its own offensive/defensive capabilities. Each encounter with SCP-6713 is different, pushing the D-Class to their limits. It serves as a catalyst for character development and plot progression. Its evolving forms can symbolize the psychological states of the characters.
*   **Proposed Arc (as an SCP):** Initially presented as a monstrous but perhaps understandable threat. As the D-Class interact with it under Thorne's protocol, its transformations become more complex and psychologically damaging. It might show signs of "learning" from encounters, adapting its forms to be more effective or more terrifying. Its ultimate fate within the novel could be continued containment, accidental release during the climax, or even a form of symbiosis or communication with one of the characters in a truly bizarre twist, depending on the desired thematic resonance.
