# World Lore: Site-113

## Overview

*   **Designation:** Site-113
*   **Classification:** Research & Containment Facility (with unofficial emphasis on anomalous weapons development)
*   **Location:** Remote, geologically unstable region – suspected to be within the Brooks Range, Alaska, or a similarly isolated Siberian mountain range. The exact location is highly classified, even to many Foundation personnel. The remoteness serves both secrecy and to minimize collateral damage in case of catastrophic breaches.
*   **Primary Mandate (Official):** To securely contain and research Keter and Euclid-class anomalous entities and objects, with a focus on those exhibiting unpredictable morphogenic or reality-altering properties.
*   **Secondary Mandate (Unofficial, driven by elements like Dr. Thorne):** To explore the potential for weaponizing or otherwise strategically utilizing anomalous properties. This is where the Janus Protocol originates.
*   **Date of Establishment:** Relatively new, operational for approximately 7-10 years. This newness contributes to its sometimes-deficient infrastructure and cutting-edge (and ethically dubious) research programs.

## Physical Layout & Architecture

*   **General Design:** Primarily subterranean, built into a mountainside or deep underground to enhance containment and reduce external visibility. The architecture is brutally utilitarian: reinforced concrete, heavy steel blast doors, stark fluorescent lighting. Function over form is the guiding principle.
*   **Levels:**
    *   **Surface Access/Level 0:** Minimal surface presence. A disguised entrance (e.g., looking like an abandoned mining operation or weather station), helipad, primary receiving and shipping for non-anomalous goods, and initial security checkpoints.
    *   **Level 1 (Admin & Barracks):** Administrative offices, personnel barracks (for researchers, security, and general staff – separate from D-Class), mess halls, basic recreation facilities, primary security hub.
    *   **Level 2 (Research Wings):** Divided into Alpha, Beta, Gamma, and Delta Wings. Each wing contains laboratories (ranging from standard biological/chemical labs to more exotic thaumaturgical or memetics labs), small-scale containment units for less dangerous anomalies, research archives, and Dr. Thorne's primary offices and the Janus Protocol command center.
    *   **Level 3 (D-Block & Testing Chambers):** Contains the D-Class personnel barracks – rows of small, identical cells. Also houses multiple specialized testing chambers, including the larger, reconfigurable arena used for SCP-XXXX trials and the Janus Protocol's more complex scenarios. Interrogation rooms and medical bays specifically for D-Class are also located here. This level is heavily monitored and access is strictly controlled.
    *   **Level 4 (Secure Containment - Euclid):** Houses Euclid-class SCPs that require active, but not extreme, containment measures. Layout is a maze of reinforced corridors and specialized containment cells tailored to individual SCP needs.
    *   **Level 5 (Deep Containment - Keter):** The most secure and deepest level of Site-113. Houses Keter-class SCPs, including SCP-XXXX (SCP-6713). Features extreme containment measures: multiple redundant blast doors, Scranton Reality Anchors (if applicable to threats), memetic kill agents embedded in warnings, automated defense systems, and dedicated MTF staging areas for immediate response to breaches. The atmosphere here is palpably oppressive.
    *   **Sub-Level (Utilities & Maintenance):** Power generators (primary and backup geothermal/nuclear), water recycling, air filtration, waste disposal (often involving incineration or other specialized anomalous waste handling). Prone to system failures due to geological instability and strained resources.

## Environment & Atmosphere

*   **Internal:** Sterile, cold, and oppressive. The constant hum of machinery, air filtration, and distant, unidentifiable anomalous sounds creates a sense of unease. Corridors are often long, identical, and labyrinthine, contributing to disorientation. The air smells of disinfectant, ozone, and occasionally stranger, unsettling odors near certain containment cells.
*   **External:** Harsh and unforgiving. Extreme cold, heavy snowfall, mountainous terrain, frequent seismic tremors. The isolation is profound.

## Key Features & Challenges

*   **Geological Instability:** The site is built in an area prone to earthquakes and tremors. This puts constant strain on the facility's structure and containment systems, leading to occasional power outages, system malfunctions, and the ever-present risk of a minor breach escalating. This is a key environmental pressure point in the novel.
*   **Under-funded/Overstretched Resources:** Being a newer site with an ambitious (and ethically questionable) research agenda, Site-113 often operates on a tighter budget than more established Foundation facilities. This can lead to older equipment in some areas, staff shortages, and cutting corners, which Dr. Thorne might exploit or rail against.
*   **The Janus Protocol:** Dr. Aris Thorne's flagship project. This makes Site-113 a hub for morally gray research and attracts a certain kind of ambitious or ruthless Foundation personnel, while potentially alienating others.
*   **Security:** Multi-layered. Biometric scanners, keycard access, armed guards, automated defense turrets in high-security zones, memetic kill agents on sensitive documents/areas. However, the human element and system complexities can be exploited.
*   **Culture:** A mix of scientific dedication, bureaucratic procedure, paranoia, and for those involved in programs like Janus, a degree of ruthlessness. D-Class are universally viewed as expendable. Information is heavily compartmentalized. Loyalty to the Foundation's mission is paramount, but interpretations of that mission can vary.

## Relevant SCPs (examples beyond SCP-6713 "Janus Bloom")

*   **SCP-████ (The "Whispering Idol"):** A small statuette that subtly influences thoughts, inducing paranoia and distrust among those nearby. Used by Thorne to sow discord among D-Class groups to test their cohesion under psychological pressure.
*   **SCP-████ (The "Corrosive Swarm"):** Microscopic organisms that can rapidly corrode metals but are repelled by certain organic compounds. D-Class might be tasked with testing new alloys or organic barriers against them.
*   **SCP-████ (The "Temporal Echo"):** An area within Site-113 where localized time distortions occur, replaying past events or causing brief temporal loops. A source of confusion and potential danger.

Site-113 is designed to be a pressure cooker, where the environment, the anomalous threats, and the human element combine to create a desperate and terrifying experience for D-9341 and his fellow D-Class.
