# World Lore: Relevant SCP File Extracts (In-Universe Format)

**Item #:** SCP-6713

**Object Class:** Keter

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-6713 is to be contained in a Type-4 Bio-Containment Sphere (T4BCS) within Sector Gamma-5 of Site-113's Deep Containment Level. The T4BCS is to be constructed of reinforced polyceramic alloy, with redundant atmospheric regulators and nutrient delivery systems. External observation is to be conducted via remote drone and Class-C personnel only. No direct biological contact is permitted outside of approved Janus Protocol testing parameters.

Janus Protocol Test Series 6713-Delta requires D-Class personnel to enter a specialized variable-environment chamber (Testing Arena C-07) into which SCP-6713 is introduced. All D-Class subjects are to be equipped with full biological telemetry sensors. Post-test, the chamber must undergo Level-5 sterilization protocols. Any biological residue exhibiting SCP-6713 properties is to be incinerated.

In the event of a containment breach, Site-113 is to enter immediate lockdown. MTF Epsilon-11 ("Nine-Tailed Fox") and MTF Gamma-5 ("Red Herrings") are to be mobilized. Primary objective is re-containment; neutralization is authorized only under O5 directive if re-containment fails and SCP-6713 breaches Site-113 perimeter with potential for widespread global contamination.

**Description:** SCP-6713, colloquially referred to as "Janus Bloom" by research staff under Dr. Thorne, is a polymorphous biological entity of unknown extraterrestrial or extradimensional origin. Its baseline state resembles a large (approx. 2-5 meters in diameter) translucent, gelatinous ovoid, exhibiting bioluminescence in hues of blue and green. Internal structures are poorly understood but appear to be non-centralized and capable of rapid reconfiguration.

SCP-6713's primary anomalous property is its extreme morphogenic capability. It can alter its physical form, chemical composition, density, and even emit various energies (thermal, kinetic, electromagnetic) and complex biological compounds (toxins, pheromones, psychoactive agents). These transformations are primarily triggered by proximity to sentient biological organisms, particularly humans, and appear to be directly influenced by the dominant emotional and cognitive states of said organisms.

Observed transformations include:
*   Formation of crystalline defensive structures (responding to fear/apprehension).
*   Extrusion of superheated protoplasmic tendrils (responding to aggression).
*   Generation of complex auditory patterns mimicking distorted human speech (responding to attempts at communication or curiosity).
*   Emission of aerosolized psychoactive compounds inducing intense paranoia or euphoria (seemingly random or in response to complex social dynamics within a group).
*   Manifestation of forms resembling specific phobias or traumatic memories of nearby subjects (under investigation, Janus Protocol Series Epsilon).

SCP-6713 displays a rudimentary reactive intelligence, adapting its forms and behaviors based on previous stimuli. It has demonstrated an ability to "learn" from encounters, making subsequent interactions increasingly unpredictable and hazardous. Its motivations, if any beyond self-preservation and reaction, are unknown.

**Addendum 6713-01 (Dr. Aris Thorne):**
> The Janus Protocol's application to SCP-6713 is proving invaluable. The entity's reactive morphogenesis provides a unique opportunity to study the interplay between anomalous phenomena and human psychological stress responses in real-time. D-Class expenditure is within acceptable parameters for an entity of this potential. The data gathered will be crucial for developing countermeasures not only for SCP-6713 but for a wide range of reality-bending and memetic threats. Its capacity to manifest abstract concepts is particularly intriguing and warrants further, more aggressive testing phases. I am confident that SCP-6713 can be 'trained' or conditioned, potentially leading to controllable anomalous effects.
>
> *Note: O5-7 has expressed concerns regarding the "aggressive testing phases" proposed by Dr. Thorne. Caution is advised. Further funding for Janus Protocol Series Epsilon is pending review.*

**Item #:** SCP-7022 (Previously "The Whispering Idol")

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-7022 is to be kept in a soundproofed, lead-lined container within a standard Secure Anomalous Object Locker at Site-113, Research Wing Beta. Access is restricted to Level-3 personnel and above with specific project authorization. Personnel interacting with SCP-7022 or its immediate vicinity for periods exceeding one (1) hour must undergo psychological evaluation and a standard memetic cleanse protocol. Direct handling is forbidden; use robotic manipulators only.

**Description:** SCP-7022 is a small (15cm height) statuette carved from an unknown black, basalt-like stone. It depicts a vaguely humanoid figure with disproportionately large, hollow eyes and elongated limbs, crouched in an attitude of listening or supplication.

SCP-7022 continuously emits a low-frequency auditory memetic agent, inaudible to standard human hearing but detectable by specialized Foundation equipment. Prolonged exposure (variable, but typically beginning within 15-30 minutes for unprotected individuals within a 5-meter radius) to this emission induces feelings of unease, followed by escalating paranoia, distrust of associates, and vivid auditory hallucinations of whispers conveying negative or accusatory statements about others present.

In group settings, SCP-7022's effect can rapidly degrade social cohesion, leading to arguments, infighting, and in extreme cases, violence. The entity does not appear to directly control subjects but rather amplifies existing insecurities and biases.

**Addendum 7022-03 (Janus Protocol Application Log - Dr. A. Thorne):**
> *Test J-7022-Alpha:* Introduction of SCP-7022 (concealed) into D-Class communal area (Group Delta-7, N=5) during a resource allocation task.
> *Result:* Task efficiency decreased by 78%. Three violent altercations recorded within 90 minutes. Two D-Class fatalities resulted from subject-on-subject violence. Remaining three subjects displayed acute paranoia and refused cooperation.
> *Analysis:* SCP-7022 demonstrates significant potential for psychological warfare and social disruption applications. Effective in undermining small group cohesion under controlled stress conditions. Further testing recommended to determine precise range modulation and potential for targeted psychological imprinting.
